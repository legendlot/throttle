Cycle Counts keep recorded stock honest against the shelf. The everyday way to run one is to scan the QR on each part bag: one scan counts a whole bag, so nobody types a quantity. On completion the system proposes stock adjustments for anything that does not match.

Two ways to start a count

Bag-Scan Count is the one to use day to day. Press it and the count opens straight away with no parts chosen, because an audit should record what is actually on the shelf rather than what you expected to find. Every bag you scan adds its part to the count the first time that part is seen. This is what sets it apart: a count started with Pick Parts only ever takes the parts on its list.

Pick Parts is the planned route. It opens a window with two tabs. Due parts lists the parts that are due a count (classed A, B or C by how much they move and what they cost); you tick the ones you want and the count opens with exactly those lines. By product counts every active part on one product’s bill of materials, including shared screws and universal parts; you just pick the product. Retired parts and parts with no stock-ledger row are left out.

Several counts open at once

You can start a new count while another is still open, for example one count per product. Each bag scanned at the Cycle Count station goes to the open count that has that bag’s part on its list.

  • A part is counted on one open count only. If a new count’s part list includes parts already on another open count (typically shared screws and universal parts), those parts are left off the new count. Garage tells you with a message such as 3 parts left off (already on CYC-016); they are counted on the count that already has them.
  • Only one bag-scan shelf count can be open at a time. While several counts are open, it catches any scanned part that is not on another count’s list. Without a shelf count, a part that is on no open count is not recorded, and the scanner says so (see the Cycle Count chapter in the Scanner part).
  • A product count or a Due parts count only takes the parts on its own list, even when it is the only count open. A bag of any other part is not recorded unless a bag-scan shelf count is open; the scanner says PART is not on any open count. Only the Bag-Scan Count adds parts the first time they are seen.

Running a bag-scan count

  1. Press + Bag-Scan Count. The count opens immediately and is ready to receive scans.
  2. On the scanner, choose Store, then Cycle Count. The station itself is covered in the Cycle Count chapter of the Scanner part.
  3. Scan the QR on each bag. One scan counts the whole bag. The part appears on this screen as soon as its first bag is scanned.
  4. Watch it fill in. The Bags Scanned tile and the Bags column refresh on their own every few seconds, so you can follow the floor without reloading.
  5. Complete the count when the shelves are done. The system proposes a stock adjustment for each part that does not match.
⚒ Scanning the same bag twice is safe

A bag only ever counts once per count. Scan it again and the number does not move; the scanner says Already counted rather than showing an error. This is deliberate, and it is what makes the count workable on a real floor: you can re-walk a rack you lost your place on, or put two people on the same aisle, without inflating the figure.

Typing a count instead

You can still enter a figure by hand on any line, which is the right thing to do for parts that are not bagged, or for a loose remainder. Type the quantity and press Enter to save that line. Where a part has both, the typed figure replaces the scanned total on that line, so enter the full quantity rather than only the loose extra.

If a part should not be on a count, you can remove it from that count. Because a part belongs to one open count only, a removed part can then be put on another count.

Recounting lines

If a line looks wrong, tick the counted lines on the open count, then press ⤶ FLAG N FOR RECOUNT and give a reason. Flagged lines are not adjusted when the count is completed, and they show in the Recount tile. The count then sits at Counted.

On a count at Counted that has flagged parts, press ⤶ START RECOUNT (N). Garage opens a new count holding just those parts, each carrying its reason. The old count closes (Reconciled) and its flagged lines show as removed, with the note recounted on CYC-xxx. Scan or enter the new count as usual, then complete it. Ideally a different person does the recount.

A recount cannot start while another open count holds any of those parts. The message names that count; complete it first, then start the recount.

⚠ Blind counting, and completing only proposes

While a count is in progress the system quantity is hidden until that line has a figure; this is deliberate, to stop the count being biased. Completing a count does not change stock: it only proposes adjustments, which still need approval and posting on the Stock Adjustments screen. Scanning on its own never moves stock either. Watch the variance colours: over (counted more than the system) shows red, under shows yellow.

ⓘ Refusals show as a red message

If Garage refuses to start, complete or cancel a count, the reason now appears as a red message at the top of the screen, so you can read what to fix.

ⓘ You do not have to count everything at once

A bag-scan count has no fixed scope. Count one rack, one product, or the whole store, and complete it when you stop. Only the parts you actually scanned are on the count, so nothing you did not look at is ever reported as missing.
