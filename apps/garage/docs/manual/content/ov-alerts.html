Alerts is a focused “what needs attention now” list: parts that have hit their reorder level, and production runs that were submitted but have not had materials issued yet. Open it to catch shortages and stalled runs before they bite.

What it’s for
Catching shortages and stalled runs.
Read or act?
Read-only.
Who
Store Production Admin
Badge
Red count in the sidebar.

The two lists

Reorder Flags

Every part at or below its reorder level, with what is on hand and the level it should not drop under. The count turns red when anything is flagged.

Submitted Runs

Production runs in Submitted status that have not yet had materials issued. These are runs the floor is waiting on the store for.

ⓘ Loads once

Alerts loads when you open it and does not refresh on its own; reopen the page to see new alerts. The sub-header total is the two lists combined (reorder flags plus waiting runs).
