Process is the core triage workspace for returns. For a selected shipment you log each returned unit, inspect it, and assign a disposition: re-dispatch, workshop repair, or write-off. When the good units are triaged, you hand them over to production.

Triaging a shipment

  1. Log each unit. Pick the return category (UDR undamaged, CXR customer return, BRV bulk/vendor) and the product. For UDR you can enter the batch label.
  2. Inspect it. Record the box condition, whether the product is present and its condition, then choose a disposition.
  3. Hand over to production. Once the shipment is fully processed, the Hand Over button groups the good (UDR and repair) units and routes them to production, out of the returns queue.
DispositionMeans
UDRSealed and intact: re-enters dispatch stock.
Send to WorkshopPresent but needs inspection or repair.
Loss · DamageBeyond repair or destroyed: raises a damage note.
Loss · RejectionEmpty box, switcheroo or wrong product: raises a rejection note.
⚠ Two things to watch

Setting Product Present = No automatically switches the disposition to Loss · Rejection, so notice that auto-selection. A loss disposition requires a description and never goes to handover; it creates a loss note reviewed on the Losses screen. The Hand Over button only appears once the shipment is fully processed.
