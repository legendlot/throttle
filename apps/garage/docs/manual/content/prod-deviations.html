Process Deviations is the full approval queue and history for documented departures from the standard process: substituting a material, skipping a step, relaxing a quality check. Staff propose deviations; approvers sign off by tier based on severity. Open it to propose, approve, acknowledge or close.

Proposing and working a deviation

+ Propose Deviation captures the type (Material Substitution, Step Skip, Quality Criteria Relaxed and so on), severity, the line, station, run, a title and description, a rollback plan, and the effective window. Tick Reactive if it is already in effect: Low and Medium reactive deviations are approved immediately but flagged for a supervisor’s retro sign-off, while High and Critical still require pre-approval. Open any row to approve, reject, escalate, acknowledge, give retro sign-off, or close (each needs a reason where it is destructive).

ⓘ You only see the buttons for your tier

Approve and Reject appear only when the deviation’s current tier matches your approval permission, so an L1 approver will not see Approve on an L3 deviation. This is the full record; the floor sees a lighter, in-force-only view in Redline’s Process Deviations screen.
