The Damage / Scrap Ledger is the register of damaged, scratched or written-off stock. It tracks each item from the moment damage is recorded through repair, return-to-vendor, scrapping or restocking. Open it to log damage, batch items for action (which prints a manifest), and read the full audit trail.

The lifecycle

Each row carries a status, shown as a tab and a badge: Pending (yellow), Sent to Repair (blue), Returned to Vendor (orange), Scrapped (red), Restocked (green), Cancelled (grey).

Search and paging

Search looks across the whole ledger, not just the rows on screen, so it finds an old entry even if it was recorded months ago. The list itself loads 500 rows at a time; a LOAD 500 MORE button appears at the bottom whenever there are more rows to fetch. While more rows exist, the row and qty counter above the list shows a + (e.g. “500+ rows”) so it is clear the total is not yet the whole count.

Working the ledger

  • + Record Damage takes many lines at once, the same way GRN Entry does. Each line has its own Part Code (pick it from the list, the same part search as GRN, and the part name and product fill in on their own; you can still edit them), Part Name, Product and whole-number Qty, plus an optional per-line Reason that overrides the entry's reason just for that line. + Add Line adds another row; the × removes one. Source, the entry Reason (shown on the manifest) and internal Notes are shared by the whole entry. A wholly blank line is ignored, but a part-filled line still missing a code, name or qty stops the whole entry from saving until it is fixed. Each line becomes its own DMG number on save.
  • Select rows, then act in bulk: Send to Repair (needs a destination), Return to Vendor (needs a vendor) or Scrap. Each groups the rows into a batch and prints a manifest.
  • Per row: History shows the full transition log; a repair row can be marked Restock (repaired) or Not Repairable; a pending row can be cancelled.
  • Reprint any batch manifest by its batch number.
⚠ Bulk actions need a consistent selection

A bulk button only works when every selected row is in an eligible status; mixing statuses disables it. “Not Repairable” does not scrap an item immediately: it sends it back to Pending so it can be batch-scrapped. Without the damage-manage permission the screen is view-only.

Ledger or Part Summary

A view switch above the tabs offers two ways to look at the same data:

  • Ledger is the row-by-row register described above, one line per DMG entry.
  • Part Summary is one row per part: quantity Pending (with a note of how many pending entries make it up), Sent to Repair, Returned to Vendor, Scrapped, Restocked, and a Total that excludes cancelled entries. Search filters the summary by part code, name or product. Each row has two buttons: Entries jumps back to the Ledger view, filtered to that part on the All tab, and Damage Out opens the damage-out form pre-filled with that part (shown only with the damage-manage permission and only when the part has pending qty).

Damage Out by Quantity

Use Damage Out by Qty (the header button, or Damage Out from a Part Summary row) to move a quantity of damaged stock out without ticking individual rows. Pick the action, Send to Repair, Return to Vendor or Scrap, then add one or more lines: pick a part that has pending damage (its pending qty is shown alongside), and type how many go out, or press ALL to fill in the whole pending quantity. + Add Part adds another line. Repair needs a Repair Destination, Return to Vendor needs a Vendor; Notes are optional. One batch number (DRB-/RTV-/DSB- as appropriate) covers the whole action and the manifest prints as it always does.

⚠ The oldest pending entries go first, and a partly-used entry splits

Damage Out by Qty always draws from the oldest pending DMG entries for that part first. If the last entry it needs is only partly used, it is split in two: the quantity going out keeps the original DMG number (its qty reduced to match), and the remainder stays pending under a brand-new DMG number, with the same source and date. Both entries' History shows the split. Asking for more than is pending is refused. Only pending entries can be moved this way; the tick-row bulk actions above still work as before, including scrapping a row already sent to repair. Nothing moves physical stock except Restock after repair, exactly as before.
