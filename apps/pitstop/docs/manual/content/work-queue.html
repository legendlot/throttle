The Queue is the CS home screen: the worklist of tickets. Agents live here to see what is assigned to them, what is overdue, and to open or create tickets. It is where a shift starts.

What it’s for
Your ticket worklist.
Read or act?
Read; open or create tickets.
Who
Agent Lead Admin
Updates
Counts every 60s.

Reading it

Five cards head the screen: My Open, Overdue (red), Awaiting Evidence 3+ days (nudge candidates), Closed Today, and Avg Close month-to-date. Below them, tabs with live counts: My Queue (the default), All Open, Awaiting Evidence, In Logistics, Inspection, Resolution, Closed.

Each row shows the ticket number, customer (with masked phone), product, the disposition badge (an AUTO tag if it was auto-created), the current stage, platform, assigned agent, and an Age / SLA figure: green up to 3 days, orange beyond, and red and bold when overdue with a “+Nd over” note.

Finding and opening tickets

Search by name, phone, order, UPC or ticket number (press / to jump in). Narrow with the disposition chips and the Category and Platform dropdowns; the filters live in the URL so a view can be bookmarked. Click a row, or use the arrow keys and Enter, to open it. New Ticket (red) creates one; Export CSV downloads the rows you can currently see.

⚠ Export is the visible list, with masked phones

Export CSV downloads only the tickets currently loaded for the tab and filters you are on, not the whole queue, and phone numbers are masked in the file. The AUTO badge means the ticket was created automatically (for example from a call), not opened by an agent.
