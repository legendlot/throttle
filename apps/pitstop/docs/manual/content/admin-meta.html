Meta Diagnostics answers one question: why did Instagram or Messenger refuse to send a reply? When an agent’s message to a customer bounces, the refusal is recorded here with Meta’s own error, and you can probe the chat to see whether the problem is our connection or that particular customer.

What it’s for
Diagnosing refused Instagram / Messenger replies.
Read or act?
Read only. It never sends a message.
Who
Admin
Records from
26 Aug 2026 onward.

Reading the list

Each row is one chat and one error code, not one message — if an agent pressed send four times and it failed four times, that is a single row with Attempts 4. Window says whether the 24-hour reply window was open at the moment of the attempt, which is the fact that separates a normal out-of-hours refusal from a genuine fault. Use the range selector to look further back.

What the error codes mean

CodeMeaningWhat to do
10Outside the 24-hour window and our “Human Agent” permission is still pending Meta review.Nothing. This is expected behaviour, not a fault. The chat reopens if the customer writes again.
100Invalid parameter — almost always a reply longer than 2,000 characters.Shorten the reply. The composer now warns before this happens.
190Our access token has expired or been revoked. Affects every chat, not one.Tell Afshaan. Replying from the Instagram app still works meanwhile.
200Permissions error, refused for that one recipient while other chats send normally.Press Diagnose on the row. This is the case the probe exists for.

Diagnose

Diagnose makes two read-only checks against Meta using the same credential the reply would have used: one for our own account (me) and one for the customer (recipient). Read the result this way:

1. me succeeds and recipient succeeds — our connection and the customer are both fine; the refusal is about the message or the window, not the chat.

2. me succeeds but recipient fails — our connection is healthy and Meta is refusing this specific person. The response body is the evidence of why.

3. me also fails — the problem is our token, not the customer. That is an Afshaan job, and it will be affecting everybody.

ⓘ It sends nothing

Diagnose is safe to press at any time, on any chat, as often as you like. It reads two things from Meta and writes nothing — the customer sees no message and the conversation is not touched.

⛔ Do not translate the raw result into an explanation

The probe output is shown exactly as Meta returned it, on purpose. In August 2026 a Meta error was explained from the documentation rather than from a measurement, and the wording that reached agents told them a reply would go through while it was in fact failing silently. If the result does not obviously say what is wrong, that is the honest answer — pass it to Afshaan rather than guessing at a cause.

Probing a chat by hand

Refusals are only recorded from 26 August 2026 onward, so an older report will not appear in the list. The box at the bottom takes a conversation id directly and probes it the same way.
