Pitstop is the customer support system for Legend of Toys. Every customer issue, whether it arrives by phone, WhatsApp or a marketplace message, becomes a ticket that an agent works from start to finish. This manual is your self-serve guide to every screen in it.

How a ticket flows

Almost everything in Pitstop revolves around a ticket moving through clear stages. The shape of that journey is set by one choice: the disposition, which is what you have decided to do for the customer.

DispositionWhat happens
Query / No actionA question or a non-issue: opens and closes quickly (intake to closed).
Awaiting infoWaiting on the customer for evidence before deciding.
Replacement / Refund / Repair / PendingThe full path: verify, schedule a pickup, receive at the warehouse, inspect, then the branch for the chosen outcome, then close.

You set the disposition during triage; from there the ticket advances stage by stage, each step asking for the details it needs (an AWB, an inspection note, a refund reference).

ⓘ Where the conversation happens

Pitstop shows the customer’s WhatsApp conversation as a read-only mirror. To actually reply to the customer you click through to BiteSpeed; two-way replies inside Pitstop are a later phase. Phone calls flow in from MyOperator and appear in the Calls log, where a missed call can be turned into a ticket.
