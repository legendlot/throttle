Reports is the “how are we doing” screen: how many cases and calls happened over a date range, what they cost, and how products, platforms, agents and departments compare. Open it to review performance.

What it’s for
Ticket and call analytics.
Read or act?
Read; CSV export.
Who
Lead Admin
Range
From / To (defaults year-to-date).

Two tabs

Tickets shows total cases and the three cost lines (return logistics, replacement units, refund payouts), a monthly trend chart coloured by outcome, and breakdowns by product, platform and agent (with each agent’s average days-to-close). Calls shows total, answered and missed calls, answer rate and average duration, with breakdowns by department, MyOperator account, direction and agent, plus an hourly distribution.

Set the From and To dates at the top; changing either refetches immediately.

⚠ Export is tickets only

The Export CSV button shows on both tabs but always exports the ticket figures (product, platform, agent and a cost summary), even when you are looking at the Calls tab. In the agent call table, “missed to returned” counts missed calls that were later called back, not still-missed ones. This screen needs the reports permission.
