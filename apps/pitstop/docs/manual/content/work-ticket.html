The ticket detail screen is the full case file for one ticket. This is where you verify the issue, advance it through its lifecycle, read the WhatsApp conversation, log notes and evidence, and assign it. It is the heart of Pitstop.

The layout

A header carries the customer and product, the disposition, an overdue badge if relevant, and the big red Advance button, with a stage stepper below. The body has three columns: the identity rail (assignment, customer, Shopify, call info, order, past cases), the work area (Issue, Return logistics, Resolution), and the activity feed (history, notes, attachments).

Advancing a ticket

  1. Set the disposition from the header dropdown during triage. This decides the whole path the ticket will take.
  2. Click Advance. The button names the next stage (for example “Schedule Pickup”). A modal asks for the fields that stage needs: a pickup needs the return AWB and courier; inspection needs an inspection note; a refund needs the amount, then the payment reference.
  3. Fill the required fields and confirm. The ticket moves to the next stage and the stepper updates.

Three side-exits handle the non-standard endings: Cancel (customer dropped), Reject (inspection found no fault), and Escalate (flag for a manager, staying in the current stage). Assignment is on the left rail: agents Claim an unassigned ticket; leads and admins can Reassign to anyone.

⚠ Disposition locks after triage

You can change the disposition freely only while the ticket is in intake or awaiting evidence. Once it enters logistics, only an admin can change it. The Issue, Return logistics and Resolution panels also unlock in sequence: a panel that is greyed out now (“unlocks once the issue is verified”) will open at the right stage. Cancel, Reject and Close are three different endings: do not use one for another.

ⓘ WhatsApp and attachments

The WhatsApp panel is a read-only mirror: to reply, use Reply in BiteSpeed. A green pill shows how long the 24-hour reply window is open. Attachments in the activity feed are links (to a WhatsApp video, a Drive photo), not uploaded files.
