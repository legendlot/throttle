Channel Master is the list of every dispatch/sales channel (Amazon, Flipkart, Cred, BOM Bulk, retail partners and so on) that shipments and units are assigned to. It’s the small but important screen that defines the choices everywhere else in Dispatch.

What it’s for
Maintaining the channel list.
Read or act?
Action: add / edit channels.
Who
Admin
No delete
Deactivate instead.

A channel’s settings

Name

The channel’s name (e.g. Amazon, Flipkart, BOM Bulk).

Type

Ecom, Retail or Other, shown as a colour badge across Dispatch.

Fulfillment

Unit (dispatched per unit) or Bulk (warehouse transfer). Bulk channels make a “Destination Warehouse” field appear when you create a shipment for them.

Sale channel

Whether the channel generates revenue. This drives the green “sold” colouring and totals on the Dispatch Overview.

Active

Whether the channel is offered when creating shipments. Inactive channels show dimmed.

Adding & editing

+ Add Channel opens an inline form; new channels are always created active. Edit on any row lets you change its type, fulfillment, sale flag and active state.

ⓘ Retire, don’t delete

There’s no delete. A channel that’s no longer used is switched to Inactive so its history stays intact and it simply stops appearing as a choice for new shipments.
