The Purchase Orders screen is the master list of every PO we have ever raised, across all categories and sources. It is where you find a PO to work, filter the pipeline, and spot anything stuck.

What it’s for
Finding and tracking purchase orders.
Read or act?
Read; open a PO to act.
Who
Procurement Approver Finance Admin
Filters
Status, source, type, plus search.

Reading the table

Each row shows the PO number, its revision number, type, source country, vendor, line count, value, expected arrival, who raised it, and its current status. Click any row to open the PO; the View button on the right does the same. The status badge tells you where the PO is in its life:

StatusWhat it means
SoftA placeholder China PO for goods not yet pinned to a registered product.
DraftBeing built; can still be edited.
AcceptedProcurement has signed off; waiting for final approval.
ApprovedFinal sign-off given; ready to send and pay.
SentIssued to the supplier.
Confirmed & Payment Done / Partially Received / ClosedLater life stages once goods are flowing in.
CancelledStopped, with a reason on the PO.

Filtering and finding

Use the search box to match a PO number, vendor, code, source, type or who raised it (type more than one word to narrow). The three dropdowns filter by status, source country and order type. The header shows how many rows match. New PO (top right, for procurement) starts a fresh purchase order.

Downloading the list

Export (top right, beside New PO) downloads the list as a spreadsheet that opens straight in Excel. It gives you exactly what is on screen — whatever the search box and the status, source and type filters are currently showing, in the same order — so clear the filters first if you want every PO.

Alongside the columns you can see, the file adds the vendor code, the currency, an approximate rupee value for foreign-currency POs, and the revision number — the fields a spreadsheet is usually built to sort and total by.

China POs follow the same rule here as everywhere else: if you are not cleared to see China values, those rows arrive marked Restricted rather than carrying a number. The download can never contain more than your screen does.

⚠ The “awaiting inward” banner

If POs have been confirmed but their goods have not yet been counted in, a yellow banner appears with a count and a link to the receiving screen in our store system. That receiving step happens outside Snorkel: Snorkel raises the order, the store counts it in.

ⓘ China values may show as “Restricted”

If you are not cleared for China procurement, the value of a China-sourced PO shows as Restricted rather than a figure. You can still see the PO exists and track its status, just not its money.
