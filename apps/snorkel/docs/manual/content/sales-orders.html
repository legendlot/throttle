Sales Orders is the sell-side of Snorkel: orders to our offline channels, General Trade and Modern Trade shops and distributors. An order is captured as a draft, confirmed (which hands it to the dispatch team), invoiced, and then collected against. This screen is the worklist of all of them.

The tiles and list

Four tiles head the screen: Open orders, the value still to dispatch, the overdue balance (click it to filter to just overdue), and total sales this financial year. An order’s life

StageWhat it means
DraftBeing built. Freely editable. Not yet seen by dispatch.
ConfirmedLocked and handed to dispatch (a shipment is created for them). Can now be invoiced.
CancelledStopped, with a reason.

Separate from that, a fulfilment label (Not dispatched, Pending, In progress, Fulfilled) is read straight from the dispatch shipment, and a payment label (Unpaid, Partial, Paid) reflects collections.

⛔ Confirming hands the order to dispatch

Confirming an order is a one-way handoff: it locks the lines for editing and creates a shipment that the dispatch team works in our production system. After that, the fulfilment status here is read from that shipment, you do not set it. Get the order right before you confirm.

ⓘ Dispatch happens in Redline

Once an order is confirmed it appears in the dispatch team’s Shipments tab. They pack and ship it there; the dispatch and delivery dates flow back here automatically and drive the payment-due clock. Nothing about boxes or scans lives in Snorkel.

Creating and working an order

New Order opens a form: pick the partner and channel, set the order date and credit days (these default from the partner), and add lines (product, model, colour, quantity, rate, discount and GST). It saves as a Draft. Open any order to work it; the buttons follow its stage:

  1. Edit — change a Draft’s lines or details. Disabled once confirmed or invoiced.
  2. Confirm → Dispatch — locks the order and creates the dispatch shipment. You are asked to confirm because it is one-way.
  3. Generate Invoice — on a confirmed order, mints the GST tax invoice and opens it to print. The number is permanent.
  4. Record receipt — in the Collections panel, log a payment (amount, date, mode, reference). Partial payments are fine; the balance and payment state update themselves.
  5. Cancel — stop a draft or confirmed order, with a reason.
⛔ One invoice per order, and it is final

Generating the GST invoice requires the order to be confirmed and every line to carry an HSN code. The invoice number is permanent and continuous for the financial year, so it cannot be re-generated and the lines freeze once invoiced. The tax splits into CGST + SGST when the partner is in our own state, or IGST when they are in another, worked out from the partner’s and our own registered GST details.
