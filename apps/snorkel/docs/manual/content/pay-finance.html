The Finance Queue is the worklist for actually paying. Everything approved and unpaid is here, most urgent first, with the payee’s bank details on the card so you do not have to go looking for them.

Each card carries

  • The amount, what it is for, and who asked.
  • The bank account — account number, IFSC, bank and UPI where held, each with a copy button.
  • The invoice, openable in one tap.
  • Whether it was approved by someone, or fell below the threshold.

Paying

  1. Make the payment in your bank.
  2. Put the UTR or reference in the box on the card.
  3. Tap Mark paid.

The person who raised it is told immediately, with the UTR. That is what stops the “is it done?” messages.

ⓘ You can reject a request yourself

If the invoice is wrong — wrong amount, wrong vendor, already paid — open the request and use Reject. You no longer have to send it back up for someone else to reject it. A reason is required, and the person who raised the request sees exactly what you wrote, so write something they can act on: “invoice is for 4,484 but the PO says 4,048” beats “wrong”.

ⓘ Putting a request on hold

Not ready to pay something — waiting on a corrected invoice, a budget check, whatever it is — without rejecting it? Use Hold on the card, or Put on hold on the request page. A reason is required and the requester sees it. This is a pause, not a closure: the request stays open on their side as “On hold with Finance”, it drops out of the To-pay total, and it moves to an On hold section below the queue with who held it, when, and why. Use Release to bring it back to the ready-to-pay list — it has to be released before it can be marked paid. You can also Reject a held request straight from there, and the requester can still cancel it themselves.

ⓘ Only the people who actually pay see the UTR box

The UTR field and Mark paid button only appear for whoever is authorised to make the payment. Everyone else who can open this queue — managers and admins included — sees “View only — payments are marked by finance.” instead, with the rest of the card (bank details, invoice, amount) still visible. Being in the Finance role is not by itself enough to pay; that is granted to specific people so the button is never offered to someone it would just reject.

No bank account on file? The card says so instead of letting you pay blind. Add the account under Payees — once — rather than chasing it in chat. Bank details are visible to Finance only.
