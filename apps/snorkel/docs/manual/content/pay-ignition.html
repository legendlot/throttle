Some payment requests do not come from a person in Snorkel. They are raised by the influencer team from a deal in Ignition. They land in your normal queue and are paid the normal way; the difference is where the details come from and where the answers go.

What arrives

  • An ordinary request with the next PAY number, category Influencer, no PO.
  • The creator’s own invoice is attached. A request is only sent once the invoice is there, so you never get one without it.
  • The payee is found or created for you from the creator’s contract: matched on PAN, then name, so an existing influencer payee is reused and not duplicated. The bank details are the ones the creator entered on the contract page.
  • An advance and its balance can be two requests on the same invoice number. The duplicate-invoice warning will show; that is expected.

Approval

The usual threshold applies. Creator requests under ₹5,00,000 normally go straight to Approved. Over that, they wait for an approver as any request does.

Hold and reject: write for the deal owner

When you hold or reject one of these, the deal owner gets a Slack message with your reason, word for word, and sees it on the deal. They are not in Snorkel and cannot ask you what you meant. So say what is wrong and what to do (“Invoice total does not match the fee. Upload the corrected invoice and raise again”), not just “wrong”. A reason is needed in any case. Hold, release and hold again messages the owner each time.

Marking paid

Mark paid as always: TDS rate if it applies, amount, UTR. The deal is updated straight away with the date, amount, UTR and TDS, and shows Paid. The amount the deal shows is the gross: what you paid plus TDS. If you fix the UTR later, the deal picks up the change within about 15 minutes.

ⓘ If it is cancelled

The owner can cancel a request in Ignition only before it reaches Snorkel. Once it is in your queue, only you cancel or reject it, and the deal shows the new status.
