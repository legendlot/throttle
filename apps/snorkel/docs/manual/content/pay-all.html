All Requests lists every payment request raised by anyone, in every status, so you can see where a payment has got to without asking around. It is view only.

What it’s forSeeing every payment request and its status.
Read or act?Read only: no approving or paying here.
WhoFinance, plus anyone who can approve or pay.
WherePayments, next to My Requests.

Who sees it

The Finance role (Finance Manager) sees All Requests, and so does anyone who holds an approve, pay or super-admin payment permission. If you do not have one of these, the entry is not in your menu; you still see your own requests under My Requests.

Using the list

The status tabs work exactly as they do on My Requests, so you can narrow to pending, approved, paid, rejected or cancelled. Click a row to open the request and see its invoice.

View only. Approving still happens on Approvals and paying still happens on the Finance Queue. There are no tick boxes or bulk buttons on this page.
If the list fails to load you will see an error, not an empty list. An empty list means there really are no requests; an error means try again.
