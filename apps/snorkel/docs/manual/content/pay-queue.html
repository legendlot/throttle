The Payment Queue is the finance worklist: every approved purchase order, grouped by how far along its payment is. From here you decide who pays a PO and record when it is done. It is the last step in the buying chain.

What it’s for
Getting approved POs paid.
Read or act?
Act: route and mark paid.
Who
Finance Procurement Admin
Tabs
To Route, Requested, Paid, All.

Reading the queue

Tabs split the approved POs by payment state, each with a count:

TabWhat it holds
To RouteApproved POs whose payment has not been requested from anyone yet.
RequestedPayment has been routed (to Finance or the requester) and is awaiting the money.
PaidDone, showing who marked it paid and when.
AllEverything together.

Each row shows the PO, vendor, value, when it was approved, and the payment state. Click the PO number to open the full PO.

Working a payment

  1. On a To-Route PO, choose who pays: → Finance (the finance team pays) or → Requester (the person who asked pays and claims it back). The PO moves to Requested.
  2. When the money has gone out, click Mark Paid. The PO moves to Paid and is stamped with your name and the date.
ⓘ The same buttons live on the PO too

You can route and mark-paid from a PO’s own detail screen as well; this queue just gathers them all in one place so finance can work down the list. Only POs that have been fully Approved appear here, never drafts or accepted-but-not-approved ones.
