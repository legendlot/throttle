A request is how you tell procurement what you need. It is deliberately free-form: you do not need to know vendors, prices or part numbers, just describe the thing. Anyone signed in can file one, and it is the front door to every purchase.

What it’s for
Asking procurement to buy something.
Read or act?
Act: you submit a request.
Who
Requester — anyone.
Result
A numbered request (PR-).

Filling it in

Only two fields are required: a short Title and the What do you need? description. Everything else is optional but helps procurement act faster.

Title *

A one-line summary, e.g. “20× ceiling fans for the new floor.”

What do you need? *

The detail: items, quantities, specs, links, and why. The more specific, the better.

Category

A loose grouping (Office, Consumable, Component, Machine…).

Suggested vendor

If you know who to buy from, say so. Procurement decides the final supplier.

Est. cost & currency

A rough figure if you have one, in INR, USD, CNY or EUR.

Urgency

Low, Normal, High or Urgent. Urgent items stand out in the list.

Needed by

The date you need it in hand, if there is a deadline.

Notes

Anything else worth saying.

Submitting

  1. Write a clear title and description. These are the two things procurement reads first.
  2. Add cost, urgency and a needed-by date if you know them, so your request can be prioritised.
  3. Click Submit Request. Snorkel gives it a number (like PR-0142) and opens the request so you can watch its progress.
✓ You do not need to know how to buy it

Leave vendors, prices and part codes to procurement if you are unsure. A good description of what and why is far more useful than a guess at the supplier.
