The list

Each vendor row shows its code, name, category, country, contact, default payment terms, typical lead time, and whether it is active. Search matches across code, name, category, country, contact and GSTIN. Anyone in procurement can view the list; the New Vendor button and the per-row Edit appear only for those who can manage vendors.

A vendor record

Identity

Name, category, source country, location.

Terms

Currency, default payment terms and typical lead time — these pre-fill the PO.

Contact

Name, phone and email.

GSTIN & address

The supplier’s tax number and address. The GSTIN’s state decides whether a PO is taxed as CGST+SGST or IGST.

Supplied items

Once a vendor is saved, you can record what they supply: by product, by part, or by PO category. This is what lets Snorkel suggest the right vendors when you raise a PO. Add an item by picking the reference and the PO category it belongs to.

⛔ The GSTIN drives the tax split

Whether a rupee PO is taxed as CGST + SGST (same state as us) or IGST (different state) is decided by the vendor’s GSTIN. A wrong or missing GSTIN means the wrong tax on every PO to that supplier, so get it right when you create the vendor.
