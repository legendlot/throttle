Social is the team’s content calendar: plan posts across channels, group them into campaigns, and watch a health monitor for posts at risk. Everyone can view it; the brand team can create and edit.

What it’s for
Planning and tracking social posts.
Read or act?
View all; brand team edits.
Who
Requester Member Lead Admin
Tabs
Calendar · Campaigns · Monitor.

The three tabs

  • Calendar: a month grid of posts. A post moves through idea → draft → approved → published (or cancelled), and holds one or more channel variants (each with its caption and asset, published per channel). Create a post with a title, date and at least one variant.
  • Campaigns: group posts under a named campaign with a date range and a status (active, completed, archived).
  • Monitor: an At Risk list (overdue posts and ones missing assets within 24 hours) plus an 8-week cadence table per channel.
ⓘ Who can change what

Anyone can view the calendar, but only the brand team can create or edit posts and campaigns; moving a post to approved and deleting a post are lead/admin actions. The Monitor tab’s red badge is your early warning that a scheduled post still has no asset.
