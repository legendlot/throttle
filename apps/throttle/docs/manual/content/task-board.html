The Board is the brand team’s live Kanban of active tasks. Members work their tasks here and leads triage the flow. Clicking any task opens a side panel with everything about it and every action you can take.

What it’s for
Working and tracking tasks.

Who
Member Lead Admin
Views
Kanban or Table.

The columns

The Kanban has a column per stage: Backlog, In Sprint, In Progress, Ext. Blocked, In Review, Approved, Delivered, Done. Each card shows its ID (for example T-001), type, title, product, owner and due date (amber when close, red when overdue). Drag a card to move it; you can only make the moves your role allows, and an illegal drop snaps back. A Table view, a person filter, and a / search are also there.

Working a task (the side panel)

Click a task to open its panel. From here you (depending on role and stage):

  • Assign: claim a task as owner, add yourself as a collaborator, or (lead/admin) set the owner.
  • Move stage: pick an allowed next stage; moving to Ext. Blocked or Abandoned needs a typed reason.
  • Submit for Review: add the deliverable link to send your work to a lead (moves it to In Review).
  • Review work (lead/admin): Approve it, or request a revision with feedback (sends it back to In Progress).
  • Deliver to the requester, or Mark Done; set priority and due date; add notes, attachments and comments.
ⓘ The flow in one line

A task goes Backlog → pulled into a sprint → a member takes it In Progress → submits for review → a lead approves the work → it’s delivered to the requester (who accepts, or it auto-accepts) → Done. A revision sends it back a step; Ext. Blocked parks it; Abandoned stops it. Big requests appear as sibling tasks like T-001/2.
