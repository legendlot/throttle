Requests is where work is asked for and tracked. A requester sees their own requests; a lead or admin sees everything coming in. It is the home screen and the starting point for new work.

What it’s for
Tracking work requests.
Read or act?
Read; launches new requests.
Who
Requester Member Lead Admin
Heading
“My Requests” / “All Requests”.

Reading it

Each request card shows its type, title, status badge, how long it has sat in its current stage, and the created date. Filter with the pills: Needs Action (yours that need a response), All, Pending, Approved, Rejected, Info Needed. Status badges: Pending (yellow), Approved (green), Rejected (red), Info Needed (blue). The time-in-stage chip turns amber after a day and red after two.

Acting on a request

+ New Request starts a new one. A lead or admin also gets an Approval Queue button. On your own cards, contextual buttons appear: Update & Resubmit when a request needs more info, Resubmit when it was rejected, and Delivered: Give Feedback when work is ready for your review.

ⓘ You see what your role allows

Requesters see only their own requests; leads and admins see the whole incoming queue. The “Needs Action” pill is the fastest way for a requester to find a request the brand team has bounced back for more information.
