The Dashboard is the manager’s reporting view: output, workload and sprint health. Leads and admins use it to see what the team is producing and where the pressure is.

What it’s for
Team output and workload reporting.
Read or act?
Read; CSV export.
Who
Lead Admin
Scope
By sprint, by person.

The three sections

  • Summary cards: In Review, Overdue, Ext. Blocked, Completion % and Spillovers. Click a card to drill into that bucket of tasks.
  • Deliverables Output: a table of completed deliverables by type, viewable by date or by person, over a date range, with a CSV export.
  • Team Workload: each person’s tasks across the stages; a person’s total turns orange when they are carrying a lot, an overload signal.
ⓘ Slice it by sprint and person

Use the sprint selector and the person filter at the top to focus the whole dashboard. The summary cards are clickable shortcuts into the exact tasks behind each number, which is the quickest way to act on what the report shows.
