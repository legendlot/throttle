Team

The team table, grouped into Lead, Brand Team and Requesters, lists each person with a Role dropdown (admin, lead, member, requester) and a Discipline dropdown (designer, 3D, copywriter, photo/video, social, lead). Changes save inline.

Ageing thresholds

For each stage you set a Warning, Critical and Auto-close time in hours. These drive the colour of the ageing dots on task cards and, importantly, the time after which delivered work auto-accepts if the requester has not responded.

⚠ You cannot change your own role

Your own role dropdown is disabled, so an admin cannot accidentally lock themselves out; another admin must make that change. Because the ageing thresholds control auto-accept, adjust them deliberately: a short auto-close means delivered work closes itself sooner.
