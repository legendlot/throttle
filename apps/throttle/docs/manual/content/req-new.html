New Request is a short wizard for asking the brand team for work. You pick a request type, choose the product if relevant, fill the type’s form, review, and submit.

What it’s for
Submitting a work request.
Read or act?
Action: creates a request.
Who
Requester Member Lead Admin
Steps
Type, product, form, review.

The request types

Pick the type that matches what you need; each has its own tailored form. The ten types are: Launch Pack (a full asset bundle for a new product), Product Creative, Social Media, Advertising, Photo & Video, Copy & Script, Design & Brand, 3D & Motion, Sale Event, and Brand Initiative (internal, brand team only).

Filling it in

  1. Choose the type and, where it applies, the product (some types let you pick several).
  2. Complete the form. Fields vary by type; a title is always required, and some fields only appear based on earlier answers.
  3. Review and submit. A success screen confirms it. Leads will then approve, reject or ask for more info.
⚠ Some types create several tasks

Launch Pack and Sale Event have a checklist, and each ticked item becomes its own task when the request is approved (the screen shows how many). Photo & Video with editing turned on creates two tasks (a shoot and an edit). So one request can spin up a whole batch of work.
