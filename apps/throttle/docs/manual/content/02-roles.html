Throttle has four roles, from the people who ask for work to the people who do and manage it. This chapter explains each one and what it can do.

The four roles

Requester

Non-brand staff who submit work requests and review what comes back. They see only Requests and Social, and only their own requests.

Member

Brand team members (designers, copywriters, video, 3D, social). They work tasks on the board: self-assign, move their tasks forward, and submit work for review.

Lead

Brand managers. Everything a member does, plus approving requests, running sprints, approving submitted work, setting priority and owners, and the dashboard.

Admin

Full control, including Settings (managing roles, disciplines and the ageing thresholds that drive auto-accept).

ⓘ Role versus discipline

A person also has a discipline (designer, 3D, copywriter, photo/video, social, lead), set in Settings, which is separate from their role. The role decides what you can do; the discipline describes what kind of work you do. Both are managed by an admin.

Who can move a task

The board only lets you make the moves your role allows; an illegal drag quietly snaps back. In short: members move their own tasks between In Sprint, In Progress, In Review and Ext. Blocked; leads and admins can do everything else (pull from backlog into a sprint, approve work, deliver, mark done, change priority and owners, and abandon).
