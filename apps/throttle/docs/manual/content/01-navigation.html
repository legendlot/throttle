Throttle opens with your work Google account and uses a simple top bar of tabs. This chapter covers signing in and finding your way around.

Signing in

Open throttle.legendoftoys.com and sign in with your @legendoftoys.com Google account. Personal accounts are refused. Signed in, you land on Requests.

The top bar

Throttle uses a horizontal nav across the top (not a sidebar). Which tabs you see depends on your role:

TabVisible to
RequestsEveryone.
BoardMember, Lead, Admin.
SprintsLead, Admin.
DashboardLead, Admin.
SocialEveryone.
SettingsAdmin.

On the right of the bar are a notification bell (for the brand team), your name and role, and Sign out. On the Board and Sprints, press / to open a quick task search.

ⓘ The notification bell

The bell (brand team only) checks every minute for activity in the last 7 days on tasks you are assigned to, excluding your own actions, and shows an unread count. Requesters do not see it. Most screens otherwise load when you open them and refresh after you act, rather than updating on their own.
