Giving Feedback is where a requester reviews work the brand team has delivered and either accepts it or asks for a revision. You reach it from the “Delivered: Give Feedback” button on your request.

What it’s for
Accepting or revising delivered work.
Read or act?
Action: accept / request revision.
Who
Requester
Reached from
A delivered request.

Reviewing delivered work

Each delivered task shows the team’s delivery message and the links to the work. For each one you choose Looks good (accept it) or Needs revision (which opens a box for what should change, plus an optional reference link). Submit your feedback for all the delivered tasks at once; the brand team is notified.

⚠ Work auto-accepts if you do nothing

A delivered task shows an “Auto-accepts on [date]” note: if you do not respond by then, it is accepted automatically and marked done. So if something needs changing, choose Needs revision before that date. Asking for a revision sends the task back to the team to rework.
