The Approval Queue is where leads and admins review incoming requests and decide what happens. Approving a request is what turns it into actual tasks for the brand team.

Reviewing a request

The queue lists pending requests oldest first, with a time-in-stage chip (grey, then amber after a day, red after two). Click one to see its full detail: type, title, who submitted it, the products and notes, and all the form fields. Then make a decision:

DecisionWhat it does
ApproveCreates the task(s) and starts the work. A note is optional.
RejectDeclines the request. A note is required, and goes back to the requester.
Need InfoSends it back for more detail. A note is required; the requester can update and resubmit.
ⓘ Approval creates the work

This is the “request approval” step (distinct from approving finished work later on the board). Approving a Launch Pack or Sale Event creates several tasks at once. Reject and Need Info both require a note so the requester knows why.
