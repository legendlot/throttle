Throttle is the brand team’s work operating system. Anyone in the company asks the brand team for creative work through a request; once approved, that request becomes one or more tasks the brand team works on a board and schedules into sprints. This manual is your self-serve guide to every screen in it.

Request, then task

Request

What someone asks for (a launch pack, a social post, a video). A requester submits it; a lead approves, rejects, or asks for more info. Approving turns it into work.

Task

A unit of work for the brand team, created when a request is approved. Tasks live on the board and move through stages until delivered and done. A big request (a launch pack) becomes several tasks at once.

How a task flows

Every task moves along this path: Backlog → In Sprint → In Progress → In Review → Approved → Delivered → Done. A task can be parked at Ext. Blocked (waiting on something outside the team) or stopped at Abandoned. Two approvals happen along the way, and they are different things:

  • Request approval: a lead approves the incoming request, which creates the task(s).
  • Work approval: a lead approves the deliverable a member submitted (In Review), sending it on to be delivered or marked done.
⚠ Delivered is not Done

Delivered means the work is with the requester for feedback; it becomes Done when the requester accepts it, when it auto-accepts after a set time, or when a lead marks it done. Keep the two approvals straight too: approving a request creates work; approving the work moves a finished task forward.
