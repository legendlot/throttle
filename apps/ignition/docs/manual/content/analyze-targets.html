Targets is where you set a views goal and a spend budget for each month, then watch how the real numbers track against them. It turns the month into a simple scorecard: are we on pace for views, and are we inside budget?

Setting a month

  1. Pick the month. In the top panel, choose the month you are setting a goal for.
  2. Enter the goals. Type a target views number and a budget in rupees, plus an optional note.
  3. Save. The month appears in the tracking table below, and re-saving the same month overwrites it.

Reading the tracking table

One row per month shows the target views beside the actual views achieved, and the budget beside the actual spend, each with a progress bar. The current month is marked in orange. The actuals are not typed in: they add themselves up from the live deals and payments for that month, so the table always reflects reality without anyone updating it.

What the colours mean

BarReading it
ViewsFills toward the goal and turns green once it reaches it. More is better.
SpendStays calm while there is room, shifts amber as it nears the budget and red once over. Less is better.
★ Click a row to edit it

Clicking any month in the tracking table loads its numbers back into the panel at the top, ready to adjust and save again. It is the quick way to nudge a goal without retyping the month.

Drilling into a month

The arrow next to a month expands it into what makes up the numbers: Spend (each deal’s amount, linking to the deal), Views (each post, with the influencer and posted date) and Conversions (orders and their value). Spend also has an Unallocated row above the table, for spend that has not yet landed against any month.

A deal with more than one video posted in different months has each of its takes counted in the month it posted, not the month the deal’s first video went up — a second take that posts next month no longer overstates this month’s total. In the Views drill-down, a take other than the first shows a small #2 (or #3, and so on) after the influencer’s name, with that take’s own posted date next to it. Every month’s rows still add up to that month’s tile. Spend and conversions are unaffected: they stay deal-level, counted by the deal’s own post date, or under Unallocated if it has none.

ⓘ Targets are per month, for the whole team

A target covers the month as a whole, not a single influencer or product, and there is one set of numbers per month. Everyone can see the tracking; setting and changing the goals needs manage access. If you only see the table and not the set-a-month panel, your access is view-only, which is normal.
