The B-List is the parked-candidates list: creators who are interested but not a current priority, a “maybe later” bench. Open it to revisit prospects you have talked to but are not actively pursuing.

What it’s for
Parked, lower-priority prospects.
Read or act?
Read; click through to a profile.
Who
Marketer Lead Admin
Includes
Influencers on the B-List.

Using it

Each row shows the code, channel, type, reach, and a Comments column noting why the creator is parked. Click a row to open the profile, where you can move them onto the Master list when you are ready to pursue them.

⚠ B-List means “Plan B”, not “banned”

Despite the name, the B-List is a bench of interested-but-not-yet-aligned creators, not a blocklist. It is where you keep leads warm for later, not where you put people you will not work with.
