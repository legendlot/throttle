Schedule shows when deals are going live across the month: a calendar of every post that is either already up or expected, so you can see the content pipeline at a glance and spot quiet or crowded weeks.

Who is overdue to post

Above the calendar, a strip counts the creators who were sent something 10 or more days ago and still have not posted. Click it to see who, how many days it has been, and whether they have an email address and a tracking link on record. Clicking a row opens that deal.

ⓘ It lists, it does not chase

Nothing is sent automatically — this is the worklist you work by hand. The strip disappears when nobody is overdue.

Moving around

The header carries the month name with arrows either side to step back and forward a month, and a Today button to jump back to the current month. On the right, two toggles switch between the Calendar and the List view of the same month. A counter shows how many posts fall in the month you are looking at.

Posted vs planned

Each deal appears on its go-live date. If the post is already up, that date is its real post date; if it is still upcoming, the date is the expected post date. A small dot tells the two apart:

DotMeaning
Filled (orange)Posted — the video is live, dated by its real post date.
Hollow (outline)Planned — expected, dated by the date the deal is due to go live.

The two views

In the Calendar view, each day cell lists the influencers posting that day; today’s cell is tinted. If a day has more than four, it shows the first few and a “+N more” line. In the List view, the same deals appear as rows with date, deal, influencer, product, stage, whether each is posted or planned, and a link to the video where one exists. In either view, clicking a deal opens its engagement.

ⓘ Only dated deals show here

A deal appears on the Schedule once it has a go-live date, whether expected or actual. Deals that have no date yet simply do not show up until one is set on the engagement. To change when something lands, set its date on the deal itself, not here.
