Ignition gives each person the access that matches their job. This chapter explains the three roles used throughout this manual.

The three roles

Marketer

Marketing Team. Runs deals day to day: searches influencers, opens engagements, advances them through the pipeline, sets quality ratings and adds notes.

Lead

Lead. Everything a marketer does, plus approval rights and access to the Reports.

Admin

Admin. Full access, including the admin screens (the user-roles reference and the data-import runbook).

ⓘ How access actually works

Ignition uses the shared company permission map. The relevant permissions are ignition_view, ignition_manage, ignition_approve, ignition_reports_view and ignition_admin, bundled into the ignition_manager and ignition_lead roles (admins get everything). Roles are actually assigned in Garage (the store app) at its Users screen, not inside Ignition. If you cannot reach something you should, ask an admin to set your role there.
