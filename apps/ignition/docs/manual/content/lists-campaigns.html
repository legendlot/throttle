Campaigns lists your named marketing campaigns. A campaign has an optional budget and spans many deals and influencers, so you can see what a push has spent against what you set aside for it.

What it’s for
Named campaigns and their spend vs budget.
Read or act?
Read, plus New campaign.
Who
Marketer Lead Admin
Status
Live.

Reading it

Each card shows the campaign name and number, its status, how many linked deals have posted (for example 2/5), Consumed spend against the budget with a progress bar, organic views and orders. The bar and percentage appear only when a budget is set; a blank budget is never read as ₹0. The search box above the list filters the loaded campaigns by name or number. New campaign asks for a name and an optional budget, then opens the new campaign. Click a card to open the campaign, where you get its rolled-up numbers (linked deals, how many have posted, total spend, views and orders) and can attach or detach deals. In Link deal, type a deal number or influencer to search, then click a row or use the ↑ ↓ keys and press Enter to link it; Enter does not pick a row for you, and a deal already linked says so. Edit changes the campaign’s Name, Budget (leave blank for no budget) and Status. A cancelled deal’s spend is shown struck through in the deals table and is not counted in Consumed.

The campaign brief

Each campaign has a Campaign brief slot: press Upload brief and pick the file you want the creators to work from. It is stored privately — the link the app opens is short-lived, so the brief is never a public URL — and you can Replace it whenever the brief is rewritten. The name and upload date show next to it, so it is obvious when a campaign is running on an old brief.
