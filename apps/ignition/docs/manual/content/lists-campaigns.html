Reading it

Each row shows the campaign number, the influencer, how many videos it bundles, the agreed total (₹) and a status. Click a row to open the campaign, where you get its rolled-up numbers — linked deals, how many have posted, total spend, views and orders — and can attach or detach deals.

The campaign brief

Each campaign has a Campaign brief slot: press Upload brief and pick the file you want the creators to work from. It is stored privately — the link the app opens is short-lived, so the brief is never a public URL — and you can Replace it whenever the brief is rewritten. The name and upload date show next to it, so it is obvious when a campaign is running on an old brief.
