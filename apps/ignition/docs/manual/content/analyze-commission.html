Commission is the monthly pay-out to a creator on an affiliate deal. This screen is where D2C checks each month’s figure, confirms the final amount and sends it on to be paid.

What it’s for
Checking and verifying monthly commission.
Read or act?
Read, plus Verify for D2C.
Who
Commission verifier (D2C) Lead Admin
Where
Analyze → Commission.

What commission is

For each affiliate deal, commission for a month is the sales made with the creator’s code that month, after returns, without GST or shipping, times the deal’s affiliate %. It applies to Affiliate and Paid + Affiliate deals, for sales from November 2026 onwards. Nothing earlier is counted.

Ignition works out an estimate for every month and refreshes it every morning until the month is verified, so late returns lower it by themselves. Once D2C verifies a month, the amount is locked.

Finding it

Open Analyze → Commission. The Month picker starts at Nov 2026 and opens on last month, the one D2C verifies. The running month is marked “so far” and shows a banner: its numbers are estimates and it can be verified only once the month is over. Estimates show when they were last updated. Leads can see the screen (view only); the people who verify are the Commission verifier and admins.

Reading the table

One row per deal. The columns are: Deal (opens the deal) and Creator; Orders; Net sales (ex-GST); Rate (the deal’s affiliate %); Ignition estimate; State (Estimate or Verified); Verified amount (shown in amber when it differs from the estimate); Reason; Verified by (name and date); and Payment (the status of the payment request, with its Snorkel number). A total row sits at the bottom.

Verifying a month

  1. Click Verify on the row (available once the month is over).
  2. Check the amount. Verified amount (₹) is filled with Ignition’s estimate. Keep it, or type the final figure after returns and cancellations.
  3. Give a reason if it differs. A different amount needs the Reason for the difference; at the estimate it is not needed.
  4. Click Verify. Your name and the date are stamped and the amount locks. A payment request is raised in Snorkel and waits for the creator’s invoice.

A verified amount of ₹0 closes the month with nothing to pay; no request is raised.

Verify at estimate (several rows)

Tick the rows that are right as they stand (or the box at the top for all of them), then click Verify at estimate. A confirmation names the month, how many raise a request and how many close at ₹0. Each row is done in turn; if some are refused, the list shows which and why, and the rest still go through.

Admins only

  • Reopen clears a wrongly verified month and puts it back to an estimate, to be verified again. It works only while the month’s payment request is not live or paid (for example after it was rejected or cancelled).
  • Rebuild estimates (top right) recalculates the month on screen straight away instead of waiting for the morning update.
⚠ If Verify is refused

Two common reasons. The month is still running: wait until it is over. Or some of the month’s orders have not synced from Shopify yet: sync the deal’s code first (Reports → Sync all, or from the code on the deal), then verify.

ⓘ Commission is on top of the fee

Commission is paid separately from the agreed fee. It never uses up the fee, and it never stops you raising or logging the fee. See Paying the Creator for the invoice.
