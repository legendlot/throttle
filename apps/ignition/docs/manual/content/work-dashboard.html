The greeting and the Video / UGC / All toggle

The page opens with today’s date and week and a one-line headline: how many posts are late, or how many drafts are waiting for review, or “All clear”. The Video / UGC / All toggle at the top right switches the pipeline and the figures below it between video deals, UGC deals, or both together. Video is the default.

The pipeline

The wide Pipeline card shows the number of active deals and the rupees committed (the total cost of the deals in the selected view). Under it a coloured bar splits the deals by stage, with a count under each stage name along the happy path (Proposed through Live; UGC uses its own stage names). Click the card to open Engagements.

The tiles

The UGC view adds UGC deals, budget consumed, likes, orders and conversion value. The All view adds Ghosted, total likes, total shares and the count of non-compliant deals.

Overdue posts and Re-engage

Overdue posts lists deals whose expected post is late, with how many days. People who can manage deals also get Flag overdue as red, which rates those creators Red. Click a row to open the deal.

Re-engage lists Green-rated creators who have been idle 60 days or more, with a count of gifted-but-never-posted deals and the unrecovered value. Each row has a Re-book button (for people who can manage deals) that opens New deal with that influencer already selected. Click the name instead to open their profile.

ⓘ Figures load once

The Dashboard loads when you open it and does not refresh by itself; reopen the page to update it.
