Payments is the running log of what we have paid influencers, deal by deal. Every advance and final payment goes here, so the totals at the top always show what has gone out today, this week, this month and all time.

The layout

Four spend tiles sit across the top: Today, This Week, This Month and All Time. Each shows the total paid in that window, the number of payments, and how many different influencers they covered. Below them is the log itself: one row per payment, newest first, showing the date paid, the influencer, the deal, the kind, the amount, and any note. The deal in a row links straight to that engagement.

Recording a payment

  1. Open the form. Click Record Payment in the top right.
  2. Find the influencer. Search by code, handle or name (type at least two characters) and pick from the results. Use Change to reselect.
  3. Pick the deal. Choose which of that influencer’s deals the payment is for. If they only have one, it is selected for you. If they have none, the form tells you to create a deal first.
  4. Enter the details. Choose the kind (Advance, Final or Other), the amount, the date paid, and an optional note such as a transfer reference. Then save.

The three kinds

KindUse it for
AdvanceMoney paid up front, before the work is delivered.
FinalThe balance paid once the deal is done.
OtherAnything that is neither an advance nor a final balance.
ⓘ A payment always belongs to a deal

You cannot log a loose payment against an influencer with no deal: pick the engagement it relates to. Payments recorded here also show up on that engagement’s own page, where the Deal Terms card reads “₹X paid of ₹Y agreed”, so the deal always reflects what has actually been sent.

⚠ Removing a payment is instant

The trash icon at the end of a row deletes that payment straight away, with no undo, and the spend tiles drop to match. Only remove a row if it was logged in error; to correct an amount, delete the wrong row and record the right one.
