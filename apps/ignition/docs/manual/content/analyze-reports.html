Reports is the spend and performance analysis for a date range: how many deals, what they cost, what they returned, and which products and creators did best.

Choosing the range

Four presets sit at the top right: 30d (the last 30 days), 90d, YTD (1 January to today) and All (everything on record). The page opens on YTD. The chosen preset is highlighted; you can also set the from and to dates by hand, in which case no preset is highlighted.

What it shows

Summary tiles give Deals created, Deals posted, Total spend, orders, Sales (coupon, net), Sales per ₹1 (coupon net sales divided by spend), Blended CPM, Median CPM, organic and paid views, likes and shares. Blended CPM is the cost of the deals that have views, divided by their organic views, times 1,000. Median CPM is the CPM of the middle deal. Commission and ad rights are left out of the cost. Panels below break the range down: By influencer tier, UGC, Spend by month, the Sales per ₹1 and CPM distributions, Spend by product, and Top performers. If nothing falls in the range you see “No deals in range”.

Which date each number uses

Deals created counts by the date the deal was created. Spend, orders, sales and likes use the deal’s post date. Views use each video’s own post date, so a month reads the same as on Targets. Dropped and ghosted deals count on their created date. The Total spend tile spells out what it includes (spend on dropped or ghosted deals, by created date) and what it leaves out (deals created in the range that have not posted yet).

Sync all

With manage access, a Sync all button sits at the top. It refreshes every active code from Shopify, affiliate codes first, and shows progress as it goes. Keep the page open until it finishes; if it stops, press it again and it carries on. The Sales tile shows when the active codes were last synced. All active codes also sync by themselves every day at 08:40 IST.
