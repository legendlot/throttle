Reports is the spend and performance analysis for a date range: how many deals, what they cost, what they returned, and which products and creators did best.

What it’s for
Spend & performance analytics.
Read or act?
Read-only.
Who
Lead Admin
Range
30d · 90d · YTD · All.

Choosing the range

Four presets sit at the top right: 30d (the last 30 days), 90d, YTD (1 January to today) and All (everything on record). The page opens on YTD. The chosen preset is highlighted; you can also set the from and to dates by hand, in which case no preset is highlighted.

What it shows

Summary tiles give deals, total spend, orders, conversion value, average CPM, average ROAS, organic and paid views, likes and shares. Panels below break the range down: By influencer tier, UGC, Spend by month, the ROAS and CPM distributions, Spend by product, and Top performers. If nothing falls in the range you see “No deals in range”.
