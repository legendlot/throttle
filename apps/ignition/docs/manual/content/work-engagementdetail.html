The engagement detail screen is the control centre for one deal: its terms, product, costs, logistics and performance, plus the controls to advance the pipeline, add notes, and flag damage to customer support. This is where most of the work happens.

What it’s for
Running one deal end to end.

Pipeline
13 stages, 3 failure exits.

The layout

The header shows the engagement number, the influencer, the current stage and deal badges, and two buttons: Advance and Flag Damage to Pitstop. A pipeline stepper shows progress. Below sit cards for Deal Terms, Product, Costs (with the rolled-up total in orange), Logistics, Post-live and Performance (views, orders, ROAS), followed by Notes and a History log.

Advancing the deal

Click Advance to move the deal to its next stage. A modal offers only the stages you are allowed to move to from where you are, plus an optional note. The happy path is Identified, Invited, Engaged, Negotiating, Agreed, Shipped, Delivered, Script Review, Script Signed Off, Scheduled, Live, Tracking, Closed; a deal that falls through exits to Declined, Ghosted or Dropped (from which the only move is to Closed).

ⓘ You cannot skip stages

Advance only ever offers the permitted next stages, so a deal moves forward one controlled step at a time and never jumps ahead or back. Once a deal is Closed the Advance button is disabled: it is a dead end. Notes you add and every stage move are recorded in the History log.

⛔ Flagging damage opens a real support ticket

Flag Damage to Pitstop opens a genuine ticket in the Pitstop customer-support system, prefilled with this deal’s influencer info, with a disposition (replacement, refund or repair) and a required description. This is a deliberate manual handoff, used when a product sent to an influencer arrives damaged. Only one Pitstop ticket can be opened per engagement; once raised, its number shows in the Logistics card.
