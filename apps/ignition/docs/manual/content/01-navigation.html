Ignition opens with your work Google account and everything sits in one slim icon rail. This chapter covers signing in and finding your way around.

Signing in

Open ignition.legendoftoys.com and sign in with your @legendoftoys.com Google account. Personal accounts are refused; if sign-in loops, sign out of any personal Google account first or use a fresh browser profile.

The menu

GroupWhat lives here
WORKDashboard, Connects, Influencers, Engagements, Schedule, Payments. (New Deal is the orange + New deal button in the top bar.)
LISTSRoster, B-List, UGC, Campaigns, Codes.
ANALYZEReports (for leads and admins) and Targets.
HELP & ADMINSystem Manual for everyone; Users and Import for admins only.

You only see the groups your role allows. Most list screens have a search box, and amounts are shown in rupees (₹).

The rail and the top bar

On a desktop the menu is an icon rail. It starts collapsed (icons only; hover an icon for its name). Click the panel button at the top of the rail, or press [ on your keyboard (when you are not typing in a box), to expand it to show the labels. Ignition remembers your choice on that browser. Your name sits at the bottom of the rail; clicking it asks you to confirm before signing you out.

The top bar holds + New deal and the app launcher. Where a page has its own search box, the top bar also shows Search this page (shortcut / or Cmd/Ctrl+K), which jumps your cursor into that page’s search box. It is not a global search or a command palette: it only searches the screen you are on, and it is hidden on pages with no search.

On a phone

At 767 pixels wide or less the rail is replaced by a bottom tab bar: Home (Dashboard), People (Influencers), Deals (Engagements), Schedule and More. More opens a sheet with the full menu, grouped like the rail, including New Deal.

ⓘ Screens load once

Ignition screens load their data when you open them and do not refresh on their own. To see new information after something changes, change a filter or reopen the page. Most lists show up to 100 or 200 rows; if a list looks cut off, narrow it with the search box or filters.
