The UGC Pipeline tracks commission-based user-generated-content deals, where the creator is paid by results. It shows ad spend, commission and return on ad spend (ROAS) alongside each deal’s stage.

What it’s for
Watching UGC deals and their ROAS.
Read or act?
Read; click through to a deal.
Who
Marketer Lead Admin
Shows
UGC engagements only.

Using it

Each row shows the engagement number, influencer, the current stage badge, the product, ad spend, commission, and ROAS (return on ad spend, to two decimals). The list is filtered to UGC deals automatically. Click a row to open the engagement and edit its numbers.

ⓘ “₹0” usually means not entered yet

Ad spend and commission default to ₹0 and ROAS shows a dash until measured, so an empty-looking row usually means the figures have not been entered yet, not that performance was zero. You enter those numbers on the engagement’s detail page, not here. The list shows up to 200 rows.
