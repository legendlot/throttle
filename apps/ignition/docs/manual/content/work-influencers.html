Profiles with no usable link

A strip at the top of the list counts influencers whose channel link is not a real web address — usually a browser tab title pasted by mistake, like “(9) Instagram”. These break profile matching and can make one person look like several. Click fix these to work through them.

Where the handle is clear, a suggested link is offered and one click accepts it. Where it is not — a display name rather than a handle, or a YouTube channel — there is a box to paste the real profile URL.

⚠ Check the suggestion before accepting it

A suggestion is built from the handle, so it can point at somebody else entirely if the handle is a common word. It is never applied on its own; that click is you confirming it is the right person.

The list

Tabs split influencers into Master, B-List and Archived. Each row shows the code, channel (and person), type (nano, micro, macro, brand, store), category, reach, location, and a rating badge. Search by code, handle, name, phone or email, and filter by type and rating. Click a row to open the profile.

Location

When you add or edit a creator, Location offers a list of cities and states as you type. Pick one where you can — typing the same city three different ways is what left Bengaluru sitting in the filter four times over, each version finding a quarter of the people. If the creator’s town is not on the list, type it anyway: the field accepts anything, and a known spelling is tidied up for you when you move on (BLR becomes Bangalore, Kerela becomes Kerala).

The profile, and the rating

A creator’s detail page shows their identity and contact details, and a table of all their engagements. Its key action is the quality rating: four buttons in the header set the creator to Green, Yellow, Red or Unrated. This is the team’s signal on whether a creator is worth working with again, and it drives the rating filter and the Roster.

⚠ Rating saves instantly

Clicking a rating button saves immediately with no confirmation, so a wrong click is live at once: just click the correct one to fix it. “First invite: Not sent” on the profile means no invite has gone out to that creator yet.
