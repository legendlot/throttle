The list

Tabs split influencers into Master, B-List and Archived. Each row shows the code, channel (and person), type (nano, micro, macro, brand, store), category, reach, location, and a rating badge. Search by code, handle, name, phone or email, and filter by type and rating. Click a row to open the profile.

The profile, and the rating

A creator’s detail page shows their identity and contact details, and a table of all their engagements. Its key action is the quality rating: four buttons in the header set the creator to Green, Yellow, Red or Unrated. This is the team’s signal on whether a creator is worth working with again, and it drives the rating filter and the Roster.

⚠ Rating saves instantly

Clicking a rating button saves immediately with no confirmation, so a wrong click is live at once: just click the correct one to fix it. “First invite: Not sent” on the profile means no invite has gone out to that creator yet.
