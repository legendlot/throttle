Ignition is the influencer-marketing CRM for Legend of Toys. It tracks the creators we work with and every deal we run with them, from first contact through the live post and its results. This manual is your self-serve guide to every screen in it.

The two things Ignition tracks

Influencers

The people and channels we work with: their reach, contact details, quality rating, and which list they sit on (the proven roster, the parked B-List, or archived).

Engagements

A single deal with one influencer (also called a “deal”). Each engagement moves through a pipeline of stages and records the terms, costs, logistics and performance.

How a deal flows

Every engagement moves along a pipeline. The happy path runs: Proposed → Planning → Shipped → Delivered → Draft received → Scheduled → Live. Live is the finish line for a video deal — the video is up, the deal is done. A deal that does not work out exits to Ghosted, Dropped or Cancelled, and On hold and Delayed are there for a deal that has stalled without dying.

ⓘ Dropped costs money. Cancelled does not.

The difference matters, because it decides whether the deal shows up in your spend. Dropped is a loss: the goods went out and never became a video, so its cost keeps counting in Reports, monthly spend and CPM. Cancelled is for a deal called off before anything was spent — postponed, or stopped before the products shipped. A cancelled deal keeps its record but is left out of every total, and it will never be chased as overdue to post.

You can move a deal to any stage, in any order — real deals do not queue politely — with one exception: a deal starts in Proposed and cannot move on until it is approved. Approving it sends it straight to Planning, so you never have to approve and then advance it as two separate jobs. You can still drop or ghost a proposal you are turning down without approving it first.

ⓘ Two badges, two meanings

Watch for two colour systems. A stage badge shows where a deal is in the pipeline (orange means it is Live or being Tracked, the “money on air” stages). A rating badge (Green, Yellow, Red, Unrated) is your quality judgment of an influencer, not a deal. Do not confuse them.
