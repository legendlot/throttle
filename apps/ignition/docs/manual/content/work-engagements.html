Reading it

Tabs give quick stage filters (All, Live, Scheduled, Draft rcvd, Delivered). Each row shows the engagement number, the influencer (channel + code), type (Video or UGC), the current stage badge, the deal type, the product and its variant, the expected post date, the actual post date, views, CPM and total cost. A small amber ⚑ beside an engagement number means that deal is not attached to any campaign — hover it for the reason. It is there so a missing campaign is catchable while you scan the list, rather than only when you go looking; the campaign filter's No campaign option gives you the same deals as a filtered view, with a count. Search by engagement number, video link, tracking or order. On the All tab, stage chips let you pick several stages at once; on the other tabs the tab itself sets the stage.

The four tiles

Above the table sit four tiles — Deals, Total cost, Views and Blended CPM. They always describe exactly the rows you are looking at, so filter the list down to a product, a stage or a date range and the tiles re-count for that selection. Blended CPM is worked out across the deals that actually have views; deals that have not posted yet are left out of it, otherwise their cost would make the CPM look worse than it is.

Filtering

Beyond the tabs and stage chips there is a deal type filter (Paid, Barter, Affiliate, Paid + Affiliate), a product filter (multi-select, the products actually on deals, with a count each), a campaign filter, and a posting-date filter with four modes. All of them combine — pick Barter and a campaign together and the list narrows to deals matching both, not either.

  • Any date — no date filtering.
  • Upcoming — not posted yet, expected today or later.
  • Overdue — not posted, and the expected date has already passed.
  • Date range — pick a From and To. A deal is judged on its actual post date, or its expected date if it has not posted.
ⓘ Your filters stay put

Filters survive going into a deal and coming back — they only reset when you clear them yourself with clear all filters. And a deal you edit stays where it is in the list: the order is the order deals were added, so nothing jumps to the top while you are reading.

ⓘ “New Deal” makes an engagement

The orange + New Deal button creates a new engagement (deal and engagement are the same thing here). Click any row to open that engagement and work it.
