Reading it

Tabs give quick stage filters (All, Live, Shipped, Script, Closed). Each row shows the engagement number, the influencer (channel + code), type (Video or UGC), the current stage badge, the deal type, the product, post date and total cost. Search by engagement number, video link, tracking or order. On the All tab a Stage dropdown lets you pick any of the 16 stages; on the other tabs the tab itself sets the stage.

ⓘ “New Deal” makes an engagement

The orange + New Deal button creates a new engagement (deal and engagement are the same thing here). Click any row to open that engagement and work it.
