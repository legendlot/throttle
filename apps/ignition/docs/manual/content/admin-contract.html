Writing the wording

Open Admin, Contract wording. There are three templates: Barter, Paid and Affiliate, picked with the tab row at the top. Each has its own Dos and don’ts and Terms. The Subject (the email subject) is shared by all three. Ignition supplies no wording of its own; every contract uses exactly what is written here. Save keeps your draft, and saves all three templates at once. A • on a tab means that template has unsaved changes; (empty) on a tab means it is missing a section. Two banners sit at the top: whether the wording is published, and whether partners@legendoftoys.com is reachable (if it is not, contracts will fail to send until that clears).

Which template gets sent

The deal type on the deal picks the template. A barter deal gets Barter, a paid deal gets Paid, and an affiliate deal gets Affiliate. A paid + affiliate deal gets Paid, with a commission line added automatically from the deal’s commission %, for example: “Commission: 10% of the sales made with your discount code, excluding GST, worked out every month”. You do not type that line into the wording.

All three templates started as copies of the wording published on 9 Nov 2026, so nothing changes for anyone until a template is edited.

Publishing

Publish needs Dos and don’ts and Terms filled in on all three templates. Once published, none of them can be emptied again. It asks you to confirm, because publishing turns on sending and the Shipping lock: from then on a video deal under the lock cannot reach Shipped or beyond until the creator accepts or someone records it by hand. Which deals come under the lock is explained in The Deal Contract.

ⓘ Later edits only affect new contracts

After publishing, saving a change applies to the next contract anyone opens. Contracts already sent keep the words they went out with.
