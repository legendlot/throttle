Writing the wording

Open Admin, Contract. Three boxes: Subject (the email subject), Dos and don’ts and Terms. Ignition supplies no wording of its own; every contract uses exactly what is written here. Save keeps your draft. Two banners sit at the top: whether the wording is published, and whether partners@legendoftoys.com is reachable (if it is not, contracts will fail to send until that clears).

Publishing

Publish needs both Dos and don’ts and Terms filled in. It asks you to confirm, because publishing turns on sending and the Shipping lock: from then on a video deal under the lock cannot reach Shipped or beyond until the creator accepts or someone records it by hand. Which deals come under the lock is explained in The Deal Contract.

ⓘ Later edits only affect new contracts

After publishing, saving a change applies to the next contract anyone opens. Contracts already sent keep the words they went out with.
