For a video deal, the Contract card on the deal page sends the creator a contract by email, records when they accept it, and collects their bank details and invoice. Until the contract is accepted, the deal cannot move to Shipped or beyond.

What it’s for
Sending and tracking the creator’s contract.
Read or act?
Act (manage access to send).
Who
Marketer Lead Admin
Applies to
Video deals, not UGC.
⚠ Off until the wording is published

This feature is off until an admin publishes the contract wording (see Contract Wording under Admin). Until then the card says the wording is not published, Send contract is greyed out, and nothing is locked.

Sending the contract

  1. Open the deal and press Send contract. The Contract card sits on the deal page. It shows the draft, written from the deal itself: the campaign, what we are sending, the deliverables, payment terms and amount (not on barter), the tracking link and code if there are any, then the published dos and don’ts and terms of the template that matches the deal type (see below). When to post is covered by the published terms. The post date is not printed, unless the deal is marked Fixed date (launch): then (video deals only; UGC deals do not use this contract) the contract also says Post date (fixed): <date>.
  2. Check and edit the text. To, Subject and Body can all be changed. If you type a different email address, tick Save this email on the influencer to keep it.
  3. Press Send. The email goes from partners@legendoftoys.com, and the creator’s replies land in that mailbox. The accept link is added automatically below your text.

The Send contract window has a line saying which template is used, for example “Template: Paid, picked from the deal type…”. Barter deals get Barter, paid deals get Paid, affiliate deals get Affiliate, and paid + affiliate deals get Paid with the commission line added from the deal’s commission %. The templates are edited in Contract Wording under Admin.

If something stops the send, the window lists it and Send stays disabled. The usual reasons: no valid email, the deal is not yet approved, no deliverables, a paid deal with no amount, or a Fixed date (launch) deal with no post date (“This deal has a fixed post date: add the date first.”). Two more blockers: an affiliate or paid + affiliate deal needs its commission % first, and every deal needs a deal type (“Set the deal type first, so the right contract template is used.”). A missing expected post date no longer stops the send.

What the Contract card shows

The card shows the status in words (Not sent, Sent waiting for the creator, Accepted by the creator, Accepted recorded by hand), who it went to, who sent it and when, and any failed send. On paid and affiliate deals it also shows whether bank details have arrived (“Received, ending 4321”) and the creator’s invoices, which open in a new tab. Nobody in Ignition sees a full account number or PAN, only the last four digits.

The buttons

  • Resend sends a fresh contract and makes the old link stop working. If the creator has already accepted, resending clears that acceptance: tick Resend anyway and give a reason, which goes in the History. They must accept again before the deal can ship.
  • Mark accepted by hand records an acceptance that happened elsewhere (say, on WhatsApp). A reason is required and goes in the History.
  • Allow bank details update (paid and affiliate deals, once bank details are in) lets the creator change them once through that deal’s accepted link, for 7 days. Without it, a received set of details cannot be changed, which stops a forwarded link from redirecting payment.

What the creator sees

The creator opens the link from the email on their phone, with no login.

  1. Read. They see the contract text.
  2. Tick and accept. They tick I have read and agree, then press I accept. We store the time they accepted.
  3. Bank details and invoice. On paid and affiliate deals only (not barter), the page then asks for bank details (account number twice, IFSC, PAN and so on, with errors shown beside each field) and an invoice upload (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB).

If they come back later, the page picks up where they stopped. A link that was superseded by a resend, or a deal that has closed, shows a plain “no longer valid” or “closed” message.

The Shipping lock

Once the wording is published, a deal under the lock cannot be moved to Shipped, Delivered, Scheduled, Posting or Live until the creator accepts or someone marks it accepted by hand. Because stages can be skipped, moving from Planning straight to Live is refused too. The refusal reads: “The creator has not accepted the contract yet. Send it from the Contract card, or record acceptance by hand, before moving this deal.”

The lock covers every new video deal, and deals that were in Proposed or Planning when it was switched on. Deals already past that point are exempt, and so are UGC deals.

ⓘ Reminders are not built

Ignition does not chase creators who have not accepted. Check the card, and resend or call them.
