Using it

Each row shows the influencer code, channel, type, reach, how many engagements they have, and their rating badge. Filter with the chips: All, Green, Yellow, Red, Unrated. The counts on the chips are exact on All. The search box filters the loaded roster; every word must match. Click a row to open the creator’s profile.

ⓘ Proven only

The Roster lists every creator with at least one deal at Shipped or later, so brand-new or earlier-stage-only creators will not appear here (find those under Influencers or B-List).
