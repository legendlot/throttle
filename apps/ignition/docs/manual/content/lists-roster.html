The Roster is the proven-influencer list: every creator who has completed at least one engagement. Open it to find vetted creators and check their quality rating before giving them new work.

What it’s for
Finding proven creators.

Who

Includes

Using it

Each row shows the influencer code, channel, type, reach, how many engagements they have, and their rating badge. Filter with the chips: All, Green, Yellow, Red, Unrated. Click a row to open the creator’s profile.

ⓘ Proven only

The Roster lists only creators with at least one completed engagement, so brand-new or in-progress-only creators will not appear here (find those under Influencers or B-List). The list shows up to 200 rows.
