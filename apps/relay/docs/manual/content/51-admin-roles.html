The permission builder. Defines what each role can do, for everyone in Relay.

What it’s for
Editing the roles themselves.
Read or act?
Action screen, with system-wide effect.
Who
Super-admin only.
Applies
Immediately, to everyone holding that role.

The permission keys

KeyAllows
relay_viewOpening Relay and reading screens. Without this, nothing.
segment_manageCreating and editing segments.
template_manageCreating and editing templates.
campaign_buildPreparing campaigns and journeys, but not releasing them.
send_activatePressing send, scheduling, and activating a journey.
approveApproving a campaign over the threshold.
data_consent_adminEditing consent and delivery blocks on a contact.
connector_channel_manageManaging sender identities and connectors.
relay_adminGranting access to others.
relay_super_adminThis screen, and Approval & Caps.
⛔ Important

Keep campaign_build and send_activate apart in any role meant for someone learning the system. Granting both is what turns “I was just trying it out” into a live send to twenty thousand people.

ⓘ Note

Roles marked system are read-only and cannot be edited or deleted. They are the safe baseline. Build a new role rather than trying to bend one of those.
