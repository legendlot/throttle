The screen you land on. It answers one question fast: is anything happening right now that needs me?

What it’s for
A live status board for the whole system.
Read or act?
Read-only. Everything on it is a link to somewhere else.
Who
Everyone with Relay access.
Updates
Refreshes itself while a campaign is sending.

The status strip

Test mode

Reads LOCKED or OPEN. Locked means nothing can reach a real customer. This is the single most important thing on the screen: check it before you assume a send went out.

Sending now

Campaigns currently fanning out, with progress. A broadcast to a large audience takes hours, so seeing something here for a while is normal.

Awaiting approval

Campaigns submitted by an Author and waiting for an Approver. If this is not empty, someone is blocked.

In queue / In flight

Messages accepted and on their way to the provider. A small number here at any time is healthy.

Revenue

Money attributed to Relay sends. See Analytics for how attribution is worked out.

Recent activity

A running list of campaign and journey lifecycle events: submitted, approved, started, finished. It is the quickest way to see what a colleague did this morning without asking them.

✓ Tip

If you are ever unsure whether a send actually went to customers, look at Test mode here first. “It said it sent but nobody got it” is almost always test mode being locked.
