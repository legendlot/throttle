The headline numbers

Delivery rate

Of what we sent, how much arrived.

Read rate

Of what arrived, how much was opened or read.

Our defects

Failures caused by us: a bad address, a variable that would not fill, a broken template. This is the number you can actually fix.

Throttled by Meta

Failures caused by WhatsApp rate limits rather than anything we did wrong. Kept separate on purpose so it is not read as a defect.

Skipped

Stopped by the send gate before going out, broken down by reason. These people received nothing.

Attributed revenue

Orders traced back to a send.

Marketing ROI

Attributed revenue against send cost. Transactional messages are excluded from this, because an order confirmation does not cause the order.

Sent, delivered, skipped, failed

These are four different things and mixing them up is the most common misreading on this screen.

StatusMeans
sentHanded to the provider.
deliveredConfirmed as arrived on the customer’s device.
skippedThe gate stopped it. Nothing was sent and nothing was charged.
failedWe tried and it did not work.
⚠ Watch out

A large skipped count is not a system fault. It usually means the audience was right but the gate did its job: no consent, cap already hit, or outside quiet hours. Read the breakdown by reason before treating it as a bug, and remember those people were not contacted at all.

ⓘ Note

Email open tracking depends on the customer’s mail app loading images, which many block, so email read rates are systematically understated. WhatsApp read receipts are reliable. Compare email to email and WhatsApp to WhatsApp, never one against the other.
