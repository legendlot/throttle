Exclusions let you say “send to this group, but not to these people”. They are the answer to the most common broadcast problem: the same customer getting two things from us in one day.

Where
On the campaign form, under the segment picker.
Three rules
All optional. Anyone matching any of them is skipped.
When checked
Continuously, while the campaign sends.
Who
Author Manager

The three rules

Don’t send to these segments

Tick any number of other segments. Anyone in them is skipped. Use it to hold back staff, existing buyers, or a group you messaged separately.

Already reached by these campaigns

Tick previous campaigns. Anyone who received one is skipped. This counts across every channel, because a campaign only has one channel: excluding a WhatsApp blast will correctly hold those people back from a follow-up email.

Recently contacted on this channel

Skip anyone we have messaged on this channel within a chosen window, from 6 hours up to 30 days. Unlike the rule above, this one is same channel only: an email this morning will not stop a WhatsApp tonight.

Why the timing matters

A large broadcast takes hours to work through its audience. Exclusions are re-checked continuously as it goes, not fixed at the moment you pressed send. So if a colleague sends something else while your campaign is still running, the people who received theirs are dropped from the rest of yours automatically.

Two campaigns running at the same time will also hold each other back, so you can start both without co-ordinating them by hand.

What counts as “contacted”

Only messages that genuinely went out. A message the send gate stopped, for example because the person had hit the frequency cap, does not count: they received nothing, so excluding them would be wrong. Journey messages do count, because from the customer’s side a journey message is just as real as a campaign one.

✓ Tip

Set the rule, then read the audience line. It updates as you tick, and tells you exactly how many people each rule removed before you commit to anything.

ⓘ Note

Exclusions lock when you submit the campaign, along with everything else. The confirmation dialog on Send now lists every rule in force, so a rule you set days ago and forgot cannot silently shrink your send.
