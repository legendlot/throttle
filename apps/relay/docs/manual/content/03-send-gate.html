Every message Relay sends is checked one last time immediately before it goes out. This chapter explains those checks in order, because they are the reason a send sometimes reaches fewer people than the screen predicted.

Applies to
Every message, every channel, every screen.
Runs
Per recipient, at the moment of sending.
Result if blocked
The message is logged as skipped, with a reason.
Set by
Super-admin on Approval & Caps.

The checks, in order

0. Test mode

A global lock. While it is on, nothing reaches anyone outside the internal test list, no matter what you do. It is the master safety switch.

1. Suppression

A hard block on an address or number, for example after a permanent email bounce or an SMS opt-out. This one outranks everything, including order confirmations.

2. Consent

Marketing needs an explicit opt-in on that channel. No opt-in, no marketing message. Order updates are not affected.

3. Frequency cap

A limit on how many marketing messages one person can receive in a rolling window. Currently three in twenty-four hours.

4. Quiet hours

No marketing during the quiet window. Set per channel, because the rules differ: promotional SMS in India is legally restricted to 10:00 to 21:00, WhatsApp is not, and email is exempt entirely.

5. Channel rules

Basic sanity: a valid email address, a plausible phone number, and for WhatsApp, an approved template if the 24 hour reply window has closed.

6. Send budget

A daily ceiling on marketing sends, used to warm up a new sending number gently. Checked last, so a slot is never wasted on a message another check would have stopped.

What bypasses what

PurposeConsent, cap, quiet hours, budgetSuppression
marketingAll applyApplies
service (a survey after the customer contacted us)Quiet hours apply, the rest do notApplies
transactional / utility (order confirmed, out for delivery)None applyApplies

The logic is simple: a customer who asked us to stop marketing at them still needs to know their parcel is arriving. But a customer whose email address hard-bounced cannot receive anything at all, because there is nowhere to send it.

Reading a skipped message

When the gate stops something, it is recorded rather than silently dropped, and the reason is shown in Analytics and on the contact. The common ones:

ReasonWhat happened
no_consentNever opted in to marketing on that channel.
freq_capAlready had the maximum number of marketing messages today.
quiet_hoursIt was outside the sending window for that channel.
suppressedHard block on the address or number.
budget_exhaustedThe daily marketing send ceiling was reached.
test_mode_blockedTest mode is on and this person is not on the test list.
⚠ Watch out

A skipped message means the person got nothing. It is not a delivery failure and it is not a retry queue. If a campaign reports a lot of budget_exhausted, those people were never contacted and will not be contacted later unless you send again.

ⓘ Note

The audience number on the campaign screen accounts for suppression, consent and your own exclusion rules. It cannot account for the frequency cap or quiet hours, because those depend on the exact moment each message is sent. Expect the delivered count to be a little lower than the predicted audience.
