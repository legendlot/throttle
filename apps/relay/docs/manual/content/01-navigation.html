Signing in

  1. Go to relay.legendoftoys.com. You will land on a sign-in screen.
  2. Sign in with Google. Only @legendoftoys.com accounts are accepted. A personal Gmail will be refused.
  3. If you see “Relay access required”. Your account is valid but has not been given a Relay role yet. Ask an admin to add you on the Users screen.

The sidebar

Overview

The landing screen. What is sending right now, what is waiting for approval, and recent activity.

Send

Campaigns, the Experiment log, and Journeys. Everything that puts a message in front of a customer.

Audience

Activity, Segments and Contacts. Who we know and what they have done.

Build & measure

Templates, Library, Links and Analytics. The parts a send is made from, and the results afterwards.

Admin

Users, Roles, Approval & Caps, Sender Identities and Connectors. Most people will not see this group at all.

Two shortcuts

⌘ K opens the command palette. Start typing a screen name or a campaign name and press Enter. It is the fastest way around the app.

Esc closes any open dialog without saving.

The bar at the top

The top bar shows where you are (group, then screen) and carries the waffle menu on the right, which jumps to the other Legend of Toys systems: Garage, Redline, Depot, Snorkel, Ignition, Docket, Pitstop, Odo and Throttle. Relay itself is pinned at the top of that menu as a reminder of where you already are.

✓ Tip

If a screen looks stale, refresh the page properly with ⌘ Shift R. A normal refresh can serve you an older cached copy of the app.
