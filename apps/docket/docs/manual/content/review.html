The Weekly Review is one page that tells a team how its week went and what is coming: the score, what slipped and why, milestones due, who has gone quiet, and the next two weeks. It is built fresh from the live tasks, so a Thursday glance and a Friday meeting read from the same source. A second page, Founders’ catch-up, is for the Founders’ own weekly conversation.

What it’s for
Reviewing the week and the next two.

The page

The title reads Review · week ending a date, with a PROVISIONAL or FINAL tag and an As of… time in Indian time.

Week stepper

Left and right arrows step a week at a time; This week jumps back. A week that has not started yet says Not started yet, and a week that was never opened says This week has no score.

Team picker

Founders and Associates choose All teams or one team. Everyone else sees their own team only. If your profile has no team you see No team on your profile; ask an admin.

Copy for Slack

Copies a ready-to-paste text summary of what is on screen. It never names a task from the Founders’ space or a founders-only milestone, whoever copies it. It is greyed out until the week has a score.

Hold Founder

Stores the provisional view as a snapshot, so the minutes of a meeting can point at exactly what was on screen (Held). Snapshots lists the stored versions for the week; open one to read it as it was, and press Back to live to return. The final score is stored automatically when the week closes.

What is in the pack

For each team there is one card with these sections, in this order:

SectionWhat it shows
This weekCommitted, on time, missed so far (or missed), still due, and the on-time percentage, marked provisional or final. See The On-time Score.
Last weekThe same for the week before, plus a small chart of the last four weeks.
SlippedEach task that missed: its owner, the committed date against the current date, how many times it moved, and every revision with its reason and when.
MilestonesMilestones due this week and whether each is done; any re-dated this week, with the reason; and warnings: no actionable tasks, no data source, not agreed.
Hand-offsHand-offs that are Overdue or Unowned (no one named, or not accepted, in the next two weeks). See Hand-offs.
StalenessFor each person with open committed tasks, how recently they updated things and the share they touched. This week only, a banner reads Quiet on…, no update on any open task if the last working day was silent. Only Founders see when a colleague was last in Docket.
Next two weeksThe milestones due, then the tasks due, grouped by person.
UnplannedHow many committed tasks have no plan link, for example 3 of 20 committed tasks have no plan link (15%). See Linking Tasks to the Plan.

Founders also get a Proposals card at the bottom (how many are waiting, and what was submitted this week), and, above the pack, a Score trend chart: on time, week by week, for the last closed weeks. It starts to draw once two weeks have closed (The trend starts after two closed weeks).

ⓘ Cut to the reader, always

The pack is built for whoever opens it. A team lead sees their own team and only the tasks they can open. Founders see every team with every task counted, and tasks in spaces they cannot open appear as +N in spaces you’re not in. A held snapshot is cut to the reader again when it is opened.

Founders’ catch-up Founder

Open Founders’ catch-up in the sidebar (or the button at the top of the Review). Anyone else who opens it sees Founders only. It has a week stepper, a Review button back, and its own Copy for Slack. It holds three things:

Cross-team asks

Open top-level tasks due this week or next that are waiting on a task owned by another team. Each ask shows the waiting task, waiting on, and the task it waits for, with the team, owner and date of each. This is where blocked cross-team work gets noticed.

Hand-offs between teams

Open hand-offs due this week or next that no one has accepted or been named for.

Top 5

Each Founder’s five most important things for the week, in rank order. Your own card is editable: each slot can hold a Plan item or a Task (use the buttons) and a note, or just a free-text note. Press Save; empty slots are left out. The other Founder’s card is read-only. Once a week is over its top 5 is a record and cannot be changed.
