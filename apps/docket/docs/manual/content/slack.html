Docket has its own Slack app. From Slack you can add a task in seconds, see what is on your plate, tick a task done, move its date, comment, accept a hand-off, and propose something for the plan, without opening Docket. Docket also sends a few well-timed direct messages to keep the week on track. Everything in Slack runs as you, with your own Docket permissions.

What it’s for
Using Docket from inside Slack.
Read or act?
Both: add and update tasks, read your list.
Who
Member Founder Associate
Where
Any Slack channel or DM: type /docket

How Docket knows it is you

Docket matches your Slack account to your Docket login by your work email. There is nothing to set up. If your email does not match, Docket replies that your Slack account is not linked and asks you to have an admin check it. A Slack account that matches no active Legend of Toys employee is refused. Because it acts as you, you can only see and change what you could in Docket itself, and the same rules apply (a deadline can be set once, then moved only with a reason).

The /docket command

Type /docket in any message box. Replies are visible only to you. /docket help shows the list below.

You typeWhat happens
/docket add <task>Adds a task, using Docket’s own quick add: @name sets the owner, !p0 to !p3 the priority, and a date or time the deadline (fri 5pm, tomorrow, 12 nov). Without @name the task is yours. Docket replies with a link to the new task.
/docket mineLists your open tasks: up to 8 overdue, 8 due today and 5 in the next 7 days, each with buttons (below). A link at the end opens the full list in Docket.
/docket propose <title> | <why>Sends a proposal to the Founders. Put the title, a vertical bar, then why it matters. If you leave out the bar, the same text is used for both.
/docket helpShows this list. Typing /docket on its own does the same.

Turning a message into a task

Two shortcuts open a small form:

Add to Docket

On any Slack message, open its … menu and choose Add to Docket. The message text becomes the task title, and the link to the message is saved on the task.

New Docket task

From the shortcuts menu anywhere in Slack, choose New Docket task for a blank form.

The form (titled Add to Docket) has:

  • Task (up to 200 characters) and Owner (defaults to you).
  • Deadline, optional, with the hint Set once, moving it later needs a reason. It must be today or later, and after 6 pm Indian time today is no longer offered.
  • Plan link, optional, if there is anything on the plan you may link to.
  • Hand off to, optional: a person on another team, with the hint Someone on another team; you stay the owner.

Press Add task. Docket confirms with a link.

Buttons on your tasks

Each task in /docket mine, and in the reminders below, carries buttons that run as you:

ButtonWhat it does
DoneMarks the task done. If the task is still waiting on open tasks, Docket refuses and tells you to mark it done in Docket, where you can confirm.
Move dateOpens a form with a New date and a required reason (Why is it moving?). The move is recorded with the reason, like a Revise in Docket.
CommentOpens a form to write a comment on the task.
Accept hand-offAppears on a hand-off addressed to you or your team. Accepts it, exactly as the Incoming list in Docket does.

If Docket cannot do something, it says so in plain words and points you to the app. For example, an abandoned task is read-only until it is reopened in Docket.

Reminder messages

Docket sends a handful of direct messages from the Docket app, in Indian time, on working days only (Docket’s working days and holidays, see Weeks and Week Ending). Each message is built for its recipient and shows only what they may see. A reminder with nothing to say is not sent.

WhenWho gets itWhat it says
09:00, every working dayEveryone who owns open tasks.Good morning, your Docket tasks: what is overdue, due today, and anything with no update for 3 working days. Each task has its buttons.
09:30, every working dayFounders and Associates.The Chase list: open tasks due in the next 2 working days with no update in 3, and any team that raised a quiet day. Founders get a one-line version.
Thursday 17:00Anyone who committed to a task this week that is still not done.Review tomorrow: the tasks you committed to this week that are not done yet, with a link to the review.
Thursday 18:00Founders and Associates.The review pack is ready: a summary, with a link to the full pack.
Friday 18:00Founders.Tomorrow’s Founders’ catch-up: the summary, with a link to the page.

A reminder is never sent twice for the same day, and tasks in the Founders’ space never appear in Slack.
