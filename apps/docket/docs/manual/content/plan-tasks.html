A task becomes part of the plan by being linked to a theme, milestone or result. The link is just one more field on the task, but it is what lets the weekly review say how much of the week’s work was planned. Linking is optional; a task with no link is called Unplanned.

What it’s for
Tying a task to a goal.
Read or act?
Act: set or clear the Plan field.
Who
Member Reviewer Admin
Applies to
Top-level tasks only.

Where to set it

PlaceHow
Inside a taskThe Plan property. Click it, search, and pick. Unplanned at the top clears the link.
New task formA Plan field that starts as Unplanned; type to search for a theme, milestone or result.
Quick captureA Plan chip beside Owner, Deadline and Priority on the capture line. It only appears when there is something for you to link to.
The listThe Plan column (turn it on in Columns). Click a cell and pick; an empty cell offers Set plan.
The BoardCards show the plan chip. Set the link from the card’s task view.

The picker lists what you may link to, each as a chain such as Grow online sales › M1 › M1.R1 · Product pages are live, and it searches codes, theme names and words in titles. You can link to a proposed or agreed item; closed ones are not offered.

What is shown on the task

The chip shows the item’s code; hover for its title. A chip reading Another owner’s milestone means the link goes to a milestone you cannot see (hover shows its theme only). A task with no link shows nothing in the list and Unplanned in the task view.

⚠ Top-level tasks only: sub-tasks inherit

A sub-task cannot carry its own link. It shows its parent’s link in grey, labelled from the parent task; to change it, change the parent. A sub-task of an unlinked parent reads Follows the parent task.

Filtering by plan

Open Filter on the Tasks list and use the Plan picker (default Any plan):

  • Pick a theme to see every task under it, including its milestones and results.
  • Pick a milestone to see tasks linked to it or to its results.
  • Pick a result to see only its own tasks.
  • Pick Unplanned to see the tasks with no link at all, the list to work through before a review.

Links in the Plan, the Tree and the review (+N more) open the Tasks list already filtered this way.

Who may link what

You can edit the Plan field on a task you may edit, and link it to an item you can see. Founders can link to anything. A task linked to a founders-only item must sit in the Founders’ space, so Docket will not let anyone else link one. Moving a task out of the Founders’ space is a Founders-only action.

ⓘ Plan and Programs are separate

A task can carry a plan link, a program, both, or neither. The plan link says which goal the work serves; a program is a free label for grouping related work, such as a one-off event, that may not sit on any milestone. Neither replaces the other, and a task with no plan link is perfectly normal.
