A record checklist is a copy of a checklist for one product or launch, ticked once instead of on a schedule. You start it from a record template (for example a launch checklist), give it a title like “Launch: Badger”, and the team works through the items together. When every item is done or marked N/A, the record finishes itself.

One-off, multi-team checklists per launch.

Routines vs records

A routine (see Checklists) repeats on a schedule and is ticked day after day. A record is a single run: it has no schedule and nobody is “assigned” it. It belongs to a space, and like a task, who can see it follows that space’s privacy. A record in a private space is invisible to people outside it.

Making a record template

On Manage, open New template. The editor starts with a Kind switch: Routine or Record. Choose Record to hide the schedule, chart type and assignees, then add sections and items as usual (an item can carry short help text). A record template shows a Record · v1 tag in the list.

  • The kind is fixed once the template is saved. You can’t turn a routine into a record template or back.
  • Editing the items later bumps the version (v2, v3 …). Records you already started are offered the new items (see Pulling template updates).
  • Archiving a record template stops new records starting from it; existing records keep their items.

The Records tab

The Records tab lists every record you can see, each with its title, product, template, owner, space, status and a progress bar. Click a title to open the record. Four filters sit above the list: template, product, status (Open + complete by default; also Open, Complete, Archived, All) and owner.

Starting a record

  1. Click New record. The button is greyed out until at least one record template exists.
  2. Type a title, such as Launch: Badger, and pick the Template.
  3. Optionally pick a Product. Launches usually begin before the product is in the catalog, so this can stay empty; set it later when the product exists.
  4. Choose the Space and Owner. The space defaults to General and the owner to you. Remember that the space decides who can see the record.
  5. Click Create. The items are copied from the template and you land on the record.

The record page

Header

The title, a status (Open, Complete or Archived), and below it the Product, Owner, Space, Template (with the version the copy was made from) and Progress.

Linked tasks

The tasks tied to this record, each with status, DKT- number, title and owner. Click one to open it; the x unlinks it (shown only if you can edit that task). Tasks in spaces you can’t open appear only as a count.

Sections and items

Each section shows its own progress. Each item has a tick box, its title and help text, who ticked it and when, a Note box, and its buttons.

History

A History list at the foot records every change: ticks, N/A, notes, linked tasks, template updates, renames and archiving, each with who and when.

Ticking, N/A and progress

Anyone who can see the record can tick it. A launch crosses teams: content ticks content, operations ticks sourcing. Every tick shows who did it and when. Click the box to tick; click again to un-tick (the item reopens).

If an item does not apply, press N/A and give a short reason (for example “No new tooling needed”). The reason is required and must be under 300 characters. An N/A item counts as complete; Undo N/A brings it back to open.

Progress counts done plus N/A against all items and reads like 6/11 (1 N/A): six of eleven items finished, one of those marked N/A. The same figure appears in the list, on each section and in the header.

ⓘ The record completes itself

When the last open item is ticked or marked N/A, the record becomes Complete and Docket says so. Re-open any item and the record goes back to Open. Nobody presses a “finish” button.

You can also use Add item at the foot to add an item only this record needs, choosing which section it joins. It does not change the template.

Pulling template updates

When the template gains items after a record was started, an open record shows a banner: Template has 2 new items — add them? Nothing already ticked changes. Press Add them to copy only the missing items.

  • It never removes or un-ticks anything.
  • An item deleted from the template stays on records already made, labelled No longer in template.
  • Complete and archived records are not offered the update.

Creating a task from an item

An open item without a task has a Create task button. It opens a small line prefilled with the item and the record title (for example “Order sample stock — Launch: Badger”); edit it if you like, then press Enter or Create task.

  • The task is made in the record’s space, with you as the owner (change the owner on the task afterwards).
  • It is linked to the record automatically, and the item now shows the task’s DKT- number, owner and status. An item holds one task.
  • Only an open item can become a task.
✓ “DKT-123 is done — tick this?”

When a task made from an item is finished and the item is still open, the item offers a one-click DKT-123 is done — tick this? button. Docket never ticks for you: finishing the task does not change the item until someone presses it, because the person ticking is vouching for the result.

Linking an existing task

You can tie any task to a record from the task itself (see Inside a Task). The rules:

  • You need edit rights on the task.
  • The record must be in the task’s space, or in General. Anything else is refused: A checklist can be linked only to a task in its own space, or from General.
  • A recurring (routine) task can’t take a record checklist, and an abandoned task must be reactivated before linking (unlinking still works).
  • A task linked to a record can’t be moved to a space that would break the rule. Docket refuses with This task is tied to a record checklist in another space. Unlink it (or move the checklist’s work) first. Unlink, then move.

Archiving

An owner, creator or admin can Archive a record (a confirm box appears). An archived record stays readable but read-only: ticking, notes and renaming stop, and trying gives This record is archived. Un-archive it to change it. Un-archive restores it. Archived records show only under the Archived or All status filter.

Finding a record

Press ⌘K and type at least two letters of a record’s title or product: matches appear under Checklists with their progress, and Enter opens the record. You only find records in spaces you can open.
