A Space is a walled-off area for tasks. Only the people it is shared with can see what is inside; being an admin does not get you in. Every task lives in exactly one space, and there is no company-wide “open” space: work sits in your team’s space, your own Personal space, or a private space someone shared with you.

What it is
A hard visibility boundary around tasks.
Affects access?
Yes, this is its whole point.
Who can make one
Anyone; you become its owner.
Per task
Always exactly one space.

The kinds of space

Team space

One for each team (for example a team called ZZ Ops). It is owned by the team’s head and shared with the whole team, so everyone directly in the team can see and work in it.

Personal space

One per person, created for you the first time you open Docket. Only you can see it. It is where work lands when it has no team space to go to.

Founders’ space

Sealed. It holds tasks linked to founders-only plan items, and only Founders can be in it.

Private space

Any space someone creates and shares, with named people or with whole teams.

Where your work lands

  • Opening Tasks takes you to your default space: your team’s space if you can add work there, otherwise your Personal space.
  • A new task with no space chosen goes to your team’s space, otherwise your Personal space.
  • Filing work for another team whose space you cannot open is refused. Hand the task off instead (see Hand-offs).
  • Plan templates have a Put the tasks in picker; it starts on your team’s space, else your Personal space.

Who sees what

You are…You see
In a space (owner, member, or on a team it is shared with)Every task in it. Inside a space visibility is flat: there is no sub-filtering.
On a task (owner, assignee, collaborator, or someone it was handed to)That task, wherever it lives, except in the Founders’ space.
A plan adminEvery team space, never a Personal space.
A Reviewer or AdminEvery team space, to read only. You cannot add or change work there.
⛔ A private space beats even Admin

Being an Admin or a Reviewer does not get you into someone’s Personal or private space. If you are not in it, the space and its tasks are invisible to you: they do not appear in lists, search, the dashboard, or anywhere else. The only sanctioned way an admin can enter is the audited break-glass recovery of an orphaned private space (covered in the admin chapter), and that act is logged.

Creating and using a space

  1. Create it with + New then New space in the top bar, or the + beside Spaces in the sidebar. Give it a name and create it. You become its owner and first member.
  2. It appears in your sidebar under My spaces (spaces you own, your Personal space first); a space someone else added you to, or shared with your team, appears under By others. Click it to see only that space’s tasks; anything you capture while inside it lands there.
  3. Add people or teams from the space’s settings (the settings button at the right of the views bar, shown to the space’s owner). Under People add individuals who have a Docket login; under Shared with teams pick Share with a team….
  4. Move existing tasks in using the Space property in the task view, or Move in the bulk bar.
ⓘ Sharing with a team reaches its direct members only

Everyone directly in a shared team can see and work in the space. Sub-teams are not included, so share with each sub-team separately. To stop, use the bin beside the team (Stop sharing with this team).

Owning a space

The space’s owner (and only the owner) can, from the space settings:

Rename

Change the space’s name.

Shared with teams

Share the space with whole teams, or stop sharing.

People

Add or remove individuals. Only employees with a login can be added. A person who is also in through a team is marked also via that team.

Transfer ownership

Hand the space to another member, for example before leaving the team.

Archive

Retire the space. It leaves the sidebar, but its tasks are kept.

Within a space, a task can be edited by its owner, its creator, or the space owner, so the person running the space can keep its tasks tidy.

ⓘ Each space has its own dashboard

From the views bar of a space, the dashboard icon opens a dashboard scoped to just that space, the same tiles and breakdowns as the org dashboard, but only counting that space’s tasks. Any member can view it, and it is the place the cards and person rows drill down into task lists. See The Dashboard.

⚠ If a space’s owner leaves

If a private space is orphaned (its owner is gone and nobody else can manage it), an admin can step in through the break-glass recovery on the admin Spaces screen, which makes them the owner and is recorded. The everyday answer, though, is to transfer ownership before the owner leaves.
