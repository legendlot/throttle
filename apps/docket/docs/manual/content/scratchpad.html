⛔ Nobody else can read your Scratchpad, not even an admin

Scratchpad notes are tied to you and to you alone. There is no admin view, no sharing, no recovery path into them. This is deliberate, so it is a safe place for rough drafts and half-thoughts. The flip side: there is no undo, a deleted note is gone for good.

The layout

On a computer the Scratchpad has up to three columns:

Notes column (left)

All your notes, with a count. The + beside the heading creates a new note; click any note to open it. Each note shows its title, or its first line if you haven’t titled it. A reminder at the bottom says the notes are private to you.

Editor (middle)

A Title box and a large free-text area for the open note. Beside the title are two buttons: a calculator button that shows or hides the calculator, and a bin to delete the note.

Calculator (right)

Shown while a note is open. Hide it with the calculator button if you want the full width for writing; Docket remembers your choice.

On a phone the notes column and the editor take turns. Tap a note to open it, and tap ‹ Notes at the top left of the editor to go back to the list.

Writing notes

Type anything in the note area, plain free text. Your changes save automatically a moment after you stop typing (and when you switch notes or leave the page), there is no save button. To title a note, fill in the title box at the top; otherwise its first line stands in. To remove a note, use the bin icon next to the title (it asks you to confirm, because there is no undo).

The calculator

Beside an open note sits a real calculator for quick sums while you think, no need to leave Docket or open another app. Type a sum into its display, or tap the on-screen keypad, and the result shows live underneath as you go. It understands the everyday things, plus, minus, times, divide, brackets and percentages:

You typeYou get
120 * 4 + 50530
1200 * 18%216  (18% of 1200)
(90 + 60) / 350

A trailing percentage like 18% is treated as “eighteen hundredths”, so 1200 * 18% gives the tax on 1200. Press Enter (or the = key) to collapse the sum down to its result, so you can carry on from the answer. C clears the display.

✓ Keyboard flow

While writing a note, press Tab to jump straight into the calculator, and Shift+Tab to jump back to your note. You can do a quick sum and return to typing without touching the mouse.

ⓘ What the Scratchpad is not

Notes are not tasks. Nothing here shows up on the board, the dashboard or anyone's list, and the calculator works one sum at a time (it doesn't remember running totals between lines). If a note turns into real work, capture it as a task on the Tasks list so it has an owner and a deadline.
