Besides the register itself, the Plan has four more screens, each answering a different question: Three weeks (what is due soon, and what needs a hand), Tree (how each goal is progressing), Templates (reusable plans) and Re-plan (is next month ready). They sit in the sidebar under Plan.

What it’s for
Looking at the plan by time, by goal, and by month.
Read or act?
Mostly read; Templates act.
Who
Member Founder Associate
Always cut to you

Three weeks

A rolling window of Overdue, This week, Next week and Week after. Each cell lists the milestones and results due in that week (a target icon, code, title and date) and the tasks due that week (a status dot, the title, the owner and date). Click a task to open it.

Rows

The Team | Person | Theme switch chooses what the rows are.

Team

Pick All teams or one team. Founders start on all teams; everyone else starts on their own team.

Warnings

Above the grid, four kinds of warning, each saying who needs to act (For Priya, For the founders). When there are none you see No warnings.

WarningWhat it means
No actionable tasksAn agreed item is due in the window and has no open task with both an owner and a date.
Not agreed yetA proposed milestone is due in the window.
Linked to a theme onlyAn open task is linked to a theme that now has agreed milestones, so it should be pointed at one. If you can edit the task, a Link to a milestone button fixes it in one click.
StaleNo update or comment for 3 or more working days.

Only top-level tasks appear. Founders also see +N in spaces you’re not in where open tasks sit in spaces they cannot open. With nothing due you see Nothing due in the next three weeks.

Tree

The whole plan as a tree: theme, milestone, result, task. Click a row to fold it open or shut. Under each plan item Docket shows counts for everything beneath it (12 open, 5 done, 2 overdue) and an on time % that compares finished tasks against their first deadline (recurring checklist work is left out).

  • Theme picks one theme or All themes; Mine only shows the items you own and the path down to them; Show closed adds done and dropped items; Expand all and Collapse all do what they say.
  • Tasks hang directly under the item they are linked to, up to 25; a +N more link opens the Tasks list filtered to that item. Click a task to open it.
  • If you cannot see an item’s parent, it hangs under the nearest one you can see. Founders also see +N hidden where tasks sit in spaces they cannot open.

Templates

A template is a reusable plan: a list of steps (milestones, results and tasks), each with a Role (such as “Lead”) and a Day offset from a start date. A launch, a hiring round or a monthly close are typical. Applying one creates everything in one go.

The list

Each template has Apply and, if it is yours, Edit (otherwise Preview). Show archived includes retired ones; New template starts one. With none, the screen says No templates yet.

Two kinds

Founders: under a theme, made by Founders, which create milestones, results and tasks under a theme; and Leads: under a milestone, which anyone who owns an agreed milestone or result can make, and which is applied under a milestone you own.

Editing

Name, optional description, who it is for, then the Steps. Each step has a title, a Role and a Day (0 is the start date, a negative number is before it; the screen spells it out, such as 3 days before). Add a milestone, result or task step where it belongs, reorder with the arrows, and press Save. Archive retires it.

Applying

Pick the theme or milestone to apply it under, the Anchor date (day 0), and who plays each role. A preview titled What will be created lists every item with its date and person. Press Apply; the result reads Applied “name” with how many plan items and tasks were created.

ⓘ Applying follows the normal rules

Created items go through the same checks as ones made by hand: spaces still decide who can have a task, and a task linked to a founders-only item is created in the Founders’ space.

Re-plan Founder

The monthly check that next month is ready to start. The title reads Re-plan · Month Year and defaults to next month; use the arrows or Next month to move.

  • Readiness: one row per milestone due that month, with ticks for Agreed, Owner, Data source and Tasks (first 2 wks) (how many actionable tasks are lined up for the first fortnight), and an overall Ready mark. The header counts how many are ready and not ready. Filter by Team; Not ready first floats the gaps to the top.
  • Risks: the risks people have proposed since the last re-plan.
  • Scores: the on-time percentage for each team for each week of the month just closed.

The screen is read-only. Getting a row ready means doing the work elsewhere (agree the milestone, give it an owner, add tasks) and coming back.
