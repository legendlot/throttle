Docket thinks in weeks as well as days. Every task with a deadline belongs to a “week ending” date, and the list, the grid and the board can group and filter by it. This is the same week the on-time score and the weekly review use, so what you see in the list matches what gets reviewed.

What a week is

A week runs from midnight to midnight in Indian time and is named by its last day, the week ending date. A task belongs to the week that contains its effective deadline: the revised deadline if it has been revised, otherwise the original one.

Three company-wide settings define the calendar:

SettingStarts asUsed for
Week ending daySundayWhere one week stops and the next begins.
Working daysMonday to SaturdayCounting “3 working days” for stale warnings and for the review’s quiet-day check.
HolidaysNoneDays that do not count as working days.

The founders decide these for the whole company; there is no screen for changing them yet, so ask the Docket owner. A later change never rewrites weeks that have already been scored.

Group by Week ending

Open the Group button on the toolbar and choose Week ending. The list reflows into groups:

  • Overdue first, then one group per Week ending date in order, and No date last.
  • The Board offers the same choice and turns each of those groups into a column.

The Week filter

In the Filter panel, Week narrows the list to one week. It works on the List, the Grid and the Board, and on My tasks.

ChoiceShows
Any weekNo week filter (the default).
This weekTasks due in the current week, including ones in it that are already past their time.
Next weekTasks due in the following week.
Week ending…Pick any day; Docket snaps it to its week and shows the resulting week ending date beside the picker.
OverdueThe same as the existing Overdue filter: open tasks whose deadline has passed.
No dateTasks with no deadline yet.
⚠ The Board’s week columns are read-only

With the Board grouped by Week ending you cannot drag a card from one week to another, and the columns have no add line. A deadline never moves by a drag. To move a deadline, open the task and Revise it with a reason (see Inside a Task). The first deadline is set freely; every later change is recorded with its reason, which is what keeps the weekly score honest.

✓ A weekly habit

Before a review, filter to This week, then Next week, and set an owner and a deadline on anything missing. Then filter the Plan to Unplanned and link what should be linked.
