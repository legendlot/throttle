Sometimes your task needs someone on another team to take it forward: design needs to produce the artwork, finance needs to approve the payment. A hand-off sends the task to that team (and optionally to a named person) and makes sure someone says “I have it”. Until they do, the task is flagged, and the weekly review notices.

What it’s for
Passing a task to another team, with an accept.
Read or act?
Act: give, and accept.
Who
Member Reviewer Admin
Applies to
Top-level tasks only.

Giving a task to another team

  1. Open the task and find Hand-off to in the properties (you need edit rights, and it must be a top-level task, not a sub-task).
  2. Pick the team. Any team except the task’s own; the first choice, Not a hand-off, removes it again.
  3. Optionally name a person. A second picker appears beside the team, reading Anyone in <team>. Choose a person in that team, or leave it open for the team to sort out.

A status line then reads Waiting for Design to accept (or Design/Asha if you named someone). A small chip such as → Design appears on the task’s row, grid card and board card, in amber until it is accepted.

ⓘ The task stays yours

You remain the owner and the deadline is the hand-off date. The receiving team does not take the task over; it confirms it has picked it up. Changing the team or the named person clears an acceptance, so the new receiver has to accept again.

Incoming: what has been handed to you

On My tasks an Incoming section appears above your groups whenever hand-offs are addressed to you or your team. It stays hidden when there are none. A number beside the heading counts the ones not yet accepted.

  • Two tabs split them: To you (you are the named person) and To your team (everyone else in the receiving team).
  • Each row shows the status dot, task number and title, from the giver and their team, who it is to, and the due date. Click it to open the task.
  • The right-hand end shows Accept if you may accept, accepted once someone has, or waiting if it is not yours to accept.

If a task is handed to you, you can see it wherever it lives, even if you would not normally see that team’s space. The one exception is the Founders’ space, which stays sealed.

Accepting

The named person, or anyone in the receiving team when no one is named, can accept; the giver cannot accept their own hand-off. Accept from the Incoming row, or press Accept hand-off beside Hand-off to inside the task.

What changes when you accept

  • The status line changes to Accepted by … with the date, and the row chip loses its amber colour.
  • If no person was named, you are recorded as the receiver.
  • The task leaves the Unowned hand-off lists in the weekly review and the Founders’ catch-up.
  • History records Hand-off accepted. The owner and the deadline do not change.
⚠ Only an open task can be accepted

Docket says This task is closed if it is already done or abandoned, and This task isn’t a hand-off any more if the giver has cleared it in the meantime.
