Announcements is the company noticeboard inside Docket: news from the founders to everyone, or from a team head to a team. You read it from the first row of the sidebar, and a number beside the row tells you how many live posts you have not yet marked seen.

Reading announcements

Click Announcements at the top of the sidebar. Each post is a card with its title, text, author, how long ago it was posted, and who it is for (an Everyone tag, or one tag per team).

  • Mark seen. Press Mark seen once you have read a post. The button turns into Seen and the sidebar number drops by one.
  • Pinned posts carry a pin and stay at the top of the list.
  • Expiry. A post can have an end date. It stays live until the end of that day (India time), then leaves your list. Switch on Show past to see expired and older posts.

Who sees a post

A post for Everyone reaches every Docket user. A post for a team reaches that team and every sub-team under it. You read a team’s posts if you are in that team, in a team above it, or if you head that team.

Posting

If you can post, a New announcement button appears at the top of the page.

You are…You can post to
FounderEveryone, or any teams.
A team headThe teams you head, and their sub-teams.
Anyone elseNothing. You read only, and the page says “Founders and team heads post news here.”
  1. Write it. Give it a Title and the Announcement text (plain text; line breaks are kept; a counter shows the limit).
  2. Choose who sees it. Pick Everyone (founders only) or one or more team chips, for example ZZ Ops or Design team. A head with a single team has it chosen already.
  3. Optional: pin and expiry. Turn on Pin to the top, and set an Expires date if the news goes stale. The cross beside the date clears it.
  4. Press Post.

Whoever manages a post (its author, and the founders) can Edit (title, text, pin, expiry), Unpin or pin, and Archive it. The audience cannot be changed once posted; archive it and post again instead. Archiving takes the post out of everyone’s list, but Slack messages already sent stay in Slack. Edits are not sent to Slack again.

Who has seen it

On a post you manage, a Seen by 12 of 20 button opens a list: Not seen yet first (with each person’s team), then Seen with when. The author and the founders can open it. A line under the post also shows how the Slack mirror went for it.

The Slack mirror

When an admin has switched the mirror on, a post for Everyone goes to the company’s Slack channel, and a team post goes to that team’s own channel. A team with no channel set simply gets no Slack message; the post is still in Docket. The text is sent as plain text, so a post can never ping the whole channel.

✓ Use Mark seen as your inbox

The sidebar number only counts live posts you have not marked seen. Clear it each morning and anything new stands out.

Setting up Slack (admins)

Admin Open Admin then Announcements (you need the admin permission). Press Save after changes.

Mode

Off: nothing goes to Slack. Test: no channel gets anything; each test recipient gets a direct message showing the post and which channel it would go to (or that live mode would skip it because no channel is set). Live: every new post goes to the real channels. Docket asks you to confirm before Live is saved.

Org channel

The channel for posts to Everyone. Enter the Slack channel ID (it looks like C0123ABCD, not the channel name).

Team channels

One channel ID per team. Leave it empty for a team that should get no Slack post.

Test recipients

The people who get the test-mode messages. With nobody here, test mode sends nothing.

⚠ Invite @Docket to each channel

The Docket bot can only post in a channel it belongs to. In Slack, type /invite @Docket in the org channel and in each team channel. If it was not invited, the post is saved in Docket, the Slack message is not sent, and the post records that the bot was not in the channel.

ⓘ Start in Test

The mirror starts in Test mode. Add yourself as a test recipient, post once, and check the direct message before switching to Live.
