Docket gives each person the access that matches their job. Unlike some systems, having no special role in Docket is not the same as having no access, every signed-in LOT person is a full Member. This chapter explains the roles used throughout this manual and what each unlocks: three for the task manager, and two more for the company Plan.

The three task-manager roles

Member

Everyone, by default. Any signed-in LOT person is a Member without an admin doing anything. A Member creates tasks, and sees their own tasks, anything they are a collaborator on, and every task in their own department. They can fully edit the tasks they own or created (and add comments, documents and sub-tasks to any task they can see).

Reviewer

The watcher. A Reviewer sees every task across the whole company, plus the review Dashboard, but read-only. They can look at anything but do not change other people's tasks. Useful for managers and leads who need the full picture.

Admin

Full control. An Admin can see and edit any task, decide who has which role, share the Dashboard, and use the admin screens. Admin includes everything a Reviewer can do.

Two more roles, for the Plan

The Plan (themes, milestones, targets and the weekly review) has its own, narrower access. Two further roles exist for it. Both are given only by a Founder, and most people have neither.

Founder

The super admin. A Founder sees the whole Plan, including each item’s target and the founders-only themes; agrees, re-dates, splits, drops and finishes milestones; decides proposals; sets the Founders’ top 5; and uses the Re-plan and Founders’ catch-up screens. A Founder is also an Admin, and can see every team’s review and score. Only a Founder can give this role to anyone, change it, or remove it.

Associate

The Founders’ Associate. Reads every plan item that is not founders-only, in full except the targets, and the weekly review for any team. It is read-only: an Associate does not change the Plan, and does not see the Founders’ catch-up or re-plan. It is a separate role from Reviewer and Admin and does not include either. Also given only by a Founder.

ⓘ How access actually works

Your access comes from a role an admin assigns you on the Users page. Most people have no role, which is the Member baseline above. Two switches sit behind the scenes: one lets a person see all org tasks and the Dashboard (that is the Reviewer), the other adds full management on top (that is the Admin). Two more switches, under Plan on the Roles screen, give the Founder and Associate access to the Plan. The tags in this manual are plain-language names for those switches. If you cannot do something you think you should, an admin needs to adjust your role.

✓ Seeing a task is not the same as editing it

A Reviewer can open every task but the controls are simply not there for them, they read. Collaborators are in between: they can comment, attach documents and add sub-tasks on a task, but the core fields (owner, deadline, status) stay in the hands of the owner, the creator, or an admin.

Two things that sit outside roles

Two parts of Docket are governed separately from the three roles above, and both get their own chapters:

  • The Dashboard can be handed to anyone, even a plain Member, without making them a Reviewer. See The Dashboard.
  • Private Spaces are gated by membership, not role. Being an Admin does not let you into someone's private space; you have to be added (or break in through the audited admin recovery). See Spaces.
