Docket opens with one click using your work Google account. Once you are in, a slim icon rail on the left takes you between the main screens, a sidebar beside it holds the Plan and your Spaces, and a top bar carries search and the + New button. This chapter covers signing in and finding your way around, on a computer and on a phone.

Signing in

Open docket.legendoftoys.com and press Sign in with Google on the sign-in card, using your @legendoftoys.com account. Personal accounts are refused; if sign-in bounces you back, sign out of any personal Google account first or use a fresh browser profile. There is nothing to set up, your account already knows which team you are on and what you are allowed to see.

The icon rail (computer)

The narrow strip at the far left is the main menu. Each icon has its name under it, and the page you are on is highlighted in yellow. You only ever see the icons your role allows.

The sidebar: Plan and Spaces

Next to the rail, on the Tasks and Plan screens, the sidebar holds the Plan and your Spaces. Programs no longer have a sidebar section; the Plan has taken their place, and old program links still open as a read-only list.

Plan

At the top, a Plan row, then a short list beneath it. Which rows you see depends on who you are:

  • Three weeks, Tree and Review: everyone.
  • Templates: only if you may make or use a template.
  • Re-plan and Founders’ catch-up: Founders only.

Each has its own chapter in The Plan part.

Spaces

General (the open space, always there), then My spaces (private spaces you own) and By others (private spaces someone added you to). The + beside the heading creates a new space.

Each group has a small arrow to fold it open or shut, and Docket remembers your choice. Customize sidebar at the bottom lets you tick whether to show My spaces and By others. The sidebar can be hidden to give the list more room: use the panel button at the left of the top bar, or press [.

On an Admin screen the sidebar switches to the three admin pages instead (see the Administration part).

The command bar: search and jump

Press ⌘K (Ctrl K on Windows and Linux), or click the search box, to open the command bar from any screen. Type a few letters and it fuzzy-matches across:

  • Tasks, in every space you can open: by title or DKT- number. Type at least two characters, or a number in any form (247, DKT-247 or #247). Words match in any order, so sop qc finds a task called “QC checks for SOP”. If nothing matches exactly, Docket tries a typo-tolerant match (chekclist finds Checklist); that fallback appears only when there are no exact hits. Each result shows its space and, for a sub-task, its parent (↳ DKT-…) on a desktop screen. Enter opens the task in place; Ctrl/⌘ + Enter opens it as a full page.
  • Spaces: jump straight to one.
  • Navigation: any screen you can reach from the rail.
  • Commands: New task / space, Go to My tasks, switch List or Board view and row density (on the Tasks list), toggle the sidebar, keyboard shortcuts. While a task you can edit is open, Add dependency to DKT-… also appears (see Inside a Task).

With nothing typed it shows your last five picks under Recent, kept separately for each person on a shared browser. Use ↑ ↓ to move, Enter to run, Esc to close. While it looks across spaces the bar shows Searching all spaces….

ⓘ You only find what you could open

Search follows the same rule as opening a task. In General you find tasks that are yours, in your department, or that you collaborate on (everyone with view-all finds every General task). A private space’s tasks appear only if you own it or are a member of it, and that includes admins. It works from any screen, not just the Tasks list.

Where you land

When you open Docket it sends you to the right place for your role. Founders and reviewers, the people who can see everything, land on the Dashboard. Everyone else lands on the Tasks list, which is where day-to-day work happens.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • ⌘K / Ctrl K opens the command bar from anywhere, even while typing.
  • / jumps into the search box on the Tasks list; on every other screen it opens the command bar.
  • c starts a new task, and [ (or ⌘\) hides or shows the sidebar.
  • ? shows the full shortcut sheet.
  • The Tasks list and an open task have their own keys, covered in those chapters.

On a phone

At phone width (767 pixels and narrower) the rail and sidebar give way to a tab bar along the bottom:

Tabs

Tasks, My tasks, Checklist, then Dashboard if you have it (otherwise Scratchpad), and More.

More

Plan, Scratchpad (when Dashboard has taken its tab), Manual, Admin for admins, Keyboard shortcuts and Sign out.

Plan and Spaces

Tap the menu button at the left of the top bar to slide the sidebar in. The top bar also has search and a + that starts a new task.

The Manual (this guide) opens inside the same frame. On a phone it shows a Contents button to pick a chapter and a PDF button to download the printable version.

✓ Quick capture on the go

The yellow + in the top bar is always one tap away on a phone. Type the title, add it, and tidy up later.
