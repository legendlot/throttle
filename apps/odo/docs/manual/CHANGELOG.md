# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-01

First edition. Built definitions-first at Afshaan's direction, with the Channels team as the
primary reader.

- **Getting Started** (3): what Odo is and is not, navigation and the freshness stamp, roles.
- **The Definitions** (5), the centrepiece: the revenue ladder and why Odo's *gross* is not the
  Channels team's *gross*; orders vs units vs AOV, including that cancellations are **orders and
  cannot be converted to units**; cancellations vs returns vs RTO and the dates each lands on;
  what counts as a sale per channel, including the outright-purchase channels where our sale to
  the buyer is the revenue event; and what is deliberately excluded from the totals.
- **Screens** (14): Overview, Sales, Catalog, Pipeline and Admin, one chapter per screen, each
  deferring to the definitions rather than restating them.

Known limitations stated in the text rather than omitted: the flat 18% tax strip understates
L.O.T Build net; standard cost is manual and stale, so margin is only as good as the cost table;
stock alerting is Website-only; and Dyno is explicitly a sandbox that must not carry a recurring
report.
