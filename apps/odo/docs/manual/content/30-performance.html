This is the screen to reconcile from

Because Performance shows each rung of the ladder rather than only the headline, it is the right screen to open when your figure and someone else's disagree. Walk down the rungs together and the difference usually names itself: a discount treatment, a returns date, or tax.

⚠ Watch out

The most recent two weeks are provisional. Late orders, cancellations and refunds are still settling, so a fortnight-old figure will firm up. Do not read a dip at the right-hand edge of the chart as a trend.
