Who
Channels
Updates
Website every 5 minutes; Amazon hourly.

Watch and History

Watch is the current position. History is the record of when a product went out of stock and came back, which is what turns "sales fell" into "we had nothing to sell".

ⓘ Note

Out of stock is confirmed across two consecutive readings rather than one, so a momentary blip during a restock does not register as an outage.

⚠ Watch out

Stock alerting and the funnel stock markers are currently Website only. Amazon stock is captured but does not raise alerts, so an Amazon stockout will not notify anyone.
