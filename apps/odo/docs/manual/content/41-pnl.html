The full profit and loss: revenue down to contribution, per channel and overall. This is the most restricted screen in Odo.

What it’s for
Revenue through to margin.
Read or act?
Read, plus manual cost entry.
Who
Super Admin only.
Grain
Monthly, by channel family.

The lines

GMV

Gross less discounts. The top of the P&L.

RTO and Refund

Goods that came back, split so an RTO is not double-counted against a refund.

Taxes

GST separated out.

COGS

Units multiplied by standard cost.

Logistics and Platform fee

Settlement-derived where a channel provides it, manually entered where it does not.

CAC

Advertising spend attributed to the channel family.

Brand marketing and SG&A

Company-level costs, entered manually.

⛔ Important

Standard cost is manual and can go stale. COGS is only as good as the last time somebody updated the cost table, and it has not been revised in several months. Check the cost basis before quoting a margin, especially a margin that looks unusually healthy.

⚠ Watch out

Fees are dated to settlement while sales are dated to the sale. A month's fees can therefore trail its revenue. This is deliberate and consistent across the P&L screens, but it makes a single month's margin noisier than a quarter's.

Manual lines

Where a channel gives us no fee or logistics feed, the figure is typed in per month. A blank is genuinely blank, not zero cost, so a channel showing no logistics may simply be unentered.
