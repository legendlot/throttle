Accessories and others

Gift wrapping, spare parts and paid repairs are bundled onto a single line on the Dashboard and are deliberately not counted in Website revenue or units. They are real money, but they are not product sales, and folding them in would inflate per-variant figures and restate months of already-published numbers.

So a figure appearing on that line and not in the totals is correct behaviour. It is a memo, shown so nothing is hidden.

Unmapped SKUs

When a channel sends a product code Odo does not recognise, the sale is held in a queue rather than guessed at. It is excluded from the totals until a human says which product it is, and the moment they do, the history backfills automatically.

This is why a channel figure can rise retrospectively: somebody resolved a mapping, and revenue that was always real became attributable. Most of the queue is zero-value replacement and spare-part codes that will never be mapped; only a minority carries value.

✓ Tip

A product name that Odo does not recognise is not always an unregistered product. LOT has sometimes sold the same item under a different name on a different channel, because margins differed by channel. Before concluding a SKU was never set up, ask whether it is an old alias.

Multi-channel fulfilment

A number of Amazon rows are orders Amazon fulfilled on our behalf that were placed somewhere else, and they arrive valued at zero. Revenue is unaffected, because zero is correct, but they do lift Amazon's unit count slightly and drag its average selling price down. This is known and currently accepted.

Settlement, fees and payouts

What a platform actually pays us is not revenue. Fees and settlements appear in P&L as costs, not as a reduction of the top line. If you are reconciling a bank credit to Odo, expect a gap the size of the platform's commission, and expect it to be legitimate.

⛔ Important

Every exclusion here is a recorded decision. If one of them looks like a bug and you are tempted to "fix" it, raise it first. Several have been re-argued and re-confirmed more than once, and reversing one silently would move numbers the business has already published.
