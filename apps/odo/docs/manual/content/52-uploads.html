What gets uploaded

Quick-commerce sales come from the platforms' own seller reports, and courier freight invoices come in as a file. Both replace the period they cover rather than adding to it, so re-uploading a corrected file is the normal way to fix a mistake.

⚠ Watch out

Upload the right period. A file covering a shorter window than you think will replace the period it covers, which can look like sales disappearing.
