Access

Odo's access list is its own. Granting someone a role here gives them nothing in any other LOT app, and their access elsewhere gives them nothing here. A role can be switched off with the active flag rather than deleted, which preserves the record of who had what.

⛔ Important

Super Admin is the only role that can open P&L, and P&L carries cost, margin and staff cost. Grant it deliberately and rarely.

Channel settings

The setting that most affects the numbers decides whether a channel's sales order is the revenue event. Turn it on for a buyer who purchases outright, such as Flipkart Managed, General Trade or Peeko. Leave it off for a platform where we sell to the consumer and count the consumer order instead.

⛔ Important

Getting that setting wrong double-counts or hides an entire channel. Turning it on for a platform we already count consumer sales from would count the same goods twice. If you are not certain which kind of channel it is, ask before changing it.
