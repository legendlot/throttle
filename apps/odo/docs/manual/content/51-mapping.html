The queue of product codes a channel sent that Odo could not match to a product. Until they are resolved, those sales sit outside every total.

What it’s for
Identify unknown channel SKUs.
Read or act?
Action.
Who
Sales Ops Admin
Effect
Resolving backfills history automatically.

Mapping one

  1. Find the row. It shows the channel, the code the channel sent, the units and the value being held.
  2. Search the variant. Type the product name and pick the exact variant.
  3. Map. The history recalculates immediately, so past months change too.
⛔ Important

Do not guess a variant. Mapping to the wrong colour books real revenue against a product that never sold it, and because the fix backfills, the error backfills too. If a line does not name a colour and the product has more than one, ask the person who owns that channel.

✓ Tip

Most of the queue is zero-value: replacement, creator-shipment and spare-part codes that are deliberately never mapped. Sort by value and work the rows that carry money.

⚠ Watch out

An unfamiliar name is not always a new product. LOT has sold the same item under a different name on a different channel because margins differed. Check for an alias before assuming a SKU was never registered.
