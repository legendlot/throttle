Odo has its own access list, separate from every other LOT app. Being an admin in Garage or Redline gives you nothing here.

Access model
Per-system, managed on Admin.
Default
No role means no access at all.
Most restricted
P&L: Super Admin only.
Kill switch
A role can be switched off without deleting it.

The roles

RoleCan open
ChannelsEvery reporting screen: Dashboard, Live, Performance, Channels, Marketing, Funnel, Products, Inventory, Dyno. Not P&L.
Sales OpsThe above, plus the Pipeline screens that keep data flowing: Mapping, Uploads, Connectors.
AdminThe above, plus Admin: who has access, and channel configuration.
Super AdminEverything, and the only role that can open P&L.

Why P&L is walled off

P&L carries product cost, margin, marketing spend and staff cost. That is the most commercially sensitive data in Odo, so it sits behind the single narrowest gate in the app rather than behind an ordinary permission. If you can see revenue but not margin, that is the design working, not a misconfiguration.

⚠ Watch out

Screenshots leak permissions. A P&L figure pasted into a channel thread is visible to everyone in it, whatever their Odo role. Quote revenue freely; treat cost and margin as need-to-know.
