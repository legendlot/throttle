Sub-pages
Cross-channel, and P&L by product.

Cross-channel

Daily run rate per product across every channel, which is the view for spotting a product accelerating or dying without having to open each channel in turn.

P&L by product

Margin per product. It opens on Gross Margin across all channels; picking a single channel extends it through contribution margin, because fees and advertising only mean something once you know which channel you are talking about.

⚠ Watch out

A product flagged no cost has no standard cost recorded, so its margin reads 100% and is wrong. Treat those rows as unknown rather than excellent. A handful of live products are currently in that state.

ⓘ Note

The Account-level (unattributable) row holds fees and spend that belong to the channel but pin to no single product. It exists so the columns still add up to the channel P&L, and it is never spread across products, because doing so would invent per-product costs.
