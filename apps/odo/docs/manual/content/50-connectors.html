What OK actually means

⛔ Important

A green tick means "we checked this feed and the run succeeded". It does not mean data arrived. A channel that genuinely sold nothing shows green, correctly. So green plus a suspiciously flat chart is not a contradiction, and it is not proof the feed is fine either. Check the row count and the last sale date, not just the tick.

The buttons

  1. Refresh. Runs the feed now from where it left off. Safe to press: re-running a period cannot double-count.
  2. Backfill. Re-walks a longer history. Slower, for when a period needs rebuilding.
  3. Disable. Stops the feed running on schedule. Use sparingly, and remember it will then go stale silently.

Unmapped counts

Each connector shows how many product codes it has seen that Odo cannot identify. A rising count means revenue is being held out of the totals, so it is worth clearing. See Mapping.
