Getting around

The left rail groups every screen under five headings, and they follow the shape of the data rather than the shape of the org:

GroupWhat lives there
OverviewDashboard and Live. Where you land, and what is happening right now.
SalesPerformance, Channels, Marketing, Funnel. Revenue over time and where it came from.
CatalogProducts, P&L, Inventory. The same money cut by product instead of by channel.
PipelineDyno, Mapping, Connectors, Uploads. The plumbing behind every other screen.
AdminAccess and channel configuration.

The date range applies to the screen you are on

Most screens carry a range picker with Today, 7D, 30D, 90D, MTD, Last mo and FY shortcuts. The range does not follow you between screens, so if two screens disagree, check that both are set to the same period before assuming anything is broken.

The freshness stamp, and how to read it honestly

The header carries a "Data as of" chip. It shows the oldest feed among those the current screen actually reads, not the newest, and that is deliberate: a stale channel can never hide behind a fresh one sitting next to it.

Green

Everything this screen reads was refreshed within about three hours.

Amber

Something is up to a day behind. Usable, but say so if you are quoting it.

Red

More than a day behind, or a feed reported an error. Check Connectors before using the number.

ⓘ Note

A healthy feed that simply had no sales still counts as fresh. "We checked and there was nothing" is a success, not a failure, so a quiet channel does not turn the chip red.
