Two session counts, deliberately

Sessions are available from both the analytics tag and the storefront itself, and the two never agree exactly because they count differently. Odo shows the conversion rate against both rather than picking a winner, so you can see how much of a change is real and how much is measurement.

ⓘ Note

Recent days are provisional here too. Orders settle and session data is revised, so the last fortnight will move.

Stock markers

The timeline marks when a product went out of stock, because an unexplained conversion dip is very often an availability problem rather than a demand one. These markers are Website-scoped.
