Reading the tiles

The headline figures follow the ladder in Gross, Net and the Revenue Ladder. Net is the one to quote. Comparisons are against the equivalent previous period, so a 30-day view compares to the 30 days before it, not to last calendar month.

⚠ Watch out

The Accessories and others line is a memo and sits outside every total on this screen. Adding it to the revenue tile will not reconcile with anything.

Product search

The product table can be searched directly, which is usually faster than going to Products when you have a specific SKU in mind.

✓ Tip

If a channel looks unexpectedly flat, check its trailing baseline before raising it. A drop from an unusually high week is a return to normal, not a collapse. This has been mistaken for a broken feed more than once.
