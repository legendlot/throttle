Website only

Live covers the Website and nothing else. Marketplaces do not push orders to us as they happen, so a quiet Live screen says nothing at all about Amazon or quick-commerce.

ⓘ Note

Live is fed by a different mechanism from the hourly figures, so Live and the Dashboard can differ for a short while. Live is ahead, not more correct: the hourly calculation is what settles cancellations and returns.

Today window and the product table

The Today window shows the day so far in IST, and the searchable product table shows what is actually moving. During a launch this is the fastest way to see whether the thing you launched is the thing selling.
