Sponsored and DSP are separate lenses

Amazon Sponsored and Amazon DSP are reported separately and must never be added together. They can target the same products, so summing them double-counts the same commercial activity.

⚠ Watch out

Spend is platform-reported and revenue is order-reported, and they settle on different clocks. A same-day ratio of the two is noisy. Compare over a week or more.
