Leave is applied for, approved and counted in Podium. Your balance is worked out from a ledger of credits and leave taken; nobody types it. Payroll still runs in RazorpayX: HR enters the unpaid days there by hand from a report in Podium.

What it’s for
Apply, approve, balances, unpaid days.
Read or act?
Act.
Who
Employee Manager HR Admin
Where
People → Leave, /leave. HR: Admin → Leave Settings, /admin/leave.

Who is covered

Leave covers Fraternitas staff on RazorpayX payroll, interns included. Floor staff on Redline attendance are not covered. If you are not covered, Leave shows “Leave is not set up for you in Podium”; ask HR. A manager who is not covered still sees the team half of the page.

HR turns leave on or off per person: open their edit form and set Leave policy to Fraternitas or Not covered.

Balances

The tiles at the top of Leave show what you can take.

  • Privilege leave (PL): 1.5 days credited each month, on the 1st.
  • Sick leave (SL): 1 day credited each month, on the 1st.
  • Period leave: shown only to people recorded as female. 1 day a month, does not build up.
  • Optional holiday: 1 a year, from the optional list.

PL and SL tiles show available, your balance and how far you can borrow. A red tile means the balance is negative. A joiner who starts on or before the 15th earns that month in full; after the 15th, the first credit is next month. Monthly credits begin from the cutover month HR sets; before that, your opening balance stands in.

Applying for leave

  1. Open Leave and find Apply for leave or work from home.
  2. Pick the Type: Privilege leave, Sick leave, Period leave, Optional holiday or Work from home.
  3. Set the dates. From, and To (leave empty for one day). For one day, Day is Full day, First half or Second half. For a range, First day is Full day or From second half, and Last day is Full day or Until first half. Optional holiday: pick one from the list. Period leave: one day, with Date.
  4. Reason is optional. Only your approver and HR see it.
  5. Check the count that appears under the form, then press Request leave. Your approver is emailed.
  • The day count is live. Weekends and fixed holidays are free: only working days (Monday to Friday) count. A holiday on a weekend gives no day in lieu.
  • PL must start today or later. SL can be applied up to 7 days back.
  • A request cannot cross 31 December, covers at most 60 working days, and cannot overlap another pending or approved request.
  • Upcoming holidays sits beside the form.

HR can record leave for someone else with the For picker on the form. It still goes to their approver.

Borrowing ahead, and unpaid days

If you ask for more PL or SL than your balance, the line under the form says so before you submit.

  • Borrowed ahead: the balance goes negative, up to what you will still earn by 31 December. Later monthly credits pay it back first.
  • Unpaid: anything beyond that is unpaid (loss of pay, LOP). It still needs approval.
⚠ Unpaid days reach payroll

Approved unpaid days go on the HR payroll report below. They are not taken from your balance.

Withdraw and cancel

  • Pending: press Withdraw in My requests.
  • Approved, not started: press Cancel. The days go back to your balance.
  • Started: “Started: ask your manager to cancel”. Your approver, their manager or HR cancels it.

Approving Manager

Requests from your team appear in Waiting for you. Each shows the dates, the day count, any borrowed or unpaid days, the person’s PL and SL, and their reason. Press Approve, or Reject (a note is required and they see it).

  • Your manager can also decide your team’s requests, to cover you when you are away. HR can too. Nobody can decide their own leave.
  • If the balance changed since the person applied, Podium reloads and shows today’s split. Check it and decide again.
  • With no manager, a founder’s leave goes to the other founder; anyone else’s goes to HR. If your manager is to be hired, your request goes to your team’s founder owner instead (a sub-team uses its parent’s owner if it has none).

Who’s out Manager HR

The Who’s out (and who works from home) card is a calendar for your team. Switch Week or Month, step with the arrows, or press Today. Gold is leave, a lighter shade is pending; half days fill half the cell; weekends and fixed holidays are greyed. Below it, each request has a Cancel button for approved leave.

Work from home

Pick Work from home as the Type. It uses no balance, never counts as leave and never reaches payroll. It is approved by your manager; your manager’s manager or HR can also approve it (HR when you have no manager). If your manager is to be hired, your team’s founder owner approves it. The form names the approver. It shows on the calendar in blue, marked WFH.

Maternity and paternity HR

These are granted by HR; they are not on the apply form and use no balance. In Grant maternity / paternity leave pick the person, the type and the start date, then press Grant. It is approved at once and the person is emailed.

  • Maternity: 26 weeks (182 calendar days). The person must be recorded as female. A note is required: record the eligibility check (80 days worked in the last 12 months).
  • Paternity: 15 calendar days from the start, weekends and holidays included (two weeks plus a day). The person must be recorded as male.
  • If gender is not recorded, fill it in the profile first. You cannot grant your own.
  • Either may cross 31 December. Ends on is optional and defaults to the full length; set it when you already know the actual end. Returning early? Cancel it from the team view and grant again with the actual end date. The person sees “Granted by HR: ask HR to change or end it”.

HR tools HR

Open Leave Settings under Admin.

Cutover

The month monthly credits start from. It can only move earlier once credits exist. Press Save.

Holidays

Date, name and Fixed or Optional. Add with Add; rename or delete future ones only. A change after a request is approved does not recount it; Podium tells you how many requests include the date. Add next year’s list before January.

Covered people

Each person’s PL and SL, and whether the opening is set. Balance opens the entry: the first time per type it is Set opening (once); after that it is Adjust, a plus or minus in steps of 0.5 with a required note. Ledger lists every credit and debit.

Payroll: unpaid days HR

Payroll — unpaid days is at the top of Leave Settings. Pick the month (it opens on this month). It lists each person with unpaid days: code, RazorpayX id, name, days and the dates. Unpaid days fall on the last working days of the request. Enter these in RazorpayX by hand. Podium does not push them.

  1. Clear the warning. If requests touching the month are still pending, a banner names them. Decide them before payroll.
  2. Download CSV and enter the figures in RazorpayX. A “missing” RazorpayX id needs fixing in the profile.
  3. Press Mark exported. Podium keeps a snapshot.
  4. Watch for changes. If a cancel or late approval changes the month after the export, the card lists who (was, now). Carry the difference into next month’s payroll.
ⓘ Exits are not here

A negative balance at exit is settled in the F&F, not on this report. A holiday added after approval can flag a row: check its dates.

Year end

  • PL carries up to 5 days. The rest lapses. A negative balance carries as it is.
  • SL lapses in full.
  • This happens by itself on the first look at balances in January.

HR From 1 November to 31 January, Year end in Leave Settings previews each person’s projected PL and SL at 31 December, what PL carries and what lapses. It is read only: balance plus credits still to come, less pending requests.

Exits

On the exit checklist, the Leave balance confirmed line fills itself. It shows PL and SL at the last working day (including credits still to land that month), and notes that SL is not paid out. A negative balance is unpaid days in the F&F settlement; positive PL is shown, and Finance decides on encashment.

  • HR cannot mark the line Done while a request is pending, or while leave runs past the last working day. Cancel or decide it first.
  • Done saves the figures on the exit. Reopening clears them.
  • For someone not covered, HR confirms the balance by hand.
