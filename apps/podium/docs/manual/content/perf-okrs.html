OKRs are how Podium tracks what we're trying to achieve each half-year and how far we've got. An Objective is a short, ambitious statement of intent; under it sit two to five Key Results — measurable checks that tell you whether the objective actually happened. They cascade from the company down to departments and individuals, run on the same twice-yearly rhythm as appraisals, and score themselves from your check-ins.

What it’s for
Set goals, track progress, grade the outcome.
Cadence
Two cycles a year (Apr & Oct), aligned to appraisals.
Who
Employee Manager HR Admin
Scope
Company & dept goals are open; individual goals stay within the reporting line.

The three levels

Company

The handful of goals the whole business is pushing on this cycle. Set by HR/leadership. Everyone can see them.

Department

A team's goals, ideally aligned up to a company objective. Set by HR or the department head. Everyone can see them.

Individual

Your own goals for the cycle, ideally aligned up to a department or company objective. You (or your manager) set them. Only you, your managers up the chain, and HR can see them — not your peers.

Key results & scoring

Each key result has a start, a target, and a live current value — or, for a yes/no result, a simple milestone. Its score is how far you've moved from start to target, capped between 0% and 100%. The objective's overall progress is the weighted average of its key results.

Two numbers matter: the auto progress (computed continuously from your check-ins) and, at the end, the final grade — a score you set yourself when the cycle moves to scoring, alongside a short reflection. The auto number is shown next to it for reference. Grading is deliberately a judgment call, not just the arithmetic.

Checking in

Open any objective and, on each key result, click Check in: enter the new current value, pick a confidence (On track / At risk / Off track), and add an optional note on what moved. Every check-in is kept as history, and the progress bar updates immediately.

✓ A weekly rhythm beats a scramble

Update your key results little and often. A result that hasn't been touched in a fortnight gets a Needs check-in flag on your My Performance page and on the cycle overview — a gentle nudge, not a telling-off.

The cycle, start to finish (HR)

HR runs cycles from OKRs → Manage cycles. A cycle moves through four states:

  • Draft — HR is building it and seeding company & department objectives; nobody else sees it yet.
  • Active — the working window. People add key results and check in; progress is live.
  • Scoring — end of cycle. Owners set the final grade and a reflection on each objective.
  • Closed — read-only archive.

OKRs & appraisals

Because a cycle is anchored to an appraisal date, the individual objectives for that period show up — read-only — on the appraisal screen, so the reviewer has the goals in front of them. They are context only and never change the rating or the increment.
