Exits is how Podium handles someone leaving. You start an exit with a last working day, work through a no-dues checklist that checks Snorkel and Docket live, and Close the exit, which is the only way a person becomes Exited. The Exits page is HR's board of everything in flight.

To start an exit

Open the person's profile (an employee uses their own My Performance page; a manager can also use Your team card there). The Exit card has a button: Resign for yourself, Start exit for someone else.

  1. Reason. Resignation, Termination, Absconding, End of internship, End of contract or Other (Other needs a note). Someone resigning for themselves is always “Resignation”.
  2. Notice date. Today for a resignation. For others it can be up to 7 days back (HR can record an older one) and never before they joined.
  3. Last working day (LWD). Defaults to the notice date plus the notice period. Only HR can set a different date, and must say why; an LWD in an earlier month (which changes a closed payroll month) also needs a reason. HR can note whether the person is eligible for rehire, privately.
  4. Press Start exit and confirm. The person stays Active, shown as On notice, and the checklist is built.

The no-dues checklist

The Exit card groups lines by owner (HR, Manager, IT, Admin, Finance, Store). HR can tick any line; a manager can tick only the manager lines. A line is marked Done or N/A (where allowed, with a reason) and can be Reopened. Some lines are checked live:

  • Assets, from Snorkel: the assets still assigned to them are listed. If Snorkel cannot tell (no linked login), HR checks by hand and marks the line Done with a note of how.
  • Open work, from Docket: their open tasks are listed.
  • Payment requests, from Snorkel: open requests are listed; Finance checks by hand if they cannot be found.
  • LOT access is done by the close itself.

The F&F line is a later phase: it is not calculated here and is not needed to close.

Closing the exit

HR sees Close exit and Cancel exit at the bottom. Under Before it can close Podium lists the live blockers: it is before the last working day, required lines are open, assets still assigned, open Docket tasks. Close stays greyed out until the list is empty. On close, the person becomes Exited on their last working day, their LOT login, Podium role and salary access end, and their direct reports move up a level. Closing is the only way to mark someone exited; the Google sync no longer exits anyone. Cancel exit (for example a withdrawn resignation) asks for a reason. After closing, HR can still add exit-interview notes and the private rehire flag.

Relieving and experience letters

HR issues both letters from Podium. They appear in the Relieving and experience letters area: on the Relieving letters line of the checklist while the exit is open, and on the Exit card once it is closed. Letters are available on or after the last working day, on open and closed exits, but never on a cancelled exit.

  1. Pick the letter. Press Relieving letter or Experience letter. If one has already been issued the button reads Re-issue….
  2. Pick a signatory. Choose from the active signatories. The person leaving cannot sign their own letter.
  3. Preview. The letter is shown as it will be issued, with its reference, date and template version. If something blocks it, a warning says what and Issue stays greyed out.
  4. Press Issue and confirm.
  • References. Relieving letters are numbered REL-YYYY-NNN and experience letters EXP-YYYY-NNN. Re-issuing keeps the same reference and bumps the revision (r2, r3…); the earlier revisions stay on record.
  • Where they are stored. Each letter is saved as a PDF among the person's documents, visible to HR and to the person. It can be downloaded from the list under the line. An issued letter is the record and cannot be deleted; issue a new revision instead.
  • Podium never emails them. HR downloads the PDF and sends it.
  • The checklist line is separate. Issuing a letter does not tick Relieving letters. HR ticks Done themselves; once both letters exist, the document attached to that tick defaults to the latest relieving letter.
⚠ When a letter is refused

The experience letter is refused if the person has no joining date on record. Both letters are refused if they have no designation. Fix the profile first, then preview again. Issuing before the last working day is also refused (“Letters are issued on or after the last working day”).

Editing the letter wording

HR edits the wording at Admin → Exit Checklist, in the Letter templates card at the bottom. Pick Relieving or Experience, then change the title, body, entity name and letterhead address. The body uses merge fields such as {{employee_name}} (the full list is shown under the editor); a blank line starts a new paragraph. Add a short version note, press Preview to see it with sample data, then Save new version. Each save makes a new version that is used from then on; letters already issued keep the version they were made with. A template with an unknown field or an unclosed section cannot be saved.

The Exits page

Four cards, top to bottom:

Open exits

Everyone on notice, their reason, last working day with days left (red when past), checklist progress and what still blocks closing, or “Ready to close”. Click a row for the person.

Google accounts to action

An exited person's Google account is never suspended automatically; until someone acts it still signs in and still costs a seat. A Google admin picks one of three: Drive → manager, suspend (hands their Drive to the manager, then suspends; use Check transfer if it is still running), Suspend now (the Drive stays with the suspended account), or Keep until… (a future date and a reason; it comes back as due once the date passes).

Backfill review

People marked exited before exits were tracked. Pick the reason, confirm the real last working day and press Confirm. No guessing: the date decides the month their pay stops counting toward overheads.

Exited, still with access

Appears only when someone exited still has a login, role, salary access, Google-admin power or assets. Revoke access removes the first four (logged); Open Google item adds them to the Google list. Nothing is revoked on its own.

ⓘ Checklist lines are editable

HR can change which lines exist and who owns them at Admin → Exit Checklist, where the letter wording is also edited.
