Users is where an admin hands out Podium roles. Every LOT person with a login appears in one list; you pick a Podium role for each. Anyone left without a role gets self-only access, which is a real, working level, just their own profile and wins.

What it’s for
Assigning a Podium role to each person.
Read or act?
Act: set one role per person.
Who
Admin
Default
No role = self-only.

Reading the list

Each row is a person with a login: their name, email, their current Podium role (or “self-only (default)” if none), and a dropdown to change it. A search box up top filters by name, email or role.

Assigning a role

  1. Find the person with the search box.
  2. Pick a role from their dropdown. The list is the roles you defined on Roles & Permissions.
  3. That's it. The change saves immediately. To remove someone's access back to baseline, choose — none (self-only) —.
ⓘ Accounts come from Garage; this only sets People access

You don't create logins here; LOT accounts are made in Garage. The Users screen only decides what a person can do in Podium. So a brand-new joiner has a login as soon as Garage sets it up, and self-only access in Podium until you give them a wider role.

⚠ One Podium role per person

Each person has exactly one Podium role at a time, so picking a new one replaces the old. If someone needs an unusual mix of access, the answer is to define a role with that mix on Roles & Permissions and assign it, rather than trying to stack roles.

✓ Manager access doesn't need a role

Remember that seeing your team comes from the org chart, not from a role here. You don't need to assign anyone a “manager” role for them to see their reports; just make sure people's managers are set on their profiles. Roles here are for the wider tiers: HR, compensation and admin.
