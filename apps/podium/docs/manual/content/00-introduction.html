Podium is the Legend of Toys people & performance system: one place for who works here, how the company is structured, the good work people do through the year, and the twice-yearly appraisal. It is the most private system we run, so almost everything you see is scoped tightly to you, your team and your role. This manual is your self-serve guide to every screen in it.

What Podium holds

Podium brings together things that used to live in spreadsheets, chat threads and people's memories:

AreaWhat it covers
PeopleEvery person's profile: contact details, employment, documents and (for HR) compensation history. A headcount dashboard and a searchable directory.
Org chartThe live reporting structure, who reports to whom, drawn from each person's manager. Snapshots let you keep a picture of how the org looked on a given date.
PerformanceContinuous record-keeping through the year: observations a manager logs, wins people record about themselves, and 1:1 meeting notes.
AppraisalsThe twice-yearly review: a self-review, a manager review, an HR calibration, the final rating shared with you, and the increment that follows.
Org designThe registry of job roles (with their job descriptions and KPIs) and the department structure everything else hangs off.

Who it is for

Everyone at Legend of Toys uses Podium, but most people use only a small corner of it:

  • Everyone can see their own profile, record their own wins, and take part in their own appraisal.
  • Managers additionally see their team, log observations and 1:1s, and review their team's appraisals.
  • HR and admins run the whole thing: people, departments, roles, documents, appraisal cycles and settings.
ⓘ Privacy is the whole point

Unlike our other systems, Podium does not show everything to everyone. What you can see depends on who you are, who reports to you, and the access your account has been given. The next two chapters explain exactly how that works, because it shapes every screen in this manual.
