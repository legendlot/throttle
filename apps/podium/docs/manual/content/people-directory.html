The Directory is the searchable list of everyone at Legend of Toys. It is where you look someone up, filter by team or status, and open a profile. For HR it is also the door to adding a new person and to pulling the latest joiners and leavers from Google.

Reading the list

Each row shows the person's code (their EMP number, in yellow), name (with a preferred name in brackets if they have one), title, department, manager and a status badge. Click any row, or use the arrow keys and Enter, to open that person's profile.

Narrowing it down

Two controls sit above the list:

  • Status tabs: Active (the default), On Leave, Notice, Exited, or All. Most of the time Active is what you want; switch to Exited or All to find someone who has left.
  • Search and department: the search box matches name, code, title or email; the dropdown filters to one department. Use them together to zero in.

HR actions

If you're HR or an admin, two buttons appear top-right:

  1. New Person opens the form to create a profile from scratch. See the New Person chapter.
  2. Sync from Google pulls the company's Google Workspace directory and shows you who's new and who looks like they've left, for you to review before anything is written. See the callout below.
ⓘ Sync from Google, in plain terms

The sync compares Podium against the Google directory and proposes changes: new joiners to add and people who've disappeared from Google (possible leavers). Nothing is saved until you confirm. You tick who to bring in and who to mark as exited; shared mailboxes and accounts you've told it to ignore are left out. It never overwrites edits you've made by hand and never guesses a reporting line, so a new joiner still needs their manager set afterwards.

⚠ A blank Directory isn't broken

If you have only self-serve access, you won't have the Directory at all; that's the access tier working as intended (see Roles & What You Can See). If you can open it but a filter returns nothing, check the status tab: someone you expect to find may have left and now sits under Exited.
