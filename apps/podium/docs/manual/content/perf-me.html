My Performance is your own corner of Podium: the wins you've recorded, the observations and 1:1 notes that have been shared with you, the open action items from your meetings, and your live appraisal. Everyone has this page, even with no other access, and it always shows only your own data.

The Appraisals block

When there's a live appraisal cycle that involves you, a yellow Appraisals block sits at the top. It's your one-click way in, and it's how you take part without needing any special menu:

  • Your own appraisal: “start your self-review,” “self-review submitted (editable),” or “your result is ready” once HR shares it. Click through to the appraisal screen.
  • Reviews you owe: if you manage people, anyone of yours waiting on your manager review appears here too.

If there's no active cycle, the block simply isn't shown. The full walkthrough is in Filling In an Appraisal.

Open action items

If your 1:1s have left you with action items that aren't done yet, they're gathered into a banner near the top, each tagged with the meeting it came from. It's a running to-do list pulled straight from your check-ins, so nothing quietly slips.

The three tabs

My Wins

Accomplishments you record about yourself. Click Add win, give it a title (and optional detail, a date and tags), and save. These are yours to add and edit, and they're the raw material for your self-review at appraisal time.

Shared With Me

Observations your managers (or HR) have explicitly chosen to share with you. You only ever see observations on this tier; the private and managers-only ones never appear here.

My 1:1s

Your one-to-one notes: the shared notes your manager wrote and the action items, which you can read. You can't edit a 1:1 (your manager owns it), and the manager-only private notes never show.

✓ Record wins as they happen

The best time to log a win is right after it lands, not the night before your self-review. A steady trickle through the year makes the appraisal write-up almost automatic, and gives your manager real examples to draw on.

ⓘ Why you can't see every note about you

“Shared With Me” shows only what was deliberately shared. Managers also keep constructive or work-in-progress observations at a more private setting while they're still forming a view. That's by design (see the Profile chapter for the visibility tiers); it isn't that something is being hidden from you unfairly, it's that not every working note is meant to be a finished message.
