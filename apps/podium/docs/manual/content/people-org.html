The Org Chart draws the company's reporting structure as a tree: who reports to whom, all the way up to the founders. It is built automatically from each person's manager, so it is always as current as the profiles behind it. You can also keep dated snapshots of how the org looked at a point in time.

What it’s for
Seeing the reporting structure.
Read or act?
Read; HR captures snapshots.
Who
Manager HR Admin
Source
Each person's manager.

Reading the chart

Each person is a node; lines connect a manager to their reports. The founders sit at the top with no manager. Click any node to open that person's profile (when you're viewing the live chart).

Snapshots

The dropdown at the top lets you switch between Live (current) and any saved snapshot. A snapshot is a frozen picture of the whole tree on the day it was captured, useful for “how were we structured at the last appraisal?” or for a board pack.

  1. To take one (HR/admin), click Capture snapshot, give it a label, and confirm. It's saved with today's date.
  2. To view one, pick it from the dropdown. The header shows a “snapshot” tag so you know you're not looking at the live chart.
⚠ Snapshots are read-only history

While you're viewing a snapshot, clicking a node won't open a profile; it's a historical record, not the live org. Switch back to Live (current) to navigate to people again. A snapshot also never changes after capture, so it stays a faithful record even as the live org moves on.

✓ If someone's in the wrong place

The chart is only ever as right as the profiles. If a person hangs under the wrong manager, the fix isn't here; it's on their profile, where HR sets their manager. Change it there and the chart redraws itself.
