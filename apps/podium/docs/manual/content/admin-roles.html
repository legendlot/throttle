Roles & Permissions is where an admin defines what people can do in Podium. Podium runs its own set of roles, separate from every other LOT system, built from four simple permissions. You create as many named roles as you like from those permissions, then hand them out on the Users screen.

The four permissions

PermissionWhat it unlocks
ViewThe directory, org chart and dashboards. The baseline for seeing anyone beyond yourself.
People (HR)Manage people, departments, roles, documents and org snapshots, and run appraisal cycles.
CompensationSee and edit pay: increments, bonuses and salary bands. Kept separate so it can be granted on its own.
AdminManage these roles, assign people to them, and change settings.

Creating a role

  1. New Role. Give it a key (a short code like hr_lead), a readable label and an optional description.
  2. Flip on the permissions it should carry. A role with none of them is a perfectly valid “self-only” role.
  3. Save, then assign people to it on the Users screen.
⚠ Any higher permission switches View on for you

If you turn on People, Compensation or Admin, the View permission is switched on and locked automatically. That's deliberate: those jobs all need to see people in the first place, so a role can't accidentally be created that locks itself out of the screens it's meant to use.

System roles

Two roles come built in and can't be reshaped: Admin (everything) and Employee (the empty, self-only default). You can rename their label and description, but not their permissions, and you can't delete them. A handful of ready-made roles (an HR preset, a compensation preset, a people-manager preset) are also provided to copy or use as-is.

⛔ Podium roles don't touch other systems

Giving someone the HR or Admin role here changes only their People & Performance access. It does not change anything they can do in Garage, Redline or any other LOT system; those are governed separately. This isolation is the whole reason Podium has its own roles. A role with people assigned to it also can't be deleted, so reassign first.
