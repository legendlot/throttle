Podium opens with one click using your work Google account, and everything is reachable from a short sidebar grouped by what it does. This chapter covers signing in and finding your way around. What appears in your sidebar depends on your access, so two people can see different menus.

Signing in

Open podium.legendoftoys.com and sign in with your @legendoftoys.com Google account. Personal accounts are refused; if sign-in bounces you back, sign out of any personal Google account first or use a fresh browser profile. There is no separate Podium password: your Google login is your login.

ⓘ First time in?

If you sign in and Podium says you don't have an employee profile linked yet, that's normal for a brand-new joiner: ask HR to link your profile to your login. Until then you can sign in but there's nothing personal to show you.

The menu

The sidebar is grouped. You only see the groups and items your access allows:

✓ If your sidebar is short, that's expected

A self-serve employee with no special access sees only My Performance, and Podium opens straight to it. That's by design, not a bug: you have full access to your own record and nothing about anyone else. The moment HR gives you a wider role, or someone starts reporting to you, the extra menus appear.

Getting around

Most list screens (the Directory, the appraisal grid) have a search box and clickable rows: click any row to open it. The Directory and other lists also support the arrow keys with Enter to open the highlighted row. The top of a detail screen has a Back button that returns you to where you came from.

Names are links almost everywhere: a person's name in the org chart, on a team feed card, or in an appraisal grid takes you to their profile (subject to what you're allowed to see).
