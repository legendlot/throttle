Candidates is the list of everyone who has applied to any opening you can see. It keeps one record per person, however many openings they apply to, and tracks where each application stands.

What it’s for
The candidate list and each person’s file.
Read or act?
Both: add, edit, move, upload.
Who
Manager HR Admin
Added from
An opening, never from this list.

Candidate versus application

Two ideas keep this tidy:

  • A candidate is the person: name, contact, employer, skills, documents. They get one code (CAND-) and one file, forever.
  • An application is that person on one opening. The status (New, Screening, Interviewing and so on) belongs to the application, so the same person can be Rejected for one role and Interviewing for another.

The list

Open Hiring → Candidates (/hiring). Status chips filter by the latest application's status (All, New, Screening, Interviewing, On Hold, Rejected, Withdrawn); the opening dropdown narrows to one opening; the search box matches name, email or phone. Each row shows the candidate, contact, current title and location, the latest opening and its status. The buttons top right are Openings and Import CVs. A reminder under the filters says candidates are added from an opening, because the JD comes first.

To add one candidate

  1. Go to the opening (Openings, then the row). Its Candidates card has an Add candidate button. It is greyed out, with the reason beside it, when the opening has no JD or is not Open.
  2. Fill the form. Only the full name is required. There are fields for email, phone, current employer and title, experience in years, location, LinkedIn, notice period in days and notes. Pick the Source (Referral, LinkedIn, Naukri, Campus, Walk-in, Agency, Careers page, Other); a referral asks who referred them; Source detail takes an agency or campus name. Current CTC and Expected CTC appear only if you can see salary on this opening.
  3. Watch for duplicates as you tab out of the email and phone boxes (below).
  4. Press Add candidate. Podium offers to open their profile so you can upload the CV straight away.

To add many at once from CV files, use Import CVs instead.

Duplicates

When an email or phone matches someone already in Podium, a yellow box lists them with their code, contact details and number of applications. Use View to look, or Add to opening to put the existing person on this opening instead of creating a second record. If the match is someone on an opening you cannot see, you only learn that one exists (“Ask HR before creating a new record”). A person who already has an active application on the same opening cannot be added to it twice.

The candidate's page

Click a row to open /hiring/detail. It has four parts:

Profile

Contact, employer, title, experience, skills, education, LinkedIn and notes. Edit changes them for everyone; the candidate is one record across all openings.

Documents

Pick a Type (CV, Education, Experience letter, Photo, Other; plus Payslip, Signed offer and Offer as sent for the salary chain; ID proof, PAN, Address proof, Bank details and Relieving letter for HR only), optionally the application it belongs to and a title, choose a file and press Upload. PDF, Word or an image, up to 15 MB. You only see the types your access allows. HR can delete any document; the person who uploaded one can delete it within 24 hours. A document that backs an offer cannot be deleted.

Applications

One row per opening, with its status, source, notice period and (if you can see salary) current and expected CTC. This is where you move a candidate along.

Timeline

Everything that happened, in order.

Statuses and moving a candidate

On an application, the Move to… dropdown offers only the moves that make sense from the current status:

FromYou can move to
NewScreening, Rejected, Withdrawn, On Hold
ScreeningInterviewing, Rejected, Withdrawn, On Hold
InterviewingRejected, Withdrawn, On Hold (and an offer can be made from here)
On HoldNew, Screening, Interviewing, Rejected, Withdrawn
OfferDeclined, Withdrawn, Rejected (close the live offer first)
AcceptedWithdrawn, No Show

Offer, Accepted and Joined cannot be picked by hand. They happen when an offer is sent, when acceptance is recorded, and when joining is confirmed (see Offers and Joining). Rejected, Withdrawn and Declined ask you to pick a reason (Not a fit, Skills, Experience, Compensation, Location, Notice period, Candidate withdrew, No response, Duplicate, Other). The reason is visible to everyone who sees the application; the optional free-text note beside it is visible only to the opening's salary chain.

The small arrows icon (Move to another opening) closes the application as withdrawn and starts a new one at New on another open opening. You must give a reason, and salary fields are not copied. Edit on an application changes its source, referrer, notice period and CTC.

ⓘ What you can see depends on your seat

The worker decides, per opening, who can see a candidate and who can see pay. If a candidate's page says “Not found, or you don't have access”, you are not on that opening's chain. Ask the hiring owner or HR.
