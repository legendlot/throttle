Departments is the list of teams at Legend of Toys, each with a head, an optional parent department, and a live headcount. It's the structure the directory filters by, the dashboard counts by, and that profiles and roles attach to.

What it’s for
Defining the team structure.
Read or act?

Who
Manager HR Admin
Feeds
Directory, dashboard, profiles.

The list

Each row shows the department's name, code, its head, its parent department (if it sits under another), and its current headcount. The headcount updates as people are assigned to or moved off the department on their profiles.

Adding or editing (HR)

  1. New Department (or the pencil to edit one) opens a small form.
  2. Set the name (the only required field), and optionally a code, a head (chosen from people), a parent department and a description.
  3. Use the Active toggle to retire a department without deleting it.
  4. Save.
ⓘ Parent department vs reporting line

The parent department nests teams for grouping (for example a Store team under Production). It is not the same as the org chart's reporting lines, which come from each person's manager, not from their department. The two can look similar but are set in different places.

✓ Set a head where you can

Naming a department head keeps the structure legible and gives the org chart and reports a clear owner per team. It's optional, but a department with no head is easy to lose track of.
