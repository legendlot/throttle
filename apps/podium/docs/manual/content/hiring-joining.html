Joining turns an accepted candidate into an employee. It happens in two deliberate steps, so an employee record never exists for someone who then fails to turn up: first the Google work account is created, then, on the day they actually start, HR confirms they have joined.

What it’s for
Work account, then confirm joining.
Read or act?
Act.
Who
HR Admin
Where
The Work account box on the candidate’s application.

Step 1: create the work account

Once the offer is Accepted, the candidate's application shows a Work account box. Only a Google admin can create the account; anyone else sees a note saying so.

  1. Check the names. Given name and family name are prefilled (each up to 60 characters).
  2. Pick the address. Podium proposes addresses at the company domain and marks each available or taken. Choose one, or choose Custom, type the part before the @ (lowercase letters, digits and dots) and press Check.
  3. Pick the org unit and press Create account, then confirm.
  4. Note the password. A box shows the new address and a temporary password, once. Press Copy, pass it to the person safely and press I've noted it. Podium never keeps it; they must change it at first sign-in.

The box then shows the address, its org unit and who created it. A Google admin can press Reset temp password for as long as the person has not joined. If creation fails or is interrupted, the box says so; press create again and Podium finishes the job without making a duplicate.

Step 2: confirm joining

On the person's first day, set the Start date (the actual date; it cannot be more than 7 days ahead or before the offer was accepted) and press Confirm joining. Confirm the prompt. In one step Podium:

  • creates the employee record with a permanent EMP code and the joining date;
  • copies the hiring documents (verified joining documents, bank details) onto the employee, and records the starting compensation;
  • sets the application to Joined, and the opening to Filled once its headcount is reached;
  • starts their onboarding (see Onboarding); if that step fails you are told to start it from the Onboarding page.

The box then links to the new employee. If the opening is full but other candidates are still active on it, a note asks you to close or move them and mark the opening Filled.

If they do not turn up

Press Mark no-show (with an optional note only the salary chain sees). The application becomes No Show and no employee is ever created. If a work account exists, a Google admin sees Suspend account; nothing is suspended automatically. The same button appears for a withdrawn candidate who already has an account.

ⓘ Rehires and account-only requests

A person who was an employee before is not supported yet: the box tells you to handle their account and record by hand. For someone who is not coming through an opening at all, Google admins have People → Create account without hiring. It needs a reason of at least 10 characters and creates only the Google account; import the person with Sync from Google once they start.
