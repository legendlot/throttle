Instead of attaching a PDF and waiting, Podium can email the candidate a private link. They read the offer on their phone, accept, decline or ask a question, and then upload their joining documents and bank details, all without an account. This chapter covers what you press and what the candidate sees.

To send the offer by email

  1. Open the offer when it is Ready to send and press Send by email.
  2. Check the addresses. To is prefilled with the candidate's email. CC is prefilled with HR, the opening's chain and the company owner; edit it freely.
  3. Press Send email. The issued PDF and a link to view and respond are sent.

The email goes out from the hiring owner's own Google mailbox (the reporting manager's if the owner has none), so it looks personal and replies come back to them. It cannot be sent from anyone else's mailbox; if the sender has no work mailbox Podium refuses. Later revisions, the confirmation and any alerts reply in the same email thread with the same subject (“Offer, designation, Legend of Toys”). The Emails card on the offer lists each one with its status (sent, failed, checking…) and whether the link is live or closed. If Gmail does not answer, Podium double-checks the mailbox before it says whether the mail went.

Resend link (same revision) sends a fresh link for the same offer (for example, to a corrected address). The older links stop working.

What the candidate sees

The link opens a mobile-friendly page under the Legend of Toys logo. What it shows depends on the offer:

Respond

While the offer is Sent and in date: a greeting, the role, Start date, Valid until and reference, a button to open the PDF, the letter and the annexure. Three tabs follow. Accept: type their full name exactly as on the offer, tick “I will join on [date]” and “I have read and accept this offer”, then Accept offer. Decline: pick a reason (Compensation, The role, Location, I accepted another offer, Personal reasons, Other), add a note if they wish and confirm. I have a question: type it and, optionally, a different joining date to discuss; it is sent to the hiring owner and the offer does not change.

Documents

After accepting: “Your acceptance is recorded” and a confirmation email. The page lists the joining documents: PAN card, ID proof, address proof, passport-size photo and cancelled cheque are required (marked *); latest payslip, relieving letter, education certificate and experience letter are optional. Each has Upload (or Add another); PDF, JPG or PNG up to 10 MB. Below it, Bank details for salary: name on the account, bank, account number (entered twice) and IFSC, then Save bank details. The page stays open until a week after the start date.

Notice

A plain message and nothing else when the offer is no longer actionable: replaced by a newer one (“check your latest email”), expired, withdrawn or declined (“contact [hiring owner]”), or the link is dead (“This link is no longer valid”). A notice shows no letter, name or figures.

If accepting would take the opening past its headcount, the candidate sees “Thank you, we'll be in touch shortly” and the hiring owner is alerted instead of the acceptance going through.

What you see afterwards

On the offer page, cards appear as the candidate acts:

  • Candidate responses: declines and questions, with their reason, text and any proposed joining date. Press Mark seen once handled; reply to a question by email.
  • Acceptance evidence: the typed name, time, confirmed joining date and the device details recorded at acceptance.
  • Joining documents: a tick for each document received. HR presses Verify (or Unverify) per file after checking it.
  • Bank details: shown masked. Only the salary allow-list can press Reveal to see the full number, and each reveal is logged.
  • Resend confirmation appears if the confirmation email to the candidate did not go.

Changing bank details

Once the candidate saves their bank details they are locked, and the page tells them to ask Legend of Toys to reopen it. HR presses Allow update on the Bank details card; the candidate may then change them once, within 7 days.

Revisions kill the old link

Each revision has its own link. When you send a new revision, the previous offer is replaced and its link stops working (it shows the “replaced” notice). Only the newest link for an offer is live.

⚠ Check the address before you press Send

Anyone holding the link can act on the offer, so it is protected only by being private and hard to guess. Each link also has limits (30 uploads, 10 questions a day, 5 bank saves). If it went to the wrong address, use Resend link to the right one, which closes the old link.
