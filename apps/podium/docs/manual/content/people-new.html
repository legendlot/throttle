New Person is the form HR uses to add someone to Podium. It's the same form you reach by clicking Edit on an existing profile, so once you know it you know both. It's grouped into sections so you can fill in what you have now and come back for the rest.

What it’s for
Adding a person (or editing one).
Read or act?
Act: creates a profile.
Who
HR Admin
Required
Full name. Everything else optional.

The sections

Identity

Full name (the only required field), preferred name, work and personal email, phone, date of birth, gender, blood group, PAN and a photo URL. Date of birth feeds the birthday reminders on the Dashboard.

Role & Reporting

Job title, department, job role (from the registry), manager, employment type, legal entity and work location. The manager you pick here is what places the person on the org chart.

Lifecycle

Joining date, probation end, confirmation date and status. Setting the status to Exited reveals an exit date and reason. Joining date feeds tenure and work-anniversary reminders.

Emergency Contact

A name and phone for emergencies.

Adding someone

  1. Enter at least the full name. You can save with just that and enrich the profile later.
  2. Fill in role and reporting. Setting the manager now means the person appears on the org chart straight away.
  3. Add lifecycle dates. The joining date is worth getting in early; tenure, anniversaries and appraisal eligibility all hang off it.
  4. Click Create person. Podium assigns a permanent employee code (an EMP number) automatically; you don't set it and it can never be changed.
ⓘ Logins are linked separately

Creating a profile does not, by itself, give someone a Podium login. A person can have a full profile, an org-chart spot, documents and compensation history without ever signing in; that's how we keep records for floor and contract staff who don't have a company Google account. Self-serve sign-in only works once their profile is linked to their @legendoftoys.com account, which usually happens automatically when you sync from Google or link them by email.

✓ Already in Google? Sync instead

If the new joiner already has a company Google account, the fastest route is Sync from Google on the Directory: it proposes them as a new joiner with their department and login pre-matched, so you confirm rather than retype. Use this form for people who aren't in Google, or to fill in the details sync can't know.
