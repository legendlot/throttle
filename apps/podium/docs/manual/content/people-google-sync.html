Podium is the source of truth for a person’s core details, and it now sends them to the company Google account for you. This chapter explains what Podium writes to Google, when, who it skips, and the Directory screen (Sync from Google) that HR and the three Google admins use to bring in new joiners and to check Google against Podium.

What it’s for
Keeping Google in line with Podium.
Direction
Podium to Google, one way.
Who
HR Admin (push: Google admins)
Where
Directory → Sync from Google; a person’s page.

Podium wins: the seven fields

Podium owns these Google fields: name, job title, department, manager, personal phone, employee code and org unit. Podium never copies them back from Google.

⚠ Editing these in Google is pointless

If you change one of the seven in Google, Podium’s next change to that person overwrites it, and the Google vs Podium tab will show it as differs. Make the change in Podium instead. Anything not in the list (passwords, suspension, groups, the sign-in address) is never touched by Podium.

A field that is blank in Podium leaves Google’s value alone. The one exception is the manager: a person with no manager in Podium has their manager removed in Google.

What happens when you save a person

When HR (or the person, on their own profile) saves a change, Podium sends only the fields that changed to Google, within seconds. The save itself never fails because of Google.

  • Google sync pending (a small chip on the person’s page): the update is on its way.
  • Not synced to Google (chip): this person is skipped; hover for the reason (see below).
  • Google not updated, retry (red banner): Google could not be reached or refused the change. The banner gives the reason and the time of the next try. Podium retries on its own after 1, 5 and 30 minutes. A Google admin sees a Retry button to try straight away; everyone else sees the banner only.

Who is never written to Google

  • Founders and super-admins, always, in full.
  • People who have exited.
  • Addresses on the ignore list.
  • Anyone in an excluded org unit (shared and system accounts).
  • People with no Google account, a suspended one, or whose work email is only an alias in Google.

Org unit on the person form

The Google org unit picker sits beside Department in the Role & Reporting section of the person form.

  • When you change the department, the picker suggests that department’s usual org unit (marked “usual for this department”), but only if the org unit is blank or still the old department’s suggestion. One you chose yourself is never replaced.
  • It cannot pick an excluded org unit; the list leaves them out and a save with one is refused.
  • Org units with nobody in them yet don’t appear. If you need a brand-new one, ask a Google admin to move the first person in Google.
  • Not set means Google keeps whatever org unit the person already has.

Directory → Sync from Google

HR and admins open it from the Directory. It has two tabs.

Tab 1: From Google

Now only for three jobs: new joiners (create them in Podium), departures and the ignore list.

  • New people: tick Import (joining date required) or Ignore. Department and manager can be set here for the new row.
  • Exit started outside Podium: people active in Podium whose Google account is suspended or gone. This is an alert only; use Start the exit here on their page to record the exit with its reason and last working day.
  • It no longer updates departments or managers from Google. Those differences are handled on the second tab, in Podium’s favour.

Tab 2: Google vs Podium

Google admins only can push; HR can read the comparison. Every synced person and field gets a state:

  • Same: Google already matches Podium.
  • Differs: Google has a different value. Ticked by default.
  • Blank in Podium: nothing to push; Google keeps its value.
  • Changed in Google: someone edited Google after Podium last pushed it. Unticked by default, so you look before Podium’s value overwrites it.
  1. Tick what you want person by person and field by field. “Show people with no differences” widens the list.
  2. Click Preview. It shows exactly what would be sent to Google; nothing is changed yet.
  3. Click Push to Google and confirm. Push only works after a Preview of the same ticks, and sends in batches of eight people.
⚠ Stale, and no automatic retry

If someone changes a value between Preview and Push, that person is refused as stale (the others in the batch still go). Preview again for them. A failed admin push is not retried automatically; the screen lists who was not pushed and why, and you preview and push again.

ⓘ Personal phone is visible in Google

The personal phone goes to Google as the mobile number, so it shows in the Workspace directory unless your Google contact-sharing settings are restricted. Check that setting with a Google admin before relying on it staying private.
