Podium decides what you can see from two separate things: your access tier (set by an admin) and your place in the org chart (who reports to you). This chapter explains both in plain terms, because together they shape every screen in the rest of this manual.

The four access tiers

Employee

The default for everyone. You can see your own profile in full, record and edit your own wins, see observations and 1:1 notes that have been shared with you, and take part in your own appraisal. You see nothing about anyone else. This is what you get with no special role assigned, and it is a real, full level of access to your data, not a locked-out state.

Manager

Anyone with people reporting to them. On top of the employee tier, you see the full profiles of everyone in your reporting line (your direct reports and the people under them), log observations and 1:1s for them, and review their appraisals. You do not get this from a role: Podium works it out from the org chart, so the day someone starts reporting to you, you become their manager in Podium automatically.

HR

The People team. Sees everyone's full profile, adds and edits people, manages departments, job roles and documents, captures org snapshots, and runs appraisal cycles. HR sees the most sensitive fields (bank details, ID proofs, PAN) that even a person's manager cannot.

Admin

Full control. Everything HR can do, plus Podium's own Roles & Permissions, assigning people to roles, and system settings (including the salary vault).

ⓘ A separate level for pay

Seeing compensation (increments, bonuses and salary bands) is a distinct permission that an admin grants on its own. So you can be HR and manage people without seeing pay, or be given pay access without being a full admin. Wherever this manual says “if you have compensation access,” that is the permission it means.

⛔ What a manager cannot see

Being someone's manager lets you see their full profile, but not their bank details, ID proofs or PAN, and not their pay. Those stay with HR and the person themselves. This is deliberate: management visibility is not the same as HR visibility.

How your access is set

Your access comes from a Podium role an admin assigns to your account (on the Users screen). Podium runs its own roles, separate from every other LOT system, so being made HR in Podium does not change your access in Garage, Redline or anywhere else. If you cannot do something you believe you should, an admin needs to adjust your Podium role; your manager visibility, separately, follows the org chart.

⚠ Two different things, often confused

“Manager” in Podium is about the org chart (who reports to you), and it drives what profiles you can open. A “role” like HR or Admin is a separate access tier an admin grants. You can be a manager with no special role at all, and still see your team, because that visibility comes from the org chart, not the role.
