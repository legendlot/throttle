Team is a manager's feed of recent activity across everyone who reports to them, directly or further down the line. It's where you keep an eye on your people's wins and observations, and where you quickly log a new observation without hunting through profiles.

What it’s for
Your team's recent performance activity.
Read or act?
Read; log observations inline.
Who
Manager HR Admin
Scope
Your whole reporting line.

Who shows up here

The feed covers your reporting chain: your direct reports and the people under them. If you don't manage anyone yet, the page says so; people appear here the moment they're set to report to you (on their profile).

Reading the feed

Each card is one piece of activity, newest first, tagged by type:

TypeWhat it shows
ObservationA note logged about the person, with its tone (Positive, Neutral, Constructive).
WinAn accomplishment the person recorded about themselves.
1:1A one-to-one's shared notes. If it's a 1:1 you're not party to, it shows as “Private 1:1” without the contents.

Click a card to jump to that person's profile. Two dropdowns at the top filter the feed: by type (just observations, just wins, just 1:1s) and by person.

Logging an observation

  1. Use the yellow “Log observation for…” dropdown and pick the person.
  2. Write what you observed and set its tone and its visibility (see the callout). Add a date and tags if useful.
  3. Save. It joins their record on their profile and your feed. Close the panel with the × when you're done.
⛔ Choose the visibility deliberately

Every observation has a visibility, and it decides who ever reads it: Private (you and HR only), Shared with managers (you, the chain above the person, and HR), or Shared with person (also the person themselves). The person never sees the first two. Pick before you save: get it right at the moment of writing rather than relying on fixing it later.

✓ Little and often beats a year-end scramble

A few honest observations spread through the period make calibration and the appraisal write-up far easier, and fairer, than trying to remember everything in one sitting. The inline logger is built so you can do it in the flow of a normal week.
