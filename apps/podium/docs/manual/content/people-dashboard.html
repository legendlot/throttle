The Dashboard is Podium's home screen for anyone with people access: a quick read on the size and shape of the company, who has just joined, and whose anniversary or birthday is coming up. It is a glance, not a place to change anything.

The headline tiles

A strip of tiles across the top counts people by their current status:

TileWhat it counts
HeadcountEveryone currently active. This is the number people usually mean by “how many are we?”
DepartmentsHow many departments exist.
On LeavePeople marked as on leave.
NoticePeople serving notice.
ExitedPeople who have left. They stay in Podium for the record; they just don't count in headcount.

The cards

Below the tiles, five cards break the numbers down and surface the near future:

By Department

Each department with its headcount, largest first. A fast read on where the people are.

By Employment Type

The active team split into full-time, intern, contract and so on.

Recent Joiners

The six newest active people, with their title and how long they've been here. Click any name to open their profile.

Upcoming Work Anniversaries

Anyone whose joining date anniversary falls in the next 30 days, with how many days away it is.

Upcoming Birthdays

The same, for birthdays in the next 30 days. Empty if nobody has one coming up.

ⓘ Why a card might be empty

The anniversary and birthday cards rely on each person having a joining date and a date of birth on file. If a card says “None in the next 30 days,” it may genuinely be quiet, or it may mean those dates are missing on some profiles. The fix is to fill them in on the Profile screen.

✓ Tip

The anniversary and birthday cards are the easiest way to never miss a moment that matters to someone. A quick scan on a Monday morning sets up the week's shout-outs.
