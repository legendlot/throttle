How a cycle works

Cycles are anchored to an appraisal date, either 1 April or 1 October. That date is also when any increment takes effect. Everything else is worked out from it: the six-month review window, and the eligibility cut-off (three months before the anchor). A cycle moves through four states:

StatusWhat it means
DraftBeing set up. Enroll people here. No reviews accepted yet.
ActiveOpen for self-reviews and manager reviews.
CalibrationReviews locked; HR sets the final ratings and shares results.
ClosedDone and read-only.

Creating and enrolling

  1. New cycle. Pick the appraisal date (1 Apr / 1 Oct) and an optional name like “H2 2026.” The window and cut-off fill in themselves.
  2. Enroll people. Open enrollment to see everyone with their eligibility worked out for you. Eligible means they joined before the cut-off; borderline and missing-join-date cases are flagged for your judgement. Tick who's in and enroll them.
  3. Activate. Move the cycle to Active to open reviews. People now see their self-review on My Performance, and managers see the reviews they owe.
ⓘ Catch-up periods are automatic

Each person's review window runs from their last appraisal (or their joining date if it's their first) up to the anchor. So someone who joined mid-period, or missed a cycle, automatically gets a longer first window rather than being short-changed. Their increment is pro-rated to match.

The cycle dashboard

Opening a cycle shows progress tiles, Enrolled, Self done, Mgr done, Finalized, Shared, Acknowledged and a PIP count, so you can see at a glance where the cohort is. Below them is the calibration grid: one row per person showing their self rating, their manager's rating, the final rating, an HR-only note, and a suggested increment percentage.

Calibrating and sharing

  1. Move the cycle to Calibration when reviews are in. This locks further editing of the reviews.
  2. Set each final rating in the grid (1–5) and add an optional calibration note. The note is HR-internal and never shown to the employee. Click Finalize.
  3. Share results. Use Share all finalized to release every finalized result at once, or share one at a time from a person's appraisal. Only then can the employee see their rating.
  4. Close the cycle when everyone's done.
⛔ Nothing reaches the employee until you Share

Self-ratings, manager ratings, your calibration note and the final rating all stay hidden from the employee until the moment you share. The employee never sees the manager's raw rating or your note, only the final rating and the manager's written feedback. Share deliberately, and only once a result is genuinely finalized.

⚠ A low final rating triggers a PIP

If a final rating falls at or below the threshold (2 by default), the appraisal is flagged as a Performance Improvement Plan and counted on the PIP tile. That's a deliberate, visible outcome, so set final ratings with care.
