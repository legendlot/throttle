Settings holds the handful of system-wide switches that change how Podium behaves for everyone: the salary vault, the appraisal eligibility rule, and the shortcuts into the permission screens. It's a short page, but a couple of its switches are consequential.

Salary Vault

This switch decides whether Podium stores absolute salary figures at all. While it's off (the default), Podium records only increment percentages and one-time bonus amounts; full base-salary and CTC figures are never kept. Turning it on lets those figures be stored and shown to people with compensation access.

⛔ Leave the vault off until the security layer is live

Company-wide standing pay is the most sensitive data we hold, so the vault is meant to stay off until an extra sign-in wall (a network-level access layer in front of the whole site) is in place. Do not switch it on as a convenience: turning it on early would store full salaries before that protection exists. Until then, percentages and bonuses cover what appraisals need.

Appraisal Eligibility

A single number: the minimum tenure, in days, before someone counts as eligible for an appraisal. It sets the bar that the enrollment screen uses when it works out who's eligible for a cycle. Edit the number and click away to save.

Permissions

A reminder that Podium runs its own permissions, with quick links to Roles & Permissions (define roles) and Users (assign them). Anyone with no role assigned gets self-only access. See those two chapters for the detail.

ⓘ These settings ripple outward

Unlike a personal preference, a switch here changes Podium for everyone. The eligibility number shapes who's offered in the next cycle; the vault setting changes what every compensation screen will store and show. Change them with that reach in mind.
