Roles & KPIs is the registry of job roles at Legend of Toys: each role's title, level, job description, the responsibilities it carries, the KPIs it's measured on, and (for those with compensation access) its salary band. People are then assigned to a role on their profile, and the role's KPIs flow into their appraisal.

The role list

The main screen lists every role with its title, code, level, department, KPI count and (if you have compensation access) the mid-point of its salary band. A locked padlock in the band column means the bands exist but you don't have access to see them. Click a row to open the role.

A role's detail

Each role has two halves:

Job Definition

Title, code, level, department, a short summary, the full job description, and a list of responsibilities (one per line). HR edits these; everyone else reads them.

KPIs

The measures this role is judged on. Each KPI has a name, description, a type (quantitative or qualitative), a target and a weight. These are what appear, per person, in the appraisal.

Salary band

Minimum, mid and maximum for the role. Visible and editable only with compensation access; hidden behind a padlock for everyone else.

People in this role

Everyone currently assigned to the role, each a link to their profile.

Creating and editing (HR)

  1. New Role on the list: enter a title (and optional code and level) to create it, then you land on its detail to flesh it out.
  2. Fill in the definition and click Save definition. Mark a role inactive rather than deleting it when it's no longer in use.
  3. Add KPIs with Add KPI, fill in each one, then Save KPIs (the definition and the KPIs save separately).
✓ Good KPIs make better appraisals

Because a role's KPIs are exactly what both the employee and the manager rate at appraisal time, time spent here pays off later. Clear names, a real target and sensible weights turn a vague review into a specific one.

ⓘ Why a band might show a padlock

Salary bands sit behind the separate compensation permission, just like pay on a profile. If you can manage roles but see a padlock on the bands, that's expected: an admin can grant compensation access if you need it.
