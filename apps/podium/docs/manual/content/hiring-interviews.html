Interviews are how a candidate moves from a screened CV to a decision. The hiring team schedules each round, the interviewers score the candidate on a scorecard after they meet, and the team records a verdict. Interviewers are ordinary employees: they get a small, separate screen called My interviews and see only what they need to judge the candidate.

Being an interviewer gives no extra access to openings, candidates or offers. It opens only the one interview you are on. Seeing an opening (as HR or an approver, for example) does not let you schedule on it.

Rounds and scorecards

An opening can have a round plan: an ordered list such as Technical, Culture, Founder. Each round has a name, optional default interviewers (pre-filled when you schedule it) and a scorecard of up to 10 criteria, each scored 1 to 5. Set this up on the opening’s Interview rounds card (see Openings).

ⓘ Changes apply from now on

Each interview keeps a copy of the scorecard it was scheduled with. Editing a round later changes only interviews scheduled afterwards.

An opening with no round plan can still hold interviews: you type a round name when you schedule, and there is no scorecard (only the overall rating and recommendation).

Scheduling an interview

Open the candidate’s page (/hiring/detail). Each application has an Interviews block with a Schedule interview button.

  1. Pick the round from the plan, or type a round name if the opening has no plan.
  2. Set the date and time. Times are entered and shown in IST, whatever your computer’s clock says. You may pick a time up to 30 days back (to record an interview that already happened) and up to a year ahead.
  3. Set duration and mode. Duration is 30 to 120 minutes in steps (any length from 10 minutes to 8 hours is accepted). Mode is In person, Video or Phone; the last box takes a room, a meeting link, or who calls whom.
  4. Choose the panel (1 to 10 interviewers). It is pre-filled from the round’s defaults. You may include yourself when scheduling.
  5. Press Schedule.
  • The opening must have a JD and be Open, and the candidate must be New, Screening or Interviewing. Otherwise the button is hidden or Podium says why. The interview records which JD version it ran against.
  • Scheduling for a New or Screening candidate moves them to Interviewing at the same moment, and the timeline records it.
  • A candidate can have only one live interview per round. A cancelled or no-show round can be scheduled again.
  • Feedback is due 24 hours after the interview time.

Editing the interview and its panel

The pencil on a scheduled interview changes the time, duration, mode, place and panel.

  • You cannot add or remove yourself on an existing panel. Joining after feedback is in would let you read it; leaving would let you dodge it. Ask another member of the hiring team.
  • A panelist who has already submitted feedback cannot be removed.
  • Once any feedback is in, the time cannot move. Cancel the interview and schedule a new one instead.
  • Moving the time before any feedback moves the pending due dates to 24 hours after the new time.

My interviews (for interviewers)

Open Hiring → My interviews (/hiring/interviews). It appears for anyone on the panel of a live interview, and for a pure interviewer it is the only hiring item they see. Three tabs, each with a count:

  • Feedback owed: interviews whose time has passed and where you have not submitted. An overdue count shows beside it (and on the menu item) once the 24 hours are up. This tab opens first when you owe anything.
  • Upcoming: interviews still to come, soonest first.
  • Done: feedback you have submitted, and interviews that need nothing from you (for example a no-show, or a candidate whose application has since closed).

Click a row to open the interview (/hiring/interviews/detail). If an interview is cancelled you lose access to it.

Giving feedback

You can submit only after the interview time has passed. Until then the page shows the scorecard but no form. The form needs:

  • a score of 1 to 5 for every scorecard criterion;
  • an overall rating of 1 to 5;
  • a recommendation: Strong yes, Yes, No or Strong no;
  • notes (optional, up to 5,000 characters).
⚠ Submit once, and it is blind

Podium asks you to confirm, and feedback cannot be edited afterwards. There are no drafts. You see the other interviewers’ feedback only after you submit your own. This holds even if you are also the hiring owner or reporting manager: while you are on the panel and have not submitted, the candidate’s page tells you your colleagues’ feedback is hidden. A hiring-team member who is not on the panel sees every submitted feedback.

On the candidate’s page each panelist shows Upcoming, Pending, Overdue or Submitted. Pending rows never show content.

Reminding an interviewer

Next to each panelist whose feedback is owed, the hiring team sees Remind. It emails the interviewer from your own work mailbox, naming the candidate code, the round and the time, with a link to My interviews. It carries no candidate name or contact details and no feedback. You can send one per interviewer every 12 hours; the row then shows how many times they have been reminded. You need an active work mailbox, and the interviewer needs a work email.

There are no automatic reminders yet. Overdue feedback is shown in the app only, so chase it with Remind.

The verdict

Once an interview’s time has passed, the hiring team presses Verdict and chooses one outcome, once per interview. Podium warns if some panelists have not submitted, but does not block you.

VerdictWhat happens to the candidate
AdvanceNothing changes. Schedule the next round, or start an offer.
HoldMoves to On Hold.
RejectNeeds a reason from the usual list, and moves to Rejected.

The optional note is seen only by the opening’s salary chain. A reject or hold follows the normal move rules, so a candidate with a live offer cannot be moved until the offer is withdrawn or closed first (see Offers).

Cancel and no-show

  • Cancel works on a scheduled interview and asks for a reason. Only the opening’s salary chain sees the reason. The panel loses access.
  • No-show appears once the interview time has passed and the candidate did not come. The candidate stays where they are, no feedback is needed, and the round can be scheduled again.
  • Closing a candidate cancels their scheduled interviews automatically. When an application becomes Rejected, Withdrawn, Declined, No Show or Joined, by any route, its still-scheduled interviews are cancelled with the reason “application closed”, and nothing remains owed on My interviews.

Google Calendar

Podium can put an interview on Google Calendar for you in one click and keep the invites in step with the interview. An admin switches this on. Until then nothing about Calendar appears anywhere, and interviews work exactly as described above.

When you schedule

For an interview in the future, the Schedule dialog shows two tick boxes:

  • Add to my Google Calendar and invite the interviewers: on by default. For a video interview it adds a Meet link.
  • Send the candidate a separate invite (time and joining details only): on when the candidate has an email. If they have none, the box says so and stays off.

You, the person scheduling, are the organiser: the event sits on your own calendar. Interviewers get an invite with a link to the interview in Podium, and a Meet link for video interviews. Guests cannot edit the event or invite anyone else.

⚠ What the candidate sees

The candidate’s invite is separate and carries only the role, the company, the time, and the Meet link or the place. It has no Podium link, no interviewer names and no round name, and candidates cannot see other guests.

Interviews already scheduled

An interview scheduled before Calendar was on, or one where you left the box off, shows Add to calendar on its row (future interviews only). It adds the event to your calendar, with the same candidate invite choice.

Keeping Calendar and Podium in step

  • Edit in Podium (time, duration, mode, place, panel): the invites update.
  • Cancel in Podium: the invites are removed and the guests are told.
  • An application closing (reject, move out, no-show) removes the invites of its cancelled interviews.
  • Someone moves the event in Google Calendar: Podium picks up the new time the next time the candidate’s page is opened, and any pending feedback due dates move with it. If feedback is already in, the time cannot move, so Podium keeps its time and moves Calendar back.
  • Someone deletes the event in Google Calendar before the interview: Podium cancels the interview too. Deleting it after the interview has happened changes nothing in Podium.
  • Changes to the guest list made in Calendar are not copied into Podium; change the panel in Podium.
