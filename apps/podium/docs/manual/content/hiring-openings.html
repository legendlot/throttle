An Opening is one open seat (or several identical seats) that Legend of Toys wants to fill. Everything in hiring hangs off an opening: its job description (JD), its approved salary band, the people who run it, and the candidates who apply. You make one here before you can add a single CV.

What it’s for
Creating and tracking openings.
Read or act?
Act: create, edit, open, hold, close.
Who
Manager HR Admin
Needs first
A JD, before any candidate.

Finding an opening

Open Hiring → Openings (/hiring/openings). The chips along the top filter by status: Live (the default: Draft, Open and On Hold together), Open, Draft, On Hold, Filled, Cancelled and All. The search box matches the opening's name. Each row shows the opening and its code, the department, the reporting manager, a status badge, whether the JD is in (“JD ✓” or “JD needed”), the salary Band status, Candidates (active over total) and Seats. Click a row to open it.

You see the openings you run, the ones you manage, the ones you are the approver on, plus everything if you hold the Hiring permission or are HR. Anyone else sees an empty list.

To create an opening

You can create one if you hold the Hiring permission or manage people. Press New opening (top right of the list) and fill four cards:

  1. Role. Pick a Job role from the registry if one fits (it prefills the designation and department), or just type the Designation (required). Choose the Department, the Type (Full-time or Intern), the Headcount (how many identical seats, 1 to 100), the city and state, an optional target join date and notes.
  2. Team. The Reporting manager (required) defaults to you. The Hiring owner is the person who runs the opening day to day and whose mailbox the offer email is sent from. It is you unless you hold the Hiring permission, in which case you can name someone else.
  3. Salary band. Enter the Band min and Band max as annual total CTC in rupees (for example ₹X to ₹Y). If you are on the salary allow-list you can instead tick Use the role's band to copy the band already set on the job role.
  4. Job description. Choose Later, Paste text, Upload a PDF or Word file, or Role's JD (copies the job role's current JD). Then press Create opening.
⚠ No JD, no candidates

An opening with no JD stays a Draft, and no CVs or candidates can be added to it. Choosing “Later” is allowed, but the opening shows a “JD needed” badge until someone adds one. Add it on the opening's page with Add JD; later edits use New version, and every earlier version stays in the version history.

The salary band: requested, then approved

Creating the opening does not approve the band. It sends the band for approval, and the screen tells you so (“Opening created, band sent for approval”). The approver is fixed at that moment (a snapshot): it is the reporting manager's manager, or a founder when the reporting manager is a founder or has no manager above them.

  • The approver sees Approve and Reject on the opening's Salary band card and is asked for a note. Nobody else sees those buttons.
  • Nobody approves their own request. If you asked for the band, someone else has to decide it. If you try to put yourself on an opening's approval chain, Podium refuses and tells you to ask a founder.
  • To change a band later press Request change, enter the new min and max, and Send for approval. It replaces any pending request. Earlier versions stay listed with their outcome (Approved, Rejected, Superseded).
  • Changing the reporting manager re-sends a pending band to the new approver.

An approved band is required before an offer can be made. The band numbers are visible only to the opening's own chain and the salary allow-list; everyone else sees “hidden”.

Running the opening

The buttons at the top of an opening depend on its status and on whether you are the hiring owner or hold the Hiring permission:

Open

Available on a Draft or On Hold opening that has a JD. Candidates can be added only while it is Open.

Hold

Pauses an Open opening. You are asked why.

Mark filled

Closes the opening as Filled. Joining someone (see Joining) also fills it automatically once the headcount is reached.

Cancel

Closes it as Cancelled; you are asked why.

Reopen

Only with the Hiring permission, on a Filled or Cancelled opening. It returns to Open, or to On Hold if it has no JD.

ⓘ Close the candidates first

You cannot mark an opening Filled or Cancelled while candidates are still active on it. Close or move each of them first (see Candidates & Pipeline).

The Team card holds the hiring owner, the reporting manager and the band approver, with Edit for the details. Changing the reporting manager or hiring owner needs the Hiring permission. The Interview rounds card holds the opening's round plan: an ordered list of rounds, each with a name, default interviewers and a scorecard of up to 10 criteria (each scored 1 to 5). The hiring owner and anyone with the Hiring permission can Add round, edit, reorder with the arrows, and Deactivate a round (a round is never deleted, so past interviews keep their record). Changes apply only to interviews scheduled afterwards; see Interviews. Each candidate row on the opening also shows how many interviews they have, the next one, and how much feedback is owed. The Candidates card and a Timeline of everything that happened sit below.
