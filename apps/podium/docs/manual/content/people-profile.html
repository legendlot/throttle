A person's profile is the single page that gathers everything Podium knows about them: their employment and personal details, their team, documents, compensation history, and the running record of their performance. How much of it you see depends entirely on who you are to that person, so this is the screen where Podium's privacy rules are most visible.

Full view vs limited view

At the top, under the person's name, you may see a yellow banner reading “Limited view, full profile is visible to HR, the person, and their managers.” That tells you you're looking at the public-facing subset (essentially their role and where they sit), not the whole profile. You get the full view only when the person is you, when you're in their management chain, or when you're HR or an admin.

⛔ What stays hidden, even in the full view

A manager seeing a report's full profile still does not see their bank details, ID proofs or PAN; those appear only for HR, an admin, and the person themselves. Compensation is hidden too, unless you specifically have compensation access. Visibility is not all-or-nothing: the most sensitive fields are walled off separately.

Editing (HR only)

If you're HR or an admin, an Edit button sits top-right. It opens the same form used to add a person (see New Person for the field-by-field guide). Note the employee code can never be changed; it is the person's permanent ID.

Documents

Documents are held privately: clicking Download fetches the file through a short-lived secure link, so the file is never sitting on a public address. HR and admins can upload new documents and delete existing ones; everyone else with the full view can only download.

ⓘ The salary vault

On the Compensation card you'll often see a note that the salary vault is disabled. While it's off, Podium records only increment percentages and one-time bonus amounts, never absolute salary figures. “Current CTC” will read “(vault off).” This is intentional until the extra security layer is in place; see the Settings chapter.

The performance tabs

Three further tabs turn the profile into the person's performance record. What you can do on them depends on whether you manage this person.

Observations

Short notes a manager logs about the person's work through the year, each with a tone (Positive, Neutral or Constructive), a date, optional tags, and a visibility setting that decides who can read it:

VisibilityWho can read it
PrivateOnly the author and HR. The person it's about never sees it.
Shared with managersThe author, the person's manager chain, and HR. Still not the person.
Shared with personThe above, plus the person themselves.

Managers (and HR) log observations; you can't write one about yourself. On your own profile, or via My Performance, you see only the observations explicitly shared with you.

Wins

Accomplishments the person records about themselves: a title, optional detail, a date and tags. Wins are self-authored, so the place you add your own is your My Performance page; here you're viewing someone's wins as part of their record.

1:1s

Notes from one-to-one meetings, logged by the manager. Each has shared notes the report can see, an optional set of action items (tickable checkboxes), and private notes for the manager alone.

⛔ Private 1:1 notes are truly private

The private-notes box on a 1:1 is visible only to the manager who wrote it and to HR. The report, and even skip-level managers, never see it; they see “Private manager notes hidden” instead. Use it freely for candid manager-only context, and equally, never write something in shared notes that you only meant for yourself.
