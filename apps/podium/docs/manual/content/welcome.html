My Profile is the page every employee, and every new joiner, uses to fill in their own details once: personal information, contact, address, bank, statutory numbers and documents. HR and payroll use what you enter here, so it is worth getting right the first time.

What it’s for
Completing and keeping your own details.

To complete your profile

Open My Profile (it is under Performance in the menu; a joiner with no other Podium access still sees it, and the “Complete your profile” banner links here). The top says how many items are still needed and your percentage complete. Anything missing is marked needed; required fields carry a *.

  1. Work down the sections. Personal (preferred name, date of birth, gender, blood group, PAN), Contact & emergency (personal email, mobile, emergency contact name and mobile), Address (current, and permanent if different), Bank (name on the account, account number, IFSC) and Statutory (PF UAN and ESIC number, if you have them).
  2. Previous employer, only if it applies. If you changed jobs this financial year (April to March), fill in Previous employer this financial year: taxable salary paid, exemptions and TDS. Only you and the payroll team can see it. Skip it otherwise.
  3. Press Save. The button stays dim until you change something, and counts your unsaved changes. Fields that fail a check (for example a PAN not shaped like ABCDE1234F) show the problem under the box.
  4. Upload documents. In Documents, the required types show as badges that turn ticked as you add them. Pick a type, choose a PDF or photo up to 15 MB and press Upload. Uploads are private to you and HR.
  5. Read your policies. Under Policies to read, press Read to open each, then I have read this to acknowledge it. If a newer version is published, read that one.

Your first weeks

A joiner with an open onboarding also sees a Your first weeks card listing what happens around joining and who does it, with a count such as 3/7 done. Your own lines tick themselves as you complete this page.

Changing PAN or bank details later

Once your PAN or bank details are filled in, you cannot overwrite them directly. The field says Changing this sends it to HR for approval. Enter the new value and Save; it becomes a request, and a blue notice reads Awaiting HR approval while the current value stays in force. You can press Withdraw on that notice at any time. The three bank fields travel together as one change. When HR approves, the new value is applied; if they reject it, you see their reason.

ⓘ “Your login is not linked to an employee profile”

If you see this instead of the form, your sign-in has not been matched to your employee record yet. Ask HR to link it.
