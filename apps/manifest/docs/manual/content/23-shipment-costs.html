When goods clear the port and again at last-mile delivery, SF records the logistics costs against the shipment. Each one deducts the shared pool the moment it is recorded. Last-mile also captures who is delivering and in which vehicle.

Recording a logistics cost

  1. Record cost. On the shipment detail, in the costs card, click Record cost.
  2. Pick the type. shipping, customs, other_fees or last_mile.
  3. Enter the rupee amount. Then confirm. The pool drops by that amount immediately.

Air freight is priced by chargeable weight and sea by container or volume, but you record the final rupee figure either way; Manifest just prompts you for the right set of costs for the mode.

✓ Tip

If a cost pushes the pool negative, Manifest nudges you to raise a draw-down. It is a funding prompt, not a block.

Last-mile: who is bringing it

At the local-transport stage, capture who is doing the last-mile delivery and the vehicle number, so the store team knows exactly who and what vehicle to expect when the material arrives.

  1. Last-mile. Click Last-mile on the shipment.
  2. Pick the partner. Choose the delivery partner from the list (code and company name).
  3. Enter the vehicle number. Then Save. It shows on the shipment for the store team.
⛔ Important

Delivery partners are always chosen from the shared list, never typed as loose text. If the partner is not listed, add it once in Admin → Logistics partners, then pick it. This keeps the partner master clean.
