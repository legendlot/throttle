There is one shared pool of money between LOT and Solve Factory. Think of it as a running tab. Understanding the pool is the key to understanding every money screen in Manifest.

LOT pays in
Pool goes up. SF holds LOT’s money.
SF spends out
Pool goes down, the moment it’s paid.
Balance
Can be positive or negative.
Truth
Always live to what has actually been paid.

How the pool moves

Pool positive

Solve Factory is holding LOT’s money, ready to spend on the next order.

Pool negative

SF has spent more than LOT has put in, so LOT owes SF. This is allowed and normal; SF recovers it as LOT funds the pool again.

The most important money rule

⛔ Important

Every payment SF makes is deducted from the pool the moment it is recorded, not later at invoice time. So the pool is always accurate to whatever has actually been paid out on LOT’s behalf.

The first two wrap up the goods purchase. The middle four are logistics. The last is SF’s fee. Every one of them reduces the pool the moment it is recorded.

ⓘ Note

Invoicing is a close-out record, not a money move. The money for goods and logistics already left the pool as it was paid. See the Invoicing chapter.
