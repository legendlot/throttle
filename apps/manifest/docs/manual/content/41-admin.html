Two levels of admin

There is a deliberate split between governing access and running day-to-day settings:

  • Super admin (the "Manifest Super Admin" role): controls who can use Manifest, builds and edits roles, and appoints other super admins. Afshaan holds this, plus anyone he grants the role (for example Vinay).
  • Manifest Admin (operational): can tune the timeline defaults and the logistics-partner master, but cannot change who has access or what a role can do.

Only super admins see the Access Control and Roles tabs. Anyone with operational admin sees the Operations tab.

The locked door

A person who has signed in but has not been granted a Manifest role sees a full-screen "No access to Manifest" message with a Sign out button, and nothing else. The same wall appears the moment someone is disabled. This is normal and safe: access is something a super admin grants on purpose, never the default.

Access Control (super admin)

Lists everyone who has a Manifest role, with their organisation, role, and active status.

  1. Grant access. Type the person’s email, pick a role, and click Grant. They must have signed in at least once first (Google for LOT staff, the email link for Solve Factory owners) so their account exists; otherwise you get a clear "ask them to sign in once first" message.
  2. Change a role. Use the role dropdown on their row to move them to a different role.
  3. Disable or enable. Disable instantly blocks login while keeping the person on the list; Enable restores it. Use this for a temporary cut.
  4. Remove. Takes the person off Manifest entirely.
ⓘ Note

Disabling here only affects Manifest. It never touches the person’s access to the other Legend of Toys systems.

⛔ Important

You cannot disable or remove yourself, and you cannot disable, remove, or demote the last active super admin. This stops anyone from accidentally locking the whole team out. Always keep at least two super admins.

Roles, the permissions builder (super admin)

A role is a named bundle of permissions. The list shows every role; the built-in ones (Manifest Super Admin, Solve Factory Owner, LOT Finance, LOT Founder) are marked System and are view-only.

  1. Create a role. Click New, give it a key, a label, and pick its side (LOT or Solve Factory). The side is fixed once created.
  2. Tick the permissions. Each capability (manage orders, record payments, create invoices, manage documents, and so on) is a checkbox, grouped by area. A Solve Factory role can only hold SF capabilities; LOT-only powers (including super admin and the cost view) are never offered on an SF role.
  3. Save, or Delete. Save writes the role. A custom role with nobody assigned can be deleted; an assigned one must be reassigned first.
ⓘ Note

You build roles out of a fixed set of capabilities. You cannot invent a brand-new power here; if a genuinely new capability is ever needed, that is a system change, not a role edit.

Operations: shipment timeline defaults

These are the day-offsets Manifest uses to pre-fill a new shipment’s expected dates, set separately for sea and air.

  1. Edit the offsets. For each mode, set how many days after the previous milestone each leg usually takes (loaded, sailing, docked, cleared, local transport, received).
  2. Save. New shipments now pre-fill from these. They are suggestions only; SF can change any date on a shipment.
ⓘ Note

These defaults are edited directly with no audit trail, on purpose; they are tuning knobs, not records. Adjust them whenever your real lead times change.

Operations: logistics partners

The shared list of carriers and last-mile transporters (freight forwarders, shipping lines, couriers). Every carrier and last-mile picker in Manifest reads from this master.

  1. Add a partner. Click Add partner, enter the company, country and the modes it supports (sea, air, land), plus IATA or SCAC codes and a tracking URL where relevant.
  2. Create. Manifest assigns the partner code. It is now selectable everywhere.
⛔ Important

Partners are never typed as free text anywhere in Manifest. If a needed carrier or transporter is missing, add it here once. This keeps the master clean and the pickers reliable.

ⓘ Note

Vendors come from the shared LOT vendor master and already cover China, international and domestic suppliers, so there is nothing to set up here for vendors.
