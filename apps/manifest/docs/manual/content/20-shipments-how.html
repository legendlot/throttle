A shipment is one physical container with its own schedule. Once goods are picked up, SF puts them on a shipment and tracks it from the factory to the LOT warehouse. Every shipment is either Air or Sea.

The shipment journey

Sea

Planned → Loaded → Sailing → Docked → Cleared → Local transport → Received.

Air

The same steps, read as Planned → Loaded → In Flight → Landed → Cleared → Local transport → Received.

Each milestone is date-stamped automatically as SF advances it.

The steps are identical; the labels, the costs you record, and the suggested timeline differ by mode. The suggested timeline per mode is editable by SF or Admin in the settings (see Admin chapter).

Allocation and the departure lock

Allocation

SF allocates an order’s items to a shipment. Usually one PO travels on one shipment, but a shipment often carries several POs, and one PO can split across more than one shipment.

Departure lock

Items can be moved between shipments while the container has not left. Once it departs (Sailing for sea, In Flight for air), the contents lock: nothing can be added, removed or moved.

✓ Tip

A single PO can split across an air shipment and a sea shipment, for example urgent units fly while the bulk sails. Just create two shipments and allocate the right items to each.
