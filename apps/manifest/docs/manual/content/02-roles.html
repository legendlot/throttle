Manifest has two parties, LOT and Solve Factory, plus an Admin layer. What you can see and do depends on which party you are and which role you hold.

Parties
LOT and Solve Factory (SF).
LOT does
Requests, funds the pool, views all.
SF does
Runs the whole lifecycle.
Cost lens
SF never sees LOT’s internal cost columns.

The roles

RolePartyCan do
LOT Finance / FounderLOTCreate requests, record LOT to SF wires, see every order, shipment and the running account.
Solve Factory OwnerSFConvert requests to POs, place them, attach the PI, pay the vendor and logistics, run shipments, invoice and close orders.
AdminLOTEverything, plus manage users and roles, the shipment timeline defaults, and the logistics-partner master.

What each side sees

LOT

The full picture: every order, every shipment, all payments and the running account. LOT funds the pool and watches it.

Solve Factory

A trimmed sidebar focused on running orders and shipments and recording what it pays. SF never sees LOT’s internal cost-per-unit or margin columns, by design.

ⓘ Note

Most of the work after a request belongs to SF. If you are on the LOT side, your main jobs are raising the request and keeping the pool funded.

⛔ Important

Admin can act on SF’s behalf (for example to drive or correct an order) because LOT runs the system. Use that carefully: the audit trail records who did what.
