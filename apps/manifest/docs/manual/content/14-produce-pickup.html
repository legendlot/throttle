Advancing production

  1. Advance the stage. On the order detail, use the Advance button to move Confirmed → Produced → Picked up as the vendor progresses. Each step is date-stamped automatically.
  2. Pay the pickup balance. At pickup, record any remaining balance or extra services as a vendor payment with type pickup balance. It deducts the pool right away.

At this point the goods purchase is fully wrapped up: advance plus pickup balance. What follows is logistics.

Cancelling an order

⛔ Important

An order can be cancelled at any time up to and including Produced. Once it is Picked up, it can no longer be cancelled, because the goods are paid for and on their way.

  1. Open the order. While it is Requested, Draft, Placed, Confirmed or Produced, a Cancel button is shown.
  2. Give a reason. A reason is required and is logged on the timeline.
  3. Confirm. The order moves to Cancelled.
⚠ Watch out

Cancelling does not automatically reverse any payments already made on the order. Those stay as pool debits and are reconciled by hand. This is deliberate; the system stays honest about money that actually moved.
