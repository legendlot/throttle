Manifest is the shared workspace where Legend of Toys (LOT) and Solve Factory (SF) run every China import together. It replaces the old WhatsApp-and-spreadsheet way of tracking orders and money with one live system both sides can see.

What it’s for
Tracking China orders end to end, with one shared money pool.
Who uses it
LOT Solve Factory
Two sides
LOT requests & funds. SF runs the lifecycle.
Live
Always current; dates stamp automatically.

The idea in one paragraph

LOT asks for what it needs and puts money into a shared pool. SF takes it from there: it raises the purchase order with the Chinese vendor, pays the vendor, ships the goods, clears customs, delivers to LOT’s warehouse, and finally invoices LOT to close the order. Everything is tracked live, with a full timestamped history on every order and every shipment, so anyone can look back and see exactly what happened and when.

The golden rule of who does what

LOT

Raises the request and funds the shared pool. That is essentially it.

Solve Factory

Owns the entire lifecycle after the request: converting it to a real PO, paying the vendor, shipping, customs, delivery, and invoicing LOT.

What this manual covers

This is a combined system and process guide. Each chapter explains what the step is in the real import process, and then exactly how to do it on the screen. Read the Getting Started part first; after that, the parts follow the natural order of a shipment, from request to invoice.

ⓘ Note

Manifest is the first LOT system with external users. Solve Factory signs in with an email link (no Google account needed), and the system only ever shows SF what it should see.
