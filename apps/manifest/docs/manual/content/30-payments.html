LOT funds the shared pool by recording its wires to Solve Factory. When the pool runs low, SF (or LOT) raises a draw-down: a request for LOT to top it up.

Who
LOT records wires · SF raises draw-downs
Where
Payments · Draw-downs
Wire effect
Pool goes up (a credit).
UTR
Required on every new wire.

Recording a LOT to SF wire

  1. Open Payments. Click Record Wire.
  2. Enter the amount and UTR. The rupee amount and the bank UTR (reference) are both required.
  3. Set the channel and method. Which SF payout channel received it, and how it was sent.
  4. Record wire. The pool goes up by that amount; SF is now holding more of LOT’s money.
⛔ Important

Every new wire must carry a UTR. This is how the wire is matched to the bank later. Older historical payments may not have one, but all new ones do.

Raising a draw-down

A draw-down is a request for LOT to put money into the pool, usually because an upcoming payment (an advance, a balance, shipping) would otherwise push the pool negative.

  1. Raise Draw-down. From the Draw-downs screen, or from the prompt that appears after a payment runs the pool low.
  2. Pick the phase and order. Goods advance, shipping & customs, local, or other; optionally tag it to a specific order.
  3. Enter the amount. Manifest shows the Yuan equivalent at the current rate. Submit.
ⓘ Note

A draw-down is a funding prompt, not a payment and not a gate. It does not move the pool by itself; LOT settles it by recording a wire. SF can always pay from its own pocket and let the pool go negative.
