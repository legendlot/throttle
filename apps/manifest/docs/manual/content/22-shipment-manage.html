The shipment detail page is where SF runs a container: advance its milestones, edit its details, attach the orders it carries, and (before it sails) move items around.

Who
Solve Factory Admin
Where
Shipments → click a shipment
Do
Advance, edit, allocate, move.
Locks
Contents freeze once it departs.

Advancing the journey

  1. Advance. The header shows a stage timeline (each milestone as a step, with its date once reached) and the current stage. Click Advance to move to the next milestone (mode-aware: Sailing/Docked for sea, In Flight/Landed for air). Each advance date-stamps automatically.
  2. Received couples the order. When all of an order’s shipment legs reach Received, the order is recognised as delivered.

Editing details

  1. Edit. Opens the carrier, container, BL/AWB and all expected dates for change. Any date change is logged as a revision; the original plan is kept.
  2. Mode lock. You can change the mode only while the shipment is still Planned. Once it is loaded, the mode is fixed.

Allocating and moving items

  1. Add an order. In Allocated items, pick an order and click Add order; its lines join this container.
  2. Move between containers (before departure). While both shipments are pre-departure, items can be re-allocated. The order’s detail page also has a “Move this PO to another container” control.
⛔ Important

Once the shipment departs (Sailing or In Flight), its contents lock. Add and move are disabled and the page shows “contents locked”. This mirrors reality: a sailed container cannot be changed.

ⓘ Note

The Timeline card lists every event on the shipment, planned dates, revisions, stage advances and payments, with timestamps, so the full history is always visible.
