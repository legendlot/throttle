Signing in

LOT team

Use Sign in with Google with your legendoftoys.com account. This is the same login you use for the other LOT systems.

Solve Factory

  1. Request a link. On the sign-in screen, choose Partner sign-in and enter your email.
  2. Open the email. Click the secure link Manifest sends you. No password, no Google account needed.
  3. First time only. An LOT admin onboards you once so the system knows who you are. After that, the email link is all you need.
⚠ Watch out

If a login link opens Manifest but it says you have no access, you have not been onboarded yet. Ask an LOT admin to add you (Admin chapter).

Getting around

Sidebar

Jump between Dashboard, Orders, Shipments, the money screens (Running Account, Draw-downs, Payments, Exchange Rates), Documents and Admin. SF sees a trimmed set.

Top bar

Shows where you are, the live CNY to INR rate, and a green LIVE dot when data is current.

Detail screens

Click any order or shipment row to open its detail, where all the actions live. A back chip returns you to the list.

Tweaks

The small dial at the bottom-right sets the accent colour and density. It only changes how the app looks for you.

✓ Tip

The app is always live. After you record something, the numbers refresh on their own; you rarely need to reload the page.
