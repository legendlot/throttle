Raising a request

  1. Open New Order. On the Orders screen, click New Order.
  2. Choose “Request”. In the Create card, set Create as to request.
  3. Give it a title. Plain and recognisable, for example Ghost 500 or Nitro restock.
  4. Pick a category. Product, Part, Sub-part, Mould, Sample or Other.
  5. Add the items you want (optional). Product, quantity and, if you know it, a per-unit price. SF will firm these up.
  6. Create request. The order appears in the list as Requested.

What happens next

The request sits in Requested until Solve Factory picks it up and converts it to a PO. From that point SF owns it: vendor, pricing, shipping, payments and invoicing. You can keep watching it on the Orders list and its detail page.

ⓘ Note

A request is deliberately light. You do not need vendor names, Yuan prices or shipping details; that is SF’s job at conversion. Just describe what you need clearly.

✓ Tip

Putting the quantity in the title (for example Ghost 500) makes the Orders list easy to scan at a glance.
