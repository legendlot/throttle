Converting a request to a PO is where Solve Factory takes ownership. SF fills in the real purchase-order detail in the vendor’s own product codes, then places it and sends the PO document to the vendor.

Convert, then fill the PO

  1. Open the request. Click it in the Orders list. While it is Requested, you see a Convert to PO button.
  2. Convert to PO. The order moves to Draft and becomes fully editable by SF.
  3. Edit the lines. For each item set the vendor item code, a description, the quantity and the unit price in Yuan. A single PO can mix items on different GST codes; set GST per line later at invoice.
  4. Add costs. Use Edit on the order to record purchase, shipping and customs as they become known. Goods are priced in Yuan; landed cost builds up as it ships.

Why the PO uses the vendor’s codes

The PO is written in the Chinese vendor’s product codes, not LOT’s names. For example, the product LOT calls Flare may appear on the vendor’s PO as 820D. Manifest stores both: the vendor item code and a readable product name.

ⓘ Note

Because the PO speaks vendor codes, Manifest does not push it into Snorkel (LOT’s procurement system) automatically. Bridging the two needs a separate vendor-code to LOT-product connector, which is planned but not built yet. Until then Manifest runs fully on its own.

Place the PO

  1. Place order. When the PO is ready, click Place order. The order moves to Placed and the timeline starts.
  2. Download the PO PDF. A printable, vendor-facing PO (in Yuan, in vendor codes) becomes available. Send it to the vendor.
⚠ Watch out

The PO PDF is a clean placeholder format for now. SF’s preferred layout will be folded in later; the numbers and codes are correct, the styling will improve.
